# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be a finite extension, let $R$ be a commutative finite-type $\bFp$-algebra, and let $M$ be an etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $\bfE_{F,R}$. For a fixed topological generator $\gamma$ of $\Gamma\cong\Z_p$, the Herr complex is
\[
C_{\Herr}^\bullet(M)=\left[0\longrightarrow M\xrightarrow{d^0_{\Herr}}M\oplus M\xrightarrow{d^1_{\Herr}}M\longrightarrow0\right],
\]
where
\begin{align*}
d^0_{\Herr}(x)&=((\varphi_M-1)x,(\gamma_M-1)x),\\
d^1_{\Herr}(x,y)&=(\gamma_M-1)x-(\varphi_M-1)y.
\end{align*}
For every finite-type $R$-algebra $S$, put $M_S=M\otimes_{\bfE_{F,R}}\bfE_{F,S}$. The cocycle, coboundary, and cohomology presheaves are defined by
\begin{align*}
Z^i(M,S)&:=Z^i(C_{\Herr}^\bullet(M_S))=:Z^i_{\Herr}(M_S),\\
B^i(M,S)&:=B^i(C_{\Herr}^\bullet(M_S))=:B^i_{\Herr}(M_S),\\
H^i(M,S)&:=H^i(C_{\Herr}^\bullet(M_S))=:H^i_{\Herr}(M_S).
\end{align*}
