# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, let $\bB=U\rtimes\bT$ be the Borel of a split pinned reductive group $\bG$ with $p>h_{\bG}$, and let $K=U_{\Phi_K}\subset U$ be a $\bT$-stable normal subgroup with a total root order compatible with $K$-height. Let $A$ be a $\bFp$-algebra and fix $\alpha\in\Phi_K$. Suppose
\[
f_{<\alpha},g_{<\alpha}\in(K_{<\alpha}\rtimes\bT)(\bfE_{F,A}),\qquad f_{<\alpha}\varphi(g_{<\alpha})=g_{<\alpha}\gamma(f_{<\alpha}).
\]
Write $f_{<\alpha}=c_{f,<\alpha}f^{\SS}$ and $g_{<\alpha}=c_{g,<\alpha}g^{\SS}$ with $c_{f,<\alpha},c_{g,<\alpha}\in K_{<\alpha}(\bfE_{F,A})$ and $f^{\SS},g^{\SS}\in\bT(\bfE_{F,A})$. The logarithmic root coordinates $c_{f,\beta},c_{g,\beta}\in U_{\beta^\vee}(\bfE_{F,A})$ for $\beta<_K\alpha$ are defined by
\[
\log_{\leq h_{\bG}}(c_{f,<\alpha})=\sum_{\beta<_K\alpha}c_{f,\beta},\qquad\log_{\leq h_{\bG}}(c_{g,<\alpha})=\sum_{\beta<_K\alpha}c_{g,\beta}.
\]
If $\alpha$ is the smallest root of $K$-height $h+1$, define $(\tr_hc_f,\tr_hc_g):=(c_{f,<\alpha},c_{g,<\alpha})$.
