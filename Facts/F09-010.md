# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let $\overline\rho:G_K\to\mathrm{GL}_d(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$ be continuous. Then $\overline\rho$ has a crystalline lift
\[
\rho^\circ:G_K\longrightarrow\mathrm{GL}_d(\overline{\mathbb Z}_p)
\]
with regular labeled Hodge--Tate weights, and $\rho^\circ$ may be chosen potentially diagonalizable.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Proceed by induction on $d$. If $\overline\rho$ is irreducible, wild inertia acts trivially and the tame inertia characters form one Frobenius orbit. Hence
\[
\overline\rho\cong
\operatorname{Ind}_{G_{K'}}^{G_K}\overline\psi
\]
for a character $\overline\psi$ of the unramified extension $K'/K$ of degree $d$. Lift its finite-order part by Teichmuller lift and multiply by labeled Lubin--Tate characters whose exponents are distinct and congruent to the required residual exponents modulo $p-1$. The resulting character $\psi$ is crystalline, reduces to $\overline\psi$, and has pairwise distinct conjugate labeled weights. Thus $\operatorname{Ind}_{G_{K'}}^{G_K}\psi$ is a regular crystalline lift. After restriction to $G_{K'}$ it is a direct sum of crystalline characters, so it is potentially diagonalizable.

If $\overline\rho$ is reducible, choose an exact sequence
\[
0\longrightarrow\overline\rho_1\longrightarrow\overline\rho
\longrightarrow\overline\rho_2\longrightarrow0
\]
with $\overline\rho_2$ irreducible. By induction and the irreducible case, choose regular potentially diagonalizable crystalline lifts $\rho_1^\circ$ and $\rho_2^\circ$. Multiplying $\rho_1^\circ$ by sufficiently large powers of labeled Lubin--Tate characters that are trivial modulo $p$ preserves its reduction and potential diagonalizability while placing all of its labeled weights strictly below those of $\rho_2^\circ$. Apply D02-006 to the residual extension. It yields a crystalline lift of $\overline\rho$ which is potentially diagonalizable. The two separated regular weight sets have no repetitions, so their union is regular. This completes the induction.
