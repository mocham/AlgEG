# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be a finite extension, let $R=\bFp[t,t^{-1}]$, let $M$ be a rank-$1$ etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $\bfE_{F,R}$, and let $S$ be a finite-type $R$-algebra. Define the modified complex $C^\bullet_{\Herr,+}(M,S)$ by
\begin{align*}
C^0_{\Herr,+}(M,S)&:=C^0_{\Herr}(M,S),\\
C^1_{\Herr,+}(M,S)&:=(S\otimes_{\F_p}k_{F_{\cyc}})\oplus C^1_{\Herr}(M,S),\\
C^2_{\Herr,+}(M,S)&:=C^2_{\Herr}(M,S).
\end{align*}
Its degree-zero differential is
\[
d^0_{\Herr,+}(f):=\begin{cases}(\text{constant term of }f,d^0_{\Herr}(f)),&M\text{ is unramified},\\(0,d^0_{\Herr}(f)),&M\text{ is ramified},\end{cases}
\]
where the constant term is evaluated in $S\otimes_{\F_p}k_{F_{\cyc}}$. Its degree-one differential is
\[
d^1_{\Herr,+}(s,x):=d^1_{\Herr}(x).
\]
The constant-term map depends on a choice of basis element $v\in M_S^\Gamma\cong S\otimes_{\F_p}k_{F_{\cyc}}$.
