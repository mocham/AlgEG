# Statement


Let $p=7$, $F=\mathbb Q_7(\mu_7)$, and let $\mathbf G$ be split pinned of type $G_2$. Write $a$ for the short simple root and $b$ for the long simple root. Choose the trivial-inertial non-Steinberg simple-root components and specialize at the common Steinberg crossing $t_a=t_b=1$. Put $r_1=a$, $r_2=a+b$, and $r_3=2a+b$. Then $r_1+r_2=2a+b$, $r_1+r_3=3a+b$, and $r_2+r_3=3a+2b$ are roots, so $J_{r_1,r_2}$, $J_{r_1,r_3}$, and $J_{r_2,r_3}$ are all required cup-product matrices at $t_a=t_b=1$. The three root modules are canonically identified copies of the self-dual trivial module, and the three pairings are nonzero scalar multiples of one nondegenerate alternating pairing on the $d_F=6$ coordinate space. No choices of the three root bases can make all three matrices equal to $I_6$. Thus cocycles cannot be chosen so that every required matrix $J_{\beta,\gamma}$ is the identity.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The extension $F/\mathbb Q_7$ has degree $d_F=6$ and contains $\mu_7$, so the mod-$7$ cyclotomic character of $G_F$ is trivial. Also $7>h_{G_2}=6$. At $t_a=t_b=1$, every root character appearing above is trivial and is its own Tate dual. Local Tate duality identifies the relevant degree-one pairing with a nondegenerate alternating form; the source setup asserts that each displayed $6\times6$ matrix is pointwise invertible. The three Chevalley root brackets are nonzero, and the possible $G_2$ structure constants $1,2,3$ remain nonzero modulo $7$, so each edge pairing is a scalar $u_{ij}\in\overline{\mathbb F}_7^\times$ times one alternating form $\Omega$. If ordered basis matrices $P,Q,R$ normalized all edges, after absorbing orientation signs into the $u_{ij}$ they would satisfy $u_{12}P^{\mathsf T}\Omega Q=I_6$, $u_{13}P^{\mathsf T}\Omega R=I_6$, and $u_{23}Q^{\mathsf T}\Omega R=I_6$. The first two equations give $Q=(u_{13}/u_{12})R$. The third would then make a nonzero scalar multiple of $R^{\mathsf T}\Omega R$ equal to $I_6$. But $R^{\mathsf T}\Omega R$ is alternating and has zero diagonal, whereas $I_6$ has diagonal entries one. This contradiction occurs on the stack chart itself and does not depend on passage to a coarse space.
