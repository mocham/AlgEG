# Statement


Let $\Gamma$ be a profinite group, let $k$ be a field, and let $A'\twoheadrightarrow A$ be a surjection of Artinian local $k$-algebras with square-zero kernel $I$. Let $1\to V\to P\to L\to1$ be an extension of affine $k$-groups in which $V$ is a vector group and the conjugation action on $V$ factors through $L$, and assume that $P(A')\to P(A)\times_{L(A)}L(A')$ is surjective. Given a continuous representation $\rho_A:\Gamma\to P(A)$ and a continuous representation $\rho_L':\Gamma\to L(A')$ whose reduction is the projection of $\rho_A$, there is a canonical obstruction class $o(\rho_A,\rho_L')\in H^2(\Gamma,V_{\rho_A}\otimes_k I)$. The pair $(\rho_A,\rho_L')$ lifts to a continuous representation $\rho_P':\Gamma\to P(A')$ if and only if $o(\rho_A,\rho_L')=0$. When $o(\rho_A,\rho_L')=0$, the set of lifts modulo conjugation by $V\otimes_k I$ is a torsor under $H^1(\Gamma,V_{\rho_A}\otimes_k I)$. In particular, vanishing of $H^2(\Gamma,V_{\rho_A}\otimes_k I)$ guarantees a lift through the vector-group layer.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Choose continuous pointwise lifts $p_\gamma\in P(A')$ of the compatible pair $(\rho_A(\gamma),\rho_L'(\gamma))$. Their multiplication defect
\[
 d(\gamma,\eta)=p_\gamma p_\eta p_{\gamma\eta}^{-1}
\]
lies in the square-zero kernel $V_{\rho_A}\otimes_k I$. Associativity gives the inhomogeneous two-cocycle identity for $d$, with the $\Gamma$-action induced by $\rho_A$. Replacing the chosen lifts by a one-cochain $a_\gamma\in V_{\rho_A}\otimes_k I$ changes $d$ by the coboundary of $a$. Thus the cohomology class $[d]$ is independent of the choices.

The chosen lifts form a homomorphism exactly when $d=0$. Consequently they can be corrected to a representation exactly when $[d]=0$ in $H^2$. If two corrected lifts exist, their ratio is a one-cocycle; conjugating by an element of $V\otimes_k I$ changes that cocycle by a one-coboundary. This identifies lift classes with an $H^1$-torsor. Continuity may be checked on the finite quotient through which the data over the Artinian discrete rings factor.
