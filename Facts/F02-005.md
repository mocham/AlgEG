# Statement


Let $(G,B,T)$ be split pinned reductive with dual Borel $\bB=U\rtimes\bT$ and positive-root set $\Phi^+$. Let $K\subset U$ be a $\bT$-stable normal subgroup and set
\[
\Phi_K:=\{\alpha\in\Phi^+:U_{\alpha^\vee}\subset K\}.
\]
Then $K=\prod_{\substack{\alpha\in\Phi^+\\U_{\alpha^\vee}\subset K}}U_{\alpha^\vee}$. Define its descending central series by $\Lie K_1:=\Lie K$ and $\Lie K_h:=[\Lie K,\Lie K_{h-1}]$ for $h>1$, let $K_h$ be the corresponding subgroup, and put $\bar K_h:=K_h/K_{h+1}$. The $K$-height of a root $\alpha\in\Phi_K$ is defined by
\[
\Ht_K(\alpha)=h\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad U_{\alpha^\vee}\subset\bar K_h,
\qquad
\bar K_h=\prod_{\substack{\alpha\in\Phi^+\\\Ht_K(\alpha)=h}}U_{\alpha^\vee}.
\]
The set of $K$-simple roots is $\Delta_K:=\{\alpha\in\Phi_K: \Ht_K(\alpha)=1\}$. In general $\Ht_K$ need not be additive; it is additive when $K$ is the unipotent radical of a parabolic.
