# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let $G$ be a flat affine group scheme of finite type over $\mathbb Z_p$. Let $\mathcal X_G$ be the Emerton-Gee stack of etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-modules with $G$-structure, and let $\mathfrak X_G$ be the derived stack of Laurent $F$-crystals with $G$-structure on the transversal absolute prismatic site of $\mathcal O_K$. Then there is an equivalence of stacks $\mathcal X_G\simeq{}^{\mathrm{cl}}\mathfrak X_G$ between the Emerton-Gee stack and the underlying classical stack of $\mathfrak X_G$.

### Expanded argument

Let $R$ be a discrete $p$-nilpotent ring. By the definition of the underlying classical prestack and Tannaka duality for the flat affine group scheme $G$, the source gives a natural equivalence
\[
{}^{\mathrm{cl}}\mathfrak X_G(R)\simeq\operatorname{Fun}^{\mathrm{ex},\otimes}\bigl(\operatorname{Rep}(G),\mathcal C_{\mathrm{Prism}}(K,R)\bigr).
\]
By D02-003, $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{Prism}}(K,R)$ is naturally exact-tensor equivalent to $\operatorname{Mod}^{\varphi,\Gamma}(\mathbf A_{K,R})$. Therefore
\[
{}^{\mathrm{cl}}\mathfrak X_G(R)\simeq\operatorname{Fun}^{\mathrm{ex},\otimes}\bigl(\operatorname{Rep}(G),\operatorname{Mod}^{\varphi,\Gamma}(\mathbf A_{K,R})\bigr)=\mathcal X_G(R).
\]
These equivalences are natural in $R$ and compatible with descent, so they assemble to the claimed equivalence of stacks.
