# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let $F_n/F$ be the unramified extension of degree $n$. Let $\mathbf G$ be a connected reductive group over $\overline{\mathbb Z}_p$ with connected center and maximal torus $\mathbf T$, let $\mathbf M\subsetneq\mathbf G$ be a proper Levi subgroup, and let $U^{\mathbf M}$ be the unipotent radical of a parabolic with Levi $\mathbf M$. Choose a cocharacter $\lambda\in X_*(\mathbf T)$, an elliptic Weyl element $w$ of order $n$ in $\mathbf M$, a representative $\dot w\in N_{\mathbf G}(\mathbf T)(\overline{\mathbb Z}_p)$, and an element $\sigma\in G_F$ whose image generates $G_F/G_{F_n}$. Let $\chi_{\mathrm{LT}}:G_{F_n}\to\mathcal O_{F_n}^{\times}$ be the Lubin-Tate character, put $\rho=\chi_{\mathrm{LT}}^{\lambda}$, and define
\[
\psi(g)=\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}\dot w^i\rho(\sigma^{-i}g\sigma^i)\dot w^{-i}\qquad(g\in G_{F_n}).
\]
Assume $\psi(\sigma^n)=\dot w^n$. For the unique expression $g\sigma^k$ with $g\in G_{F_n}$ and $0\le k<n$, put
\[
N_w(\chi_{\mathrm{LT}}^{\lambda})(g\sigma^k)=\psi(g)\dot w^k.
\]
This is a representation $G_F\to\mathbf T(\overline{\mathbb Z}_p)\langle\dot w\rangle$. Define $\underline\lambda_w\in X^*(\operatorname{Res}_{F/\mathbb Q_p}\mathbf T)$ to be its Hodge type. Define
\[
\mathcal X_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}^{\underline\lambda_w,\mathrm{tame}}
\]
to be the integral moduli stack of potentially semistable $U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle$-valued Galois representations of Hodge type $\underline\lambda_w$ and tame inertial type. Define
\[
\mathfrak X_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}^{\underline\lambda_w,\mathrm{tame}}
\]
to be its rigid generic fiber.
