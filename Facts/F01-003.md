# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be a finite extension and let $\bG$ be a connected reductive group with connected center. Assume $p>h_{\bG}$, where $h_{\bG}$ is the Coxeter number of $\bG$, and let $\bB$ be a Borel subgroup of $\bG$. Write $\cX_{\bB,\red}^{\EG}$ for the reduced Borel-valued Emerton--Gee stack. If
\[
\dim \cX_{\bB,\red}^{\EG}\le [F:\Q_p]\dim(\bG/\bB),
\]
then $\cX_{\bB,\red}^{\EG}$ is equidimensional of dimension $[F:\Q_p]\dim(\bG/\bB)$.

## Expanded self-contained proof

### Proof

Put d=[F:Q_p] dim(G/B). For a Borel subgroup, dim(G/B) is the number #Phi+ of positive roots, so d=[F:Q_p]#Phi+.

The proved form of Corollary cor:XB-dim in the source (lines 2756-2773) says that every irreducible component of the reduced Borel-valued stack X_B has dimension at least [F:Q_p]#Phi+. Its proof is the induction supplied by the preceding polynomial charts: at each positive root alpha, the next destackified chart is either an affine-space extension or a single-hypersurface extension (lines 2718-2733). Krull principal ideal theory therefore gives the required lower-dimensional growth at every step (lines 2739-2754), and induction over the positive roots gives the stated lower bound. The standing assumptions p>h_G and connected center are exactly those under which these charts and the truncated exponential are constructed.

By the hypothesis dim X_{B,red}^{EG} <= d, every irreducible component also has dimension at most d. Combining this with the lower bound, every irreducible component has dimension exactly d. Hence X_{B,red}^{EG} is equidimensional of dimension [F:Q_p] dim(G/B).
