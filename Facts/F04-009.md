# Statement


Let $F_n/F$ be an unramified extension of $p$-adic fields of degree $n$ with $(n,p)=1$, and let $\bfE_F$ and $\bfE_{F_n}$ be the characteristic-$p$ fields of norms attached to the cyclotomic towers of $F$ and $F_n$. Then $\bfE_{F_n}/\bfE_F$ is an unramified extension of degree $n$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

### Proof

Put L=F(mu_{p^infinity})^{Delta_F}. Every finite subextension of L/F has p-power degree. Since [F_n:F]=n is prime to p, F_n and L are linearly disjoint over F. Consequently L_n:=F_nL satisfies [L_n:L]=n. Moreover, base change of the unramified extension F_n/F along L/F remains unramified, so L_n/L is unramified of degree n and its residue-field extension has degree n.

For the cyclotomic field-of-norms construction one may choose compatible norm parameters so that

E_F = k_L((T)) and E_{F_n}=k_{L_n}((T)).

Under these identifications the induced inclusion E_F -> E_{F_n} is extension of the constant residue field k_L -> k_{L_n}; the uniformizer T is unchanged. It therefore has ramification index one and residue degree n, hence is unramified of degree n.

### Faithful source argument

The source observes that L/F is pro-p and that F_nL/L is unramified of degree n; the field-of-norms Laurent-series description then gives the conclusion above.
