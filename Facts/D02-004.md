# Statement


Let $F_n/F$ be unramified of degree $n$ prime to $p$, put $R=\overline{\mathbb F}_p[t^{\pm1}]$ and $\Delta_n=\operatorname{Gal}(F_n/F)$, and let $M^{\prime}$ be the universal unramified-twist rank-one etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $F_n$ obtained by base change from $F$. Put $d_F=[F:\mathbb Q_p]$ and $d_{k_F}=[k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}:\mathbb F_p]$. Then there is an isomorphism of $R[\Delta_n]$-modules \[H^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M^{\prime})\cong R[\Delta_n]^{\oplus(d_F+d_{k_F})}.\]

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Apply COR4-001 with $G=\Delta_n$. It gives an isomorphism of $R[\Delta_n]$-modules
\[H^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M')\cong R[\Delta_n]^{\oplus d_F}\oplus\bigl(R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_{F_{n,\mathrm{cyc}}}\bigr)_{\bar\chi}.\]
The finite-field normal basis theorem gives
\[k_{F_{n,\mathrm{cyc}}}\cong\mathbb F_p[\Delta_n]^{\oplus d_{k_F}}.\]
Tensoring with $R$ gives $R[\Delta_n]^{\oplus d_{k_F}}$, and diagonally twisting a regular representation by the one-dimensional character $\bar\chi$ does not change its isomorphism class. Combining the two summands yields
\[H^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M')\cong R[\Delta_n]^{\oplus(d_F+d_{k_F})},\]
as claimed.
