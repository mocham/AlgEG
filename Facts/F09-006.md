# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let $T/K$ be an unramified torus. Every continuous character $\bar f:T(K)\to\overline{\mathbb F}_p^\times$ admits a crystalline lift $f:T(K)\to\mathcal O_{\overline{\mathbb Q}_p}^\times$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let `C=T_int(O_K)` and `A=T(K)/C`. The Kottwitz quotient `A` is finitely generated. Apply D01-008 directly to `bar f`: it gives a crystalline character `g_0:T(K)->Qpbar^x` which is unit-valued on `C` and reduces there to `bar f`.

Because `v_p(g_0)` vanishes on `C`, it is a homomorphism `nu:A->Q`. Write `A` as a finite torsion group plus a free group. The map `nu` kills the torsion. Choose on a basis of the free part a character `u:A->Qpbar^x` whose valuation is `-nu`, and take `u=1` on the torsion. The inflated `u` is trivial on `C`, so F09-027 makes it crystalline. Consequently `g_1=u g_0` is crystalline and unit-valued on all of `T(K)`, while its compact reduction is still `bar f|_C`.

The character `r=bar f bar g_1^{-1}` is now defined on all of `T(K)` and is trivial on `C`. It factors through the finitely generated group `A`, hence has finite image in the torsion group `Fbar_p^x`. The multiplicative Teichmuller section gives a continuous character `[r]:A->O_Qpbar^x`. Since `[r]` is trivial on `C`, F09-027 makes it crystalline. Thus `[r]g_1` is an integral crystalline character whose reduction is `bar f` everywhere.

This valuation correction supplies the step omitted by the printed proof before it reduces the norm-descended algebraic character globally.
