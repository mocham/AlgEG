# Statement


Let $F_n/F$ be unramified of degree $n$ prime to $p$, let $A$ be a $\bFp$-algebra, let $\varphi$ denote absolute Frobenius, let $\Gamma$ denote the cyclotomic Galois group, and let $\sigma$ generate $\Gal(\bfE_{F_n}/\bfE_F)$. Base change along $\bfE_{F,A}\to\bfE_{F_n,A}$ is an equivalence between:

1. etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-modules $(M,\phi_M,\gamma_M)$ over $\bfE_{F,A}$; and
2. etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-modules $(M',\phi_{M'},\gamma_{M'})$ over $\bfE_{F_n,A}$ equipped with an isomorphism $\sigma_{M'}:\sigma^*M'\xrightarrow{\sim}M'$ that commutes with $\phi_{M'}$ and $\gamma_{M'}$ and satisfies the cyclic cocycle condition.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Set $C=\bfE_{F,A}$, $C_n=\bfE_{F_n,A}$, and $G=\operatorname{Gal}(\bfE_{F_n}/\bfE_F)=\langle\sigma\rangle$. By F04-009, $\bfE_{F_n}/\bfE_F$ is unramified of degree $n$, hence finite etale Galois; after extension of coefficients to $A$, $C_n/C$ is still finite faithfully flat with its $G$-action. Faithfully flat Galois descent therefore gives an equivalence between finite projective $C$-modules and finite projective $C_n$-modules endowed with semilinear $G$-descent data. Since $G$ is cyclic, such data are equivalently an isomorphism $\sigma^*M^{\prime}\to M^{\prime}$ whose $n$ iterates compose to the identity. The unramified Galois action on $C_n$ commutes with absolute Frobenius and with the cyclotomic $\Gamma$-action. Consequently the semilinear Frobenius and $\Gamma$ operators on a base-changed module are morphisms of descent data and descend uniquely; conversely, operators commuting with the descent datum arise by base change from the descended module. The relations between $\varphi$ and $\Gamma$ are preserved and reflected by faithful flatness. Finally, the existence of an etale lattice is fpqc local for this finite etale extension, so the etaleness condition is likewise preserved and reflected. Base change and descent are thus quasi-inverse on the two stated categories.
