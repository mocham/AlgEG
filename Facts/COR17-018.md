# Statement


Let \(F/\mathbb Q_p\) be finite, let \(\mathbf G\) satisfy the standing split, connected-center hypotheses, and assume \(p>h_{\mathbf G}\). Retain the twisted Levi, prime-to-\(p\) Weyl descent, relative-height filtration, normalized universal unramified-twist family, and complete invariant BCH chart setup. Let \(\boldsymbol\alpha\) be a relative-root orbit of cardinality \(q\), put
\[
N=[F:\mathbb Q_p]+[k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}:\mathbb F_p],
\]
and let \(S=X_{<\boldsymbol\alpha}(C)\) be the represented lower recursive chart. Take \(U_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\) in the central relative-height associated graded layer.

Locally over \(S\), the next chart has one of the forms
\[
X_{\le\boldsymbol\alpha}(C)\cong
\begin{cases}
S\times\mathbb A^{qN},&H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(U_{\boldsymbol\alpha})=0,\\[2mm]
\operatorname{Spec}_S\!\left(
\mathcal O_S[s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}]/
(s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}+A_{\boldsymbol\alpha})
\right)\times_S\mathbb A_S^{qN},&H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(U_{\boldsymbol\alpha})\ne0,
\end{cases}
\]
where \(u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\) is the simple Cartier parameter for the cyclotomic divisor and \(A_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\) is any lift of the coefficient of the complete invariant BCH obstruction. Replacing \(A_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\) by \(A_{\boldsymbol\alpha}+u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}Q\) is carried by \(s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\mapsto s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}-Q\).

Consequently, if every irreducible component of \(S\) has dimension at least \(d\), then every irreducible component of \(X_{\le\boldsymbol\alpha}(C)\) has dimension at least
\[
d+qN.
\]
Iterating over the relative central series gives the same additive lower bound layer by layer.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

COR2-002 makes \(U_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\) a central commutative layer, so lifting a lower object amounts to choosing one modified-Herr cochain whose differential equals the complete BCH obstruction. Shapiro over the unramified orbit-splitting field and D04-006 give \(qN\) free modified-Herr coordinates. Because the splitting degree is prime to \(p\), Reynolds invariants are exact; the descent equations select this invariant direct summand and create no additional obstruction classes.

If top Herr cohomology vanishes, every obstruction has a primitive and the next stage is the affine bundle shown above. At a cyclotomic crossing, the \(q\) absolute top classes form one transitive orbit, so the invariant top cohomology is the single simple Cartier module \(R/(u_{\boldsymbol\alpha})\). D04-001 represents its first Tor by one coordinate \(s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\). COR14-003 identifies the inhomogeneous class with the invariant projection of the complete absolute-root BCH polynomial, and COR6-003 gives the displayed equation and triangular change of coefficient lift. Centrality ensures that there are no further equations.

In the crossing case the next chart is one principal hypersurface in \(S\times\mathbb A^{qN+1}\); otherwise it is \(S\times\mathbb A^{qN}\). Krull's principal ideal theorem therefore gives the lower bound \(d+qN\) in both cases. Induction along the relative-height central filtration proves the final assertion, exactly as in Lin25's upper-central-series argument.
