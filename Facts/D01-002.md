# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $L/K$ be finite Galois, let $E/\mathbb Q_l$ be finite, and let $G$ be a possibly disconnected reductive group over $E$. Let $Y_{L/K,\varphi,\mathcal N}$ be the finite-type affine $E$-scheme of triples $(\Phi,N,\tau)$ defining framed $G$-valued Weil-Deligne representations trivial on $I_L$, and let $G\text{-}\mathrm{WD}_E(L/K)=[Y_{L/K,\varphi,\mathcal N}/G]$. Then the stack is generically smooth and equidimensional of dimension zero, while $Y_{L/K,\varphi,\mathcal N}$ is generically smooth, reduced, locally a complete intersection, and equidimensional of dimension $\dim G$. Its nonsmooth locus is exactly the locus where $H^2(\operatorname{ad}D)\ne0$. A closed point is called very smooth when its image after every finite extension $K^{\prime}/K$ is smooth in the corresponding Weil-Deligne moduli space; the very smooth closed points are Zariski dense.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

BG19 proves generic smoothness by constructing a dense open on which the obstruction group $H^2(\operatorname{ad}D)$ vanishes. Its deformation complex has Euler characteristic zero, so the tangent-space dimension is $\dim G+\dim H^2(\operatorname{ad}D)$; this gives both equidimensionality and the stated description of the nonsmooth locus. For a fixed inertial representation $\tau$, the framed scheme is cut out inside the smooth space $Z_G(\tau)\times\operatorname{Lie}Z_G(\tau)$ by $\dim Z_G(\tau)$ equations. Etale-local sections of $G\to G/Z_G(\tau)$ therefore make the whole framed scheme a local complete intersection. It is Cohen-Macaulay and generically reduced, hence reduced. For very smoothness, BG19 shows that over any fixed finite coefficient extension one finite extension $K^{\prime}/K$ detects smoothness after all finite extensions. Intersecting the dense $H^2$-vanishing open for that $K^{\prime}$ with a Zariski-dense set of coefficient-rational points yields a Zariski-dense set of very smooth points.
