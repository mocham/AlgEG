# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $(G,B,T)$ be split pinned reductive with dual pinned group $(\bG,\bB,\bT)$, and fix a positive root $\alpha$. On the unramified root-character locus, let $\mathcal X_{\leq\alpha,\mathrm{un}}$ classify etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-structured $(U_{\leq\alpha}\rtimes\bT)$-torsors $P'_A$ over $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf E_{F,A}$, with induced $(U_{<\alpha}\rtimes\bT)$-torsor $P_A$. Let $q:\mathcal X^+_{\leq\alpha,\mathrm{un}}\to\mathcal X_{\leq\alpha,\mathrm{un}}$ be the exact forgetful morphism whose objects over $P'_A$ are $\Gamma$-stable sections $\xi:P_A\to P'_A$. For an $A$-point $x$, choose an ordinary section $\xi_0$, let $M_{0,x}$ be the module of differences of sections, and let $o_x\in H^1(\Gamma,M_{0,x})$ be the affine obstruction class. Define $\mathcal X^{\Gamma\text{-split}}_{\leq\alpha,\mathrm{un}}$ to be the fppf substack on which $o_x$ becomes zero after an fppf cover. Suppose the sheaves $B\mapsto(M_{0,x,B})^\Gamma$ glue, compatibly with base change, to the additive group $\mathcal V$ of a rank $d=[k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}:\mathbb F_p]$ vector bundle. The morphism $q$ is a $\mathcal V$-torsor over $\mathcal X^{\Gamma\text{-split}}_{\leq\alpha,\mathrm{un}}$ and
\[
[\mathcal X^+_{\leq\alpha,\mathrm{un}}/\mathcal V]\cong\mathcal X^{\Gamma\text{-split}}_{\leq\alpha,\mathrm{un}}.
\]
Under the vector-bundle and base-change hypotheses, fppf-local vanishing of $o_x$ over every affine coefficient scheme $\operatorname{Spec}A$ is equivalent to global vanishing. For a chosen base-change-compatible trivialization $\mathcal V\cong\mathbb G_a^{\oplus d}$, one also has
\[
[\mathcal X^+_{\leq\alpha,\mathrm{un}}/\mathbb G_a^{\oplus d}]\cong\mathcal X^{\Gamma\text{-split}}_{\leq\alpha,\mathrm{un}}.
\]

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The projection $P'_A\to P_A$ is an ordinary additive torsor over the affine scheme $P_A$, so its quasi-coherent $H^1$ vanishes and an ordinary section $\xi_0$ exists. This does not make $\xi_0$ $\Gamma$-stable. By D12-002, the cocycle $c_\gamma=\gamma(\xi_0)-\xi_0$ defines an intrinsic class $o_x$, and after every equivariant base change the fixed-section sheaf is nonempty exactly when the pulled-back class vanishes.

The difference of two $\Gamma$-stable sections is in $(M_{0,x,B})^\Gamma$, and translation by such an invariant takes one stable section to another. Hence the fixed-section sheaf is a pseudo-torsor under $\mathcal V$. By the definition of the $\Gamma$-split locus it is fppf locally nonempty there, so it is a $\mathcal V$-torsor. The map in this assertion is precisely $q$, which forgets the section while retaining the same $P'_A$; no different modified-Herr or coefficient morphism is substituted. The standard quotient of a torsor by its acting group gives the displayed isomorphism.

Over $\operatorname{Spec}A$, a torsor under the additive group of a vector bundle is a torsor under a quasi-coherent module. Its fppf cohomology in degree one agrees with quasi-coherent cohomology and vanishes on an affine scheme. Thus a locally existing stable section can be replaced by a global one, so local vanishing of $o_x$ is equivalent to $o_x=0$. Finally, a chosen base-change-compatible trivialization of $\mathcal V$ identifies its action with that of $\mathbb G_a^{\oplus d}$. COR6-002 gives the corresponding invariant-module formula only after choosing an invariant basis in the universal Laurent family; the extra gluing and base-change hypothesis here is therefore explicit rather than inferred from that calculation.
