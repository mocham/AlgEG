# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, let $\bG$ be a connected reductive group with connected center and Coxeter number $h_{\bG}$, and assume $p>h_{\bG}$. Put $\dF=[F:\Q_p]$, let $\bB=U\rtimes\bT$ be a split Borel subgroup of $\bG$ with positive roots $\Phi^+$, and let $\cX_{\bB}(C)$ be the locus over an irreducible component $C$ of the mod-$p$ Borel Weil--Deligne stack $\cW_{\bB}$. If
\[
\dF\ge\#\Phi^++2,
\]
then every $\cX_{\bB}(C)$ is irreducible. Consequently, $\cX_{\bB}\to\cW_{\bB}$ is generically smooth and induces a bijection on irreducible components.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

F01-001 supplies the connected-center and Coxeter-bound standing hypotheses, COR14-002 supplies the corrected fine-locus equations, COR15-001 supplies the exact change-of-group dimension formula, COR15-003 supplies the regular full-unipotent top-component argument, and COR17-005 supplies the sharpened codimension estimate for every non-full stratum.

Put
\[
N=\#\Phi^+,\qquad \ell=\operatorname{rank}\Phi,\qquad m=\#\Phi_K,
\]
and, for a fine configuration $\Psi=(\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$, put
\[
S=\Phi_K\cap\Psi_2,\qquad a=\#S,\qquad q=\dim_{\mathbb Q}\operatorname{Span}_{\mathbb Q}(\Psi_2),\qquad s=\#((\Phi^+\setminus\Phi_K)\cap\Psi_0).
\]
The linear equations in COR14-002 leave only the $a$ coordinates $s_\alpha$ indexed by $S$ and cut the torus-coordinate space in codimension $q$. After the exact stabilizer subtraction of COR15-001, COR17-005 therefore gives
\[
\dim\cX_{\bB}[K]\langle\Psi\rangle\le \dF m+\ell+a-q-s.
\]
If $r(S)=\dim_{\mathbb Q}\operatorname{Span}_{\mathbb Q}(S)$, then $S\subseteq\Psi_2$ gives $q\ge r(S)$. Monotonicity of nullity in the vector matroid on $\Phi^+$ gives
\[
a-q\le a-r(S)\le N-\ell.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\dim\cX_{\bB}[K]\langle\Psi\rangle\le \dF m+N-s
\]
and hence
\[
\dF N-\dim\cX_{\bB}[K]\langle\Psi\rangle\ge \dF(N-m)-N+s.
\]
For $K\ne U$, one has $N-m\ge1$. At the stated threshold $\dF\ge N+2$, the right-hand side is at least $2+s>0$. Thus every non-full fine stratum has strict positive codimension relative to the top Borel dimension $\dF N$. The complete BCH terminal equations omitted in deriving this upper bound can only lower the dimension, so they do not weaken the estimate.

Fix an irreducible component $C\subset\cW_{\bB}$. COR15-003 gives the regular full-unipotent stratum attached to $(\Psi_0(C),\Psi_2(C))$ an irreducible closure $Z_C$ which is a top component of $\cX_{\bB}(C)$; its top-stratum purity and generic-image argument also exclude every non-component-indexing full-unipotent pair from contributing an irreducible component. The fine stratification is finite. Its purity input forces the generic point of any irreducible component of $\cX_{\bB}(C)$ to occur in top dimension, while the strict estimate above excludes every stratum with $K\ne U$. The only remaining generic stratum is the component-indexing regular full-unipotent stratum, so every irreducible component equals $Z_C$. Hence $\cX_{\bB}(C)$ is irreducible.

The same regular full-unipotent stratum is smooth over the dense corresponding Weil--Deligne fine locus used in COR15-003. Since it is dense in $\cX_{\bB}(C)$, the morphism $\cX_{\bB}\to\cW_{\bB}$ is generically smooth over every $C$. Moreover, $C\mapsto Z_C$ gives one irreducible component of $\cX_{\bB}$ with dense image in each component $C$ of $\cW_{\bB}$, and the preceding generic-point argument shows that every component arises uniquely in this way. This proves the asserted bijection on irreducible components.
