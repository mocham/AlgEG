# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $R=\overline{\mathbb F}_p[t^{\pm1}]$, and let $M$ be an unramified rank-one etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $\mathbf E_{F,R}$. Choose an unramified basis $e$ for which $M=\mathbf E_{F,R}e$ and $\gamma(e)=e$ for every $\gamma\in\Gamma$. Then $$M^\Gamma=(\mathbf E_{F,R}^\Gamma)e=\left(R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}\right)e.$$ In particular, the chosen basis gives an isomorphism $$M^\Gamma\cong R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}},$$ and this identification is basis-dependent.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

For $m=ae\in M$, the equality $\gamma(m)=m$ for every $\gamma\in\Gamma$ is equivalent to $\gamma(a)=a$, because the chosen unramified basis is fixed by $\Gamma$. Hence $M^\Gamma=\mathbf E_{F,R}^\Gamma e$. Dependency S03-001 identifies the invariants of this exact Laurent-family coefficient ring with $R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}$, proving the displayed formula. Sending $ae$ to $a$ gives the final isomorphism. Replacing $e$ by another invariant basis rescales that isomorphism, which is why the literal equality in F01-024 requires the omitted basis convention.
