# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, let $\bB=U\rtimes\bT$ be a split Borel subgroup, let $K\subset U$ be a $\bT$-stable normal subgroup, and let $X_{K\rtimes\bT}$ be the destackified moduli scheme of $(K\rtimes\bT)$-valued representations. For $\Psi=(\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$ with $\Psi_0,\Psi_2\subset\Phi^+$, define $X_{K\rtimes\bT}\langle\Psi\rangle\subset X_{K\rtimes\bT}$ to be the locally closed locus whose $\bFp$-points have torus semisimplification $\bar\rho^{\mathrm{ss}}:\Gal_F\to\bT(\bFp)$ satisfying, for every $\alpha\in\Phi^+$,
\[
H^2(\Gal_F,U_{\alpha^\vee}(\bFp))\ne0\Longleftrightarrow\alpha\in\Psi_2,\qquad H^0(\Gal_F,U_{\alpha^\vee}(\bFp))\ne0\Longleftrightarrow\alpha\in\Psi_0.
\]

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

### Well-definedness of the locus

For a geometric point of X_{K semidirect T}, its torus semisimplification makes each one-dimensional root group U_{alpha-dual}(Fpbar) a Galois module with character chi_alpha. Then H^0 is nonzero exactly when chi_alpha is trivial. By local Tate duality, H^2(Gal_F,U_{alpha-dual}(Fpbar)) is dual to H^0 of the dual module twisted by the mod-p cyclotomic character, so H^2 is nonzero exactly when chi_alpha is the mod-p cyclotomic character.

On the torus-character moduli space, the conditions chi_alpha=1 and chi_alpha=epsilon-bar are closed conditions. Their pullbacks along the torus-semisimplification morphism are therefore closed in X_{K semidirect T}. Since Phi^+ is finite, requiring these equalities for alpha in Psi_0 or Psi_2 and requiring their complements for all other positive roots is a finite intersection of closed and open loci, hence a canonical locally closed locus. Its geometric points are exactly those specified in the statement.
