# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $G/\mathbb Z_p$ be connected reductive, and let $R$ be a truncated Artinian local animated p-nilpotent ring with finite residue field. If $\mathfrak X_G$ is the derived stack of Laurent $F$-crystals with $G$-structure and $\mathcal F_{K,G}$ is the derived unframed continuous Galois-representation functor, then there is a natural equivalence $\mathfrak X_G(R)\simeq\mathcal F_{K,G}(R)$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Min25 compares both sides with the same descent anima $BG(R,G_K)$. For the Laurent-crystal side, the prismatic period-ring comparison is fully faithful on all cosimplicial continuous-cochain terms and an equivalence in degree zero; the source proves this first for discrete $R$ by Tannaka duality and then inductively on the truncation degree using the cotangent complex. For the representation side, the geometric realization of continuous $G_K$-cochains gives the same $BG(R,G_K)$. Connected reductivity is used to show every $G$-torsor over $R$ is equivalent to the trivial one: Lang's theorem gives the finite-field base case, and square-zero Postnikov pullbacks give the induction. Thus both constructions are naturally equivalent on every stated $R$.
