# Statement

Let K/Q_p be finite, let P=U semidirect M be a parabolic, and let rho:Gal_K->M(Qpbar) be crystalline with P-minus-dominant Hodge type. If H^2(Gal_K,U(Qpbar))=0, then every P-valued extension of rho is crystalline.

# Proof or Examples

## Expanded self-contained proof

### Proof

By Corollary 4 in the cited work, every lift rho_prime: Gal_K -> P(Qpbar) of rho is semistable, and the possible Weil-Deligne types of such lifts are parameterized by H^2(Gal_K,U(Qpbar)). Under the hypothesis that this group is zero, there is therefore only one possible Weil-Deligne type.

The split lift Gal_K -> M(Qpbar) -> P(Qpbar) is crystalline because rho is crystalline. Hence its Weil-Deligne type is crystalline (in particular, its monodromy is zero and its inertial Weil action is trivial). Every P-valued lift has this same type. Since every lift is already semistable, the standard semistable/crystalline criterion now shows that every lift is crystalline.

### Faithful quotation

```text
Corollary 5. Let rho : GalK -> M(Qpbar) be a crystalline representation whose Hodge type is P-minus-dominant. Endow Lie U(Qpbar) with GalK-action via ad rho. Assume H^2(GalK,U(Qpbar)) = 0. Then any rho_prime : GalK -> P(Qpbar) such that rho_prime x_P M = rho is crystalline.

Proof. By Corollary 4, the Weil-Deligne type of rho_prime is unique. But rho itself composed with M -> P is a crystalline representation.
```
