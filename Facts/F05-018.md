# Statement


Let $A$ be the coordinate ring of an irreducible affine variety over $\overline{\mathbb F}_p$, let $B=A[s_1,\dots,s_m]/(f_1,\dots,f_m)$, and let $g:\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ be the induced morphism. If there is a point $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}B$ at which the square Jacobian matrix $(\partial f_i/\partial s_j)_{1\leq i,j\leq m}$ is invertible, then $g(\operatorname{Spec}B)$ contains an open neighborhood of $g(\mathfrak p)$. In particular, after replacing both spectra by nonempty open subschemes, $g$ is etale and surjective.

## Expanded self-contained proof

### Faithful original proof

```tex
\begin{proof}
Let $\cU_B \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(B)$ be the principal open subscheme where the determinant of the Jacobian matrix $J$ is invertible. By hypothesis, the point $\mathfrak{p}$ lies in $\cU_B$.
The morphism $g$ restricted to $\cU_B$ is \'etale and thus
has open image.
\end{proof}
```
