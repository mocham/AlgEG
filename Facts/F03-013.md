# Statement


Let $\Phi$ be a reduced root system with positive roots $\Phi^+$ and simple roots $\Delta$. Give $\Phi^+$ the directed-graph structure in which $\beta\to\alpha$ exactly when $\alpha-\beta\in\Delta$. For each integer $h$, define $\fB(\Phi^+,\Delta,h)$ to be the induced subgraph on the roots of height $h$ or $h+1$. It is bipartite and is called the associated bipartite graph of height $h$.
