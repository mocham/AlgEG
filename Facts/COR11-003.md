# Statement


Let \(F/\mathbb Q_p\) be finite, put \(d=[F:\mathbb Q_p]\), and let \(\mathbf G\) be a connected reductive group with connected center and \(p>h_{\mathbf G}\). Let \(\mathbf B\) be a Borel, let \(N=\#\Phi^+\), and put \(D=dN\). For every fine configuration \((K,\Psi_0,\Psi_2)\), impose the same fine nonvanishing conditions on the corrected cone presentation obtained by retaining every linear and quadratic term and deleting only terms of degree at least three. Use the stabilizer subtraction of COR15-001 for its stack-adjusted dimension.

Assume either that \(\mathbf G\) has type \(F_4\) or that \(d>\dim(\mathbf G)/2\). Then every corrected open cone stratum has stack-adjusted dimension at most \(D\). Equality can occur only in the following cases.

1. \(K=U\) and
\[
\dim_{\mathbb Q}\operatorname{Span}_{\mathbb Q}\Psi_2
=
\dim_{\mathbb Q}\operatorname{Span}_{\mathbb Q}(\Psi_2\cap\Delta).
\]
The corrected cone and fine strata are smooth, and on every standard simple-root nonvanishing chart they are related by a finite root-height-triangular isomorphism inducing the identity on the quadratic associated graded.

2. \(F=\mathbb Q_p\), \(\mathbf G\) has type \(F_4\), and the configuration belongs to a Serre-indexed top-dimensional realization
\[
Z\in\operatorname{Irr}_{24}(S_j),
\qquad j\in\{\mathrm I,\mathrm {II},\mathrm {III},\mathrm {IV}\},
\]
of one of the four row types in COR18-001. In every such row realization \(\Psi_2\subseteq\Delta_K\), so the corrected cone and fine presentations are globally equal and smooth.

Every other corrected open cone stratum has stack-adjusted dimension strictly smaller than \(D\). No assertion is made that the chartwise triangular isomorphisms in case 1 glue to one global presentation isomorphism.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

COR11-001 writes the fine generators, modulo their common linear ideal, as complete quadratic terms plus terms of degree at least three; the cone retains the complete quadratic terms. For a non-full stratum in the large-degree range, COR17-005 uses only the linear equations common to both presentations. If \(m=\#\Phi_K\) and \(s=\#((\Phi^+\setminus\Phi_K)\cap\Psi_0)\), it gives
\[
D-\dim_{\mathrm{adj}}X^{\mathrm{cone}}_{K,\mathbf T}\langle\Psi\rangle
\ge d(N-m)-N+s.
\]
Since \(K\ne U\) gives \(N-m\ge1\) and \(d>\dim\mathbf G/2\) implies the integer inequality \(d\ge N+1\), this lower bound is positive.

In type \(F_4\), D11-001 gives the exhaustive corrected-cone equality rows, and COR18-001 refines those four row types by their Serre-indexed irreducible top-dimensional realizations. For \(d\ge2\), every non-full cone is strictly below \(24d\). For \(d=1\), exactly the realizations of rows I--IV attain 24. In each row \(\Psi_2\subseteq\Delta_K\), so no higher-root obstruction equation occurs and the fine and cone ideals are the same common linear ideal.

Now let \(K=U\) and work on a standard nonvanishing chart. For every obstruction height \(h\), F03-019 and the complete BCH formula give a matched pivot block \(\mathbf z_h\) and an invertible monomial matrix \(M_h\) such that
\[
\mathbf q_h=M_h\mathbf z_h+\mathbf b_h,
\qquad
\mathbf r_h=M_h\mathbf z_h+\mathbf b_h+\mathbf c_h,
\]
where \(\mathbf c_h\) contains only cubic and higher terms and neither \(\mathbf b_h\) nor \(\mathbf c_h\) depends on \(\mathbf z_h\). Inducting upward in root height, projection away from all pivot blocks identifies both presentations with the same polynomial/Laurent chart in the unmatched variables. The composition of the two projection inverses is a finite regular triangular isomorphism; its inverse is obtained by the same recursion. This proves chartwise smoothness and the dimension formula
\[
D-
\left(
\dim\operatorname{Span}_{\mathbb Q}\Psi_2-
\dim\operatorname{Span}_{\mathbb Q}(\Psi_2\cap\Delta)
\right),
\]
yielding exactly the stated equality condition.
