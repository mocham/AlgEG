# Statement


The type-$B_2$ data distinguish the complete class-three BCH obstruction from the incomplete one-sided ordered expression. Let $k=\mathbb F_{101}$ and let $G$ be split adjoint of type $B_2$, with positive roots $a,b,c=a+b,d=2a+b$ and brackets $[e_a,e_b]=e_c$ and $[e_a,e_c]=2e_d$. Put

$$F_0=e_a+e_b,\qquad G_0=2e_a+\frac43e_b+\frac13e_c.$$

Let $t_f$ have root eigenvalues $(2,4)$ on $(a,b)$ and let $t_g$ have root eigenvalues $(3,5)$. Then

$$\operatorname{BCH}(F_0,\operatorname{Ad}(t_f)G_0)=5e_a+\frac{19}{3}e_b+\frac{10}{3}e_c+2e_d,$$

$$\operatorname{BCH}(G_0,\operatorname{Ad}(t_g)F_0)=5e_a+\frac{19}{3}e_b+\frac{10}{3}e_c-2e_d.$$

Thus the lower data are a cocycle modulo $U_d$ and the corrected top-root obstruction is $-4e_d$. The incomplete pure ordered expression gives $-e_d/9$ instead. Consequently $c_{f,d}=e_d/396$, $c_{g,d}=0$ is rejected by the corrected equation even though it satisfies the incomplete expression, whereas $c_{f,d}=e_d/11$, $c_{g,d}=0$ satisfies the corrected top-root equation and the full cocycle equation. This is a regression example caught by the complete expansion, not a counterexample to that expansion.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

For $X=x_ae_a+x_be_b+x_ce_c$ and $Y=y_ae_a+y_be_b+y_ce_c$, the complete class-three formula gives

$$\pi_d\operatorname{BCH}(X,Y)=x_ay_c-x_cy_a+\frac16(x_a-y_a)(x_ay_b-x_by_a).$$

The two summands after the quadratic term arise from $[X,[X,Y]]/12$ and $[[X,Y],Y]/12$; both are required. Here

$$\operatorname{Ad}(t_f)G_0=4e_a+\frac{16}{3}e_b+\frac83e_c,\qquad \operatorname{Ad}(t_g)F_0=3e_a+5e_b.$$

Substitution gives $8/3-2/3=2$ for the first BCH top coordinate and $-1-1=-2$ for the second. Their difference in the sign convention of the Herr criterion is therefore $-4$. By contrast, the one-sided series has top coordinates $3$ and $28/9$, so its difference is $-1/9$; equivalently its quadratic difference is $-11/3$ and its two pure cubic contributions are $4$ and $-4/9$.

The linear top-root side for $c_{f,d}=xe_d$, $c_{g,d}=0$ is $(1-45)xe_d$. At $x=1/396$ it is $-e_d/9$ and fails the corrected equation. At $x=1/11$ it is $-4e_d$. The two full BCH top coordinates are then $2+1/11=23/11$ and $-2+45/11=23/11$, proving the full cocycle equality. Modulo $101$, the corrected and incomplete obstruction values are respectively $97$ and $56$.
