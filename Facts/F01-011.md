# Statement


For a stratum of the partially reduced Borel-valued Emerton--Gee stack presented by explicit polynomial equations, remove the cubic and higher-degree terms to obtain the truncated polynomial equations. The algebraic variety or algebraic stack defined by these truncated equations is called the *cone model* of the stratum.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

This is a definition relative to the fixed explicit polynomial presentation and its grading. Concretely, each defining polynomial f is replaced by its degree-at-most-two truncation f_{<=2}; the cone model is cut out by the resulting truncated equations. For example, x + y^2 + x y^2 + x^4 is replaced by x + y^2.

The definition alone does not assert that the construction is independent of generators, that the cone model is isomorphic to the original stratum, or that dimensions agree. The source separately says that Grobner-basis arguments prove non-drop of dimension in the applications.
