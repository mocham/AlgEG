# Statement


Let $k$ have characteristic $p>6$, let $\Phi$ be a root system of type $G_2$ with simple roots $a$ short and $b$ long, let $\mathbf M$ be the rank-one Levi generated by $a$, and put $w=s_a$. Then $w$ is elliptic in $W_{\mathbf M}$ and the radical-root orbit of $b$ is $\{b,3a+b\}$. In the dual-root indexing put $\delta=b^\vee$ and $\epsilon=(3a+b)^\vee$. The bare product $U_\delta U_\epsilon$ in the full positive unipotent group is not a subgroup. Both orbit roots have $\mathbf M$-relative height one, however, and the product of their images in $U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge1}/U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge2}$ is an order-independent commutative relative-root module.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The reflection formula gives $s_a(b)=b-\langle b,a^\vee\rangle a=b+3a$, so the orbit is $\{b,3a+b\}$. In the dual $G_2$ root system, $\delta+\epsilon=(3a+2b)^\vee$ is a root and the root string through $\epsilon$ in the $\delta$ direction contains $\epsilon-\delta=a^\vee$. The Chevalley bracket formula therefore gives $[e_\delta,e_\epsilon]=\pm2e_{\delta+\epsilon}$. This is nonzero because $p>6$. If the image of the multiplication map $U_\delta\times U_\epsilon\to U$ were a subgroup, its tangent space $\mathfrak g_\delta\oplus\mathfrak g_\epsilon$ would be a Lie subalgebra, contradicting this bracket. Hence the bare two-factor product is not a subgroup in the full unipotent group.

The coefficient of the simple root outside $\mathbf M$ is one for both $b$ and $3a+b$, so both have relative height one. Every commutator factor has relative height at least two and dies in $U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge1}/U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge2}$. The two images therefore commute there, and ordered-root factorization makes their product an order-independent vector subgroup, exactly as in COR2-002.

## Post-proof remarks

Reclassified after human review from a counterexample role to a verified example motivating COR2-002. The original orbit and nonclosure calculation is correct after replacing the claimed coefficient $\pm1$ by $\pm2$. It tests the bare product in the full unipotent group, whereas the surrounding source cohomology and filtration force the relative-root object to live in the relative-height associated graded. In that quotient the displayed commutator has height two and vanishes. Statement and proof were independently rechecked with the corrected root-string coefficient.
