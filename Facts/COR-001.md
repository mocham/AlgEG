# Statement


Let $\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$ be a split Borel subgroup over $\overline{\mathbf F}_p$, and let $\bar\rho:\operatorname{Gal}_F\to\mathbf B(\overline{\mathbf F}_p)$ be a Galois representation. Suppose $K\subset U$ is minimal among the $\mathbf T$-stable normal subgroups of $U$ such that a $\mathbf B(\overline{\mathbf F}_p)$-conjugate of $\bar\rho$ factors through $K\rtimes\mathbf T$, and choose such a conjugate. For the descending central series $K_1=K$ and $K_h=[K,K_{h-1}]$, put $\bar K_1=K_1/K_2$ and write the induced representation as $c\,\bar\rho^{\mathrm{ss}}$, where $c=\sum_{\operatorname{Ht}_K(\alpha)=1}c_\alpha$ and $[c_\alpha]\in H^1(\operatorname{Gal}_F,U_{\alpha^\vee}(\overline{\mathbf F}_p))$. For a root $\alpha$ of $K$-height one, put $K_{\widehat\alpha}=\prod_{\beta\in\Phi_K,\,\beta\ne\alpha}U_{\beta^\vee}$. If $K_{\widehat\alpha}$ is a $\mathbf T$-stable normal subgroup of $U$, then $[c_\alpha]\ne0$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Assume that $[c_\alpha]=0$. Choose an element of the root group $U_{\alpha^\vee}$ whose coboundary is $c_\alpha$. Conjugating $\bar\rho$ by this element kills the $\alpha$-coordinate of the cocycle in $K/K_2$; commutator corrections lie in higher $K$-height root groups and hence in $K_{\widehat\alpha}$. Consequently the conjugated representation factors through $K_{\widehat\alpha}\rtimes\mathbf T$. By hypothesis $K_{\widehat\alpha}$ is a $\mathbf T$-stable normal subgroup of $U$, and it is proper because its root set omits $\alpha$. This contradicts the defining minimality of $K$. Therefore $[c_\alpha]\ne0$.

The removability hypothesis is necessary for this minimality argument. In the type-$A_2$ example recorded in C04-002, removing $U_{13}$ from $K=U_{12}U_{13}$ leaves $U_{12}$, which is not normal in the full upper-unipotent group, so minimality cannot be applied to that root.
