# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $\mathbf G$ be a connected reductive group with connected center, assume $p>h_{\mathbf G}$, fix a Borel subgroup $\mathbf B$, and let $\Phi^+$ be the corresponding set of positive roots. Put $D=[F:\mathbb Q_p]\#\Phi^+$. Suppose the reduced Emerton-Gee stack $\mathcal X_{\mathbf G,\mathrm{red}}^{\mathrm{EG}}$ is equidimensional of dimension $D$. Let $C$ be an irreducible component of the Borel-valued mod-$p$ Weil-Deligne stack $\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}$, and suppose the maximally nonsplit locus $\dot{\mathcal X}_{\mathbf B}(C)$ is irreducible of dimension $D$ and has nonzero simple-root cohomology class at every geometric point. Then the scheme-theoretic image of $\dot{\mathcal X}_{\mathbf B}(C)$ in $\mathcal X_{\mathbf G,\mathrm{red}}^{\mathrm{EG}}$ is an irreducible component.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let $Y_C$ be the scheme-theoretic image. Because the source is irreducible, $Y_C$ is irreducible. COR5-001 gives $\dim Y_C\geq\dim\dot{\mathcal X}_{\mathbf B}(C)=D$. Since the ambient reduced stack is equidimensional of dimension $D$, every irreducible closed substack has dimension at most $D$, so $\dim Y_C=D$. Thus $Y_C$ is contained in an irreducible component $Z$ of dimension $D$ and has the same dimension as $Z$. A proper irreducible closed subset of the noetherian irreducible stack $Z$ has strictly smaller dimension. Hence $Y_C=Z$, proving that the image is an irreducible component.
