# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, and let $\mathbf G$ be a split connected reductive group over $\overline{\mathbb F}_p$ with connected center and Borel subgroup $\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$; assume $p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Let $\Delta$ be the simple positive roots, let $\mathbf M\subsetneq\mathbf G$ be a Levi subgroup containing $\mathbf T$ with simple roots $\Delta_{\mathbf M}$, let $U^{\mathbf M}$ be the unipotent radical of the parabolic with Levi $\mathbf M$, write $\Phi_{\mathbf M}$ for the roots of $\mathbf M$, and let $w\in N_{\mathbf M}(\mathbf T)$ represent an elliptic Weyl element. Fix an elliptic torus-normalizer Galois representation $\bar\rho^{\mathrm{ss}}:G_F\to\mathbf T(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)\langle w\rangle$.

For a positive root $\gamma=\sum_{\delta\in\Delta}n_\delta\delta$, put $\operatorname{Ht}^{\mathbf M}(\gamma)=\sum_{\delta\notin\Delta_{\mathbf M}}n_\delta$, and let $U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge h}$ be generated by root groups of relative height at least $h$. Let $\boldsymbol\Phi^+=\Phi^{\mathbf M+}/\langle w\rangle$ be the set of $w$-orbits of positive roots in $U^{\mathbf M}$. If $\boldsymbol\alpha\in\boldsymbol\Phi^+$ has relative height $h$, define $U_{\boldsymbol\alpha}$ to be the product of the images of its root groups in $U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge h}/U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge h+1}$; this is a commutative orbit module.

Let $\mathbf K\subset U^{\mathbf M}$ be $\mathbf T\langle w\rangle$-stable, and let $X_{\mathbf K\rtimes\mathbf T\langle w\rangle}$ be the recursive twisted Borel chart attached to the universal torus-normalizer family extending $\bar\rho^{\mathrm{ss}}$. Give every $U_{\boldsymbol\alpha}$ the induced universal $G_F$-action, and assume its relative Herr cohomology sheaves $H^i_{\mathrm{Herr}}(U_{\boldsymbol\alpha})$ are coherent on the chart.

Choose subsets $\boldsymbol\Psi_0,\boldsymbol\Psi_2\subset\boldsymbol\Phi^+$ such that no two absolute roots in $\bigcup_{\boldsymbol\alpha\in\boldsymbol\Psi_2}\boldsymbol\alpha$ have a nonzero difference in $\Phi_{\mathbf M}$, and put $\boldsymbol\Psi=(\boldsymbol\Psi_0,\boldsymbol\Psi_2)$. Define
\[
X_{\mathbf K\rtimes\mathbf T\langle w\rangle}\langle\boldsymbol\Psi\rangle\subset X_{\mathbf K\rtimes\mathbf T\langle w\rangle}
\]
to be the locally closed rank stratum on which, for $i\in\{0,2\}$ and every $\boldsymbol\alpha\in\boldsymbol\Phi^+$, the sheaf $H^i_{\mathrm{Herr}}(U_{\boldsymbol\alpha})$ is finite locally free of rank one if and only if $\boldsymbol\alpha\in\boldsymbol\Psi_i$.

## Expanded self-contained proof

This is a definition. Every ambient object is fixed in the Statement. For a coherent module, the finite-locally-free rank-one locus is determined by Fitting ideals and is locally closed; its complement is constructible and the simultaneous finite collection of prescribed rank-one and non-rank-one conditions gives the indicated rank stratum. The non-obstructing restriction is written directly as the absence of a nonzero Levi-root difference, avoiding the source typo that compares a subset of the root system with the singleton $\{0\}$. The orbit module is placed in the relative-height associated graded, where its root factors commute and form the Galois module used by Herr cohomology.
