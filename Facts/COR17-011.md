# Statement


Let \(K/\mathbb Q_p\) be finite, let \(\mathcal O\) be the integers in a finite extension of \(\mathbb Q_p\), and put \(A=\mathcal O\langle T^{\pm1}\rangle\). Let \(\operatorname{ur}_T:G_K\to A^\times\) be the universal unramified character sending arithmetic Frobenius to \(T\), and let \(M_T\) be the etale \((\varphi,\Gamma)\)-module attached to \(A(1)\otimes_A\operatorname{ur}_T\). On the big affine formal site over \(\operatorname{Spf}A\), the presheaf
\[
S\longmapsto\operatorname{Tor}_1^A\bigl(H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M_T),S\bigr)
\]
on \(p\)-adically complete \(A\)-algebras is represented by
\[
\operatorname{Spf}A\langle Y\rangle/(Y(T-1)).
\]
This formal scheme has exactly two irreducible components, defined by \(Y=0\) and \(T-1=0\). If \(k\) and \(E\) are the residue and fraction fields of \(\mathcal O\), its special fiber is
\[
\operatorname{Spec}k[T^{\pm1}]\ \cup\ \operatorname{Spec}k[Y],
\]
and its rigid generic fiber is
\[
\operatorname{Sp}E\langle T^{\pm1}\rangle\ \cup\ \operatorname{Sp}E\langle Y\rangle.
\]

## Expanded self-contained proof

COR17-001 gives a natural, coefficient-base-change-compatible isomorphism
\[
H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M_T)\cong A/(T-1)
\]
with exponent one. This calculation is made on the normalized rank-one component on which tensoring by the cyclotomic character leaves the universal unramified parameter \(T\); it does not assert the same quotient on every tame-character component.

Substitution into D11-003 identifies the displayed coefficient-algebra Tor presheaf with the kernel of multiplication by \(T-1\), represented by the coordinate \(Y\) subject to \(Y(T-1)=0\). The two minimal-prime, special-fiber, and rigid-generic-fiber calculations in D11-003 then give the remaining assertions.
