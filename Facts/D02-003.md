# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be a finite extension and let $R$ be a commutative $p$-nilpotent ring. Let \[\mathcal C_{\mathrm{Prism}}(K,R)=\operatorname{Vect}((\mathcal O_K)_{\mathrm{Prism}},\mathcal O_{\mathrm{Prism},R}[1/\mathcal I_{\mathrm{Prism}}])^{\varphi=1}\] be the category of Laurent $F$-crystals on the absolute prismatic site with $R$-coefficients, and let $\operatorname{Mod}^{\varphi,\Gamma}(\mathbf A_{K,R})$ be the category of etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-modules over the coefficient period ring. There is a natural exact symmetric-monoidal equivalence \[\mathcal C_{\mathrm{Prism}}(K,R)\simeq\operatorname{Mod}^{\varphi,\Gamma}(\mathbf A_{K,R}).\]
