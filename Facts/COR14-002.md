# Statement


Under the recursive chart hypotheses, each fine-locus equation is the full class-by-class BCH obstruction, while its oriented leading Jacobian block is preserved up to coordinate permutations and unit row or column scalings. Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let $\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$ be a split pinned Borel with $p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Let $K\subset U$ be $\mathbf T$-stable and normal, with roots $\Phi_K$, $K$-height $\operatorname{Ht}_K$, and $K$-simple roots $\Delta_K$. Fix a fine pair $\Psi=(\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$.

Assume, at each ordered step indexed by one root $\alpha$, that the lower recursive chart exists with universal lower logarithms $F_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}},G_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}}$ satisfying the lower cocycle equation. Put

$$P_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}}=\operatorname{Ad}(f^{\mathrm{ss}})\varphi(G_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}}),\qquad Q_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}}=\operatorname{Ad}(g^{\mathrm{ss}})\gamma(F_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}}),$$

and define the complete root obstruction

$$D_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}=\pi_\alpha\left(\operatorname{BCH}(G_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}},Q_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}})-\operatorname{BCH}(F_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}},P_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}})\right),$$

using every BCH class through $\operatorname{ht}(\alpha)$. Assume also that at a locus where the root character is cyclotomic the root-$\alpha$ obstruction module is cyclic, generated by $b_{\alpha,\mathrm{std}}$ modulo $u_\alpha=t_\alpha-1$, and that $d(c_{\alpha,\mathrm{std}})=u_\alpha b_{\alpha,\mathrm{std}}$. Let $A_\alpha$ be any lift to the lower coordinate ring of the coefficient of $[D_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}]$ relative to $[b_{\alpha,\mathrm{std}}]$, and set

$$r_\alpha=s_\alpha(t_\alpha-1)+A_\alpha.$$

Then the next Steinberg root chart is cut out by $r_\alpha=0$, while a non-Steinberg step indexed by one root is an affine bundle. Changing the lift $A_\alpha$ changes the root-indexed step hypersurface only by a triangular translation of $s_\alpha$. Consequently, when the given recursive hypotheses hold for all roots, the fine locus has the conditional presentation

$$\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb F_p[x_{\alpha,i},s_\alpha,t_\delta^{\pm1}:\alpha\in\Phi_K,\delta\in\Delta]/I^{\mathrm{BCH}},$$

where $I^{\mathrm{BCH}}$ is generated by $s_\alpha$ for $\alpha\notin\Psi_2$, by $r_\alpha$ for $\alpha\in\Psi_2$ with $\operatorname{Ht}_K(\alpha)>1$, and by $t_\alpha-1$ for $\alpha\in\Psi_0\cup\Psi_2$.

For the displayed leading-block formula below, first make this triangular translation and choose the normalized lift whose entire dependence on the height-$(\operatorname{Ht}_K(\alpha)-1)$ block is the explicit degree-two cup-product block.

Choose an orientation of each unordered decomposition $\alpha=\beta+\delta$ having at least one summand in $\Delta_K$, and let $\mathcal D_\alpha$ be the resulting set. Choose the blockwise local-duality normal forms of COR17-017. For each oriented pair let $J_{\beta,\delta}$ be the retained tensor defining the pairing on regular coordinates: it is a unit multiple of the identity on a pair of distinct blocks paired by duality and a unit multiple of
$$H_{2m}=\operatorname{antidiag}(\underbrace{1,\ldots,1}_{m},\underbrace{-1,\ldots,-1}_{m})$$
on a block paired with itself by duality; all Chevalley, output-generator, and pairing units are retained. Put
$$\langle\vec x_\beta,\vec x_\delta\rangle_{\beta,\delta}=x_{\beta,\mathrm{reg}}^{\mathsf T}J_{\beta,\delta}x_{\delta,\mathrm{reg}}+x_{\beta,d_F+1}s_\delta+x_{\delta,d_F+1}s_\beta.$$
Then

$$r_\alpha=s_\alpha(t_\alpha-1)+\sum_{(\beta,\delta)\in\mathcal D_\alpha}N_{\beta,\delta}\langle\vec x_\beta,\vec x_\delta\rangle_{\beta,\delta}+Q_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}.$$

The remainder contains every other quadratic term and every cubic or higher BCH term, including mixed terms in the two inputs. It is independent of the entire coordinate block of roots having $K$-height $\operatorname{Ht}_K(\alpha)-1$. Therefore the leading root-height Jacobian matrices used by the grouped cone and F4 rank computations are permutation/unit equivalent to the former scalar-coordinate matrices, not entrywise identical. Their ranks and witnessing minors, and hence the existing Sage artifacts, remain valid, although the full fine-locus polynomials are changed.

In the resulting fine-locus presentation one also imposes the common terminal linear equation
\[
x_{\alpha,[F:\mathbb Q_p]+[k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}:\mathbb F_p]}=0
\qquad(\alpha\notin\Psi_0),
\]
as in COR11-001. This equation is retained unchanged in the cone model.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The one-root criterion gives the complete obstruction $D_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}$. Its cohomology coefficient vanishes after adding the standard top coordinate exactly when $s_\alpha(t_\alpha-1)+A_\alpha=0$, proving the root-indexed step assertion. If the chosen lift of the coefficient is replaced by $A_\alpha+(t_\alpha-1)q$, the translation $s_\alpha\mapsto s_\alpha-q$ identifies the two hypersurfaces. Iterating gives the given conditional ideal presentation. Before writing the leading-block formula, use the same translation to normalize the lift so that no additional height-$(h-1)$ term is hidden in a multiple of $t_\alpha-1$.

The BCH contribution of bracket length two is $[X,Y]/2$. After the two ordered root decompositions are combined with both the cochain interchange sign and the Chevalley interchange sign, each unordered decomposition contributes once with coefficient $N_{\beta,\delta}$. COR17-017 identifies the resulting regular-coordinate cup tensor blockwise: paired bases give a retained unit multiple of the identity on a pair of distinct blocks paired by duality, while graded commutativity gives a retained unit multiple of the symplectic antidiagonal $H_{2m}$ on a block paired with itself by duality. This proves the given oriented tensor term without imposing an invalid global identity-form basis choice.

The degree-one cup product also contributes the two exceptional-coordinate terms $x_{\beta,d_F+1}s_\delta+x_{\delta,d_F+1}s_\beta$; these belong to the displayed pairing, not to $Q_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}$. If a monomial in a root-$\alpha$ equation contains a coordinate of $K$-height $h-1$, where $h=\operatorname{Ht}_K(\alpha)$, the remaining root weight has $K$-height one. Such a contribution is exactly one of the displayed quadratic terms, and no cubic or higher word contains that block. Therefore $Q_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}$ is independent of the entire height-$(h-1)$ block. On a block paired with itself by duality, the hyperbolic slice of COR17-017 replaces the former scalar leading matrix by coordinate permutations and unit row or column scalings. Thus the matrices need not agree entrywise, but their ranks and nonzero minors agree up to relabeling and multiplication by units.

The focused Sage tests compute the complete symbolic A2 quadratic and B2 cubic polynomials and independently regenerate the positive F4 Chevalley algebra through height 11. Their scalar-coordinate leading matrices and immutable AlgEG manifests provide the existing rank witnesses. The blockwise tensor correction carries each witness to a unit multiple of a permuted witness, so the same set of primes dividing the indicated integer, ranks, minors, JSON artifacts, and checksum sidecars remain valid without claiming entrywise equality of the corrected matrices.

The terminal coordinate is the final unramified coordinate in the modified Herr chart.  The definition of the fine stratum sets it to zero exactly when the corresponding $H^0$ does not jump; this is the common linear equation isolated in COR11-001.
