# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $R=\overline{\mathbb F}_p[t^{\pm1}]$, let $M$ be a ramified rank-one etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $\mathbf E_{F,R}$, and let $S$ be a finite-type $R$-algebra. Form a modified Herr complex by adjoining $S\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}$ to degree one and using the ramified differentials $d^0_+(f)=(0,d^0_{\mathrm{Herr}}(f))$ and $d^1_+(s,x)=d^1_{\mathrm{Herr}}(x)$. Then there is a canonical decomposition $$C^\bullet_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M,S)=C^\bullet_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M,S)\oplus(S\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}})[-1].$$ Consequently, $$H^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M,S)\cong H^1_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M,S)\oplus(S\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}),$$ while its degree-zero and degree-two cohomology agree with ordinary Herr cohomology.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

In the ramified branch of the definition, $d^0_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(f)=(0,d^0_{\mathrm{Herr}}(f))$, and $d^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(s,x)=d^1_{\mathrm{Herr}}(x)$. Thus no differential enters or leaves the new first summand in degree one. It is therefore a direct summand complex concentrated in degree one, and the cohomology formulas follow.
