# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, put $d_F=[F:\mathbb Q_p]$, and let $\mathbf G$ be a split connected reductive group over $\overline{\mathbb F}_p$ with connected center. Fix a Borel subgroup $\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$ with positive roots $\Phi^+$ and simple roots $\Delta$, assume $p>h_{\mathbf G}$, and let $\Psi=(\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$ be a fine pair. Then the full-unipotent stratum
\[
\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}[U]\langle\Psi\rangle=\dot{\mathcal X}_{\mathbf B}\langle\Psi\rangle
\]
is smooth of dimension
\[
d_F\#\Phi^+-\left(\dim\operatorname{Span}_{\mathbb Q}\Psi_2-\dim\operatorname{Span}_{\mathbb Q}(\Psi_2\cap\Delta)\right).
\]
If $\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}[U]\langle\Psi\rangle$ is its image in the Borel-valued mod-$p$ Weil--Deligne stack, then
\[
\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}[U]\langle\Psi\rangle\longrightarrow\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}[U]\langle\Psi\rangle
\]
induces a bijection on irreducible components.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Cover the full-unipotent fine open by affine charts. For every
simple root, its prescribed coordinate vector is nonzero. After
choosing the matching supplied by F03-019, choose on each chart
one nonzero coordinate for every simple-root edge used by the
matching and relabel that coordinate as index $1$. This is a
chartwise convention and does not assert that an original global
coordinate $x_{\delta,1}$ is everywhere invertible.

By COR14-002, every fine-locus equation is the complete
class-by-class BCH obstruction. COR4-002 identifies its coherently
oriented quadratic leading term. Every restored mixed term is
independent of the height-$(h-1)$ coordinate block, so F03-019
supplies the same square Jacobian minor with determinant a
nonzero monomial in the chosen simple-root units. Height-ordered
elimination successively solves one matched variable in each full
BCH equation. Thus every fiber over
$\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}[U]\langle\Psi\rangle$ is an affine space
of constant dimension, and F09-008 gives smoothness. Counting the
remaining root coordinates and independent torus relations gives
the displayed dimension. The resulting smooth surjection has
geometrically irreducible fibers and induces the asserted
bijection on irreducible components.

gives the full BCH equations; all terms beyond the oriented quadratic terms

gives a full-rank Jacobian minor which is a unit on each chosen open.

irreducible affine fibers. Hence the morphism induces a bijection of

\end{proof}
