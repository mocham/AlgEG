# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let $\mathbf G=\operatorname{PGL}_2$. There is a finite set of Hodge types $\{\underline\lambda_s:s\in S\}$ such that the special fiber of the union of crystalline stacks $\bigcup_{s\in S}\mathcal X_{\operatorname{PGL}_2}^{\underline\lambda_s,\tau_{\mathrm{triv}}}$ contains every irreducible component of $\mathcal X_{\operatorname{PGL}_2,\mathrm{red}}^{\mathrm{EG}}$, where $\tau_{\mathrm{triv}}$ is the trivial inertial type. The same assertion holds with $\operatorname{PGL}_2$ replaced by its Borel subgroup.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put \(D=[F:\mathbb Q_p]\). For the reductive \(\operatorname{PGL}_2\) clause, choose a closed point on each irreducible component away from all the other components. COR19-002 gives finite scheme-image coverage by rank-one Borel/parabolic reduction sources. The rank-one source dimensions in that coverage are at most \(D\), with the Borel source dimension supplied by the product calculation F02-017, so every ambient reductive component has dimension at most \(D\).

D04-005 gives the chosen point a regular crystalline lift with trivial inertial type: the Borel case uses the lower-right-normalized subgroup of \(B_{\operatorname{GL}_2}\), while a point in no Borel is covered by Lin22. F05-023 makes the corresponding fixed-type crystalline image closed and flat, and F01-005 gives the reduced special-fiber component through the chosen point dimension \(D\). This closed irreducible subset lies in the unique ambient component containing the seed point. The preceding ambient upper bound makes the containment equal-dimensional, hence it is the whole ambient component. This removes the former equal-dimension containment gap.

For the Borel clause, F02-017 and F02-019 identify every component and the unique connected cyclotomic crossing
\[
s(t-1)=0.
\]
This crossing has exactly the horizontal component \(V(s)\) and the Steinberg component \(V(t-1)\). Away from the cyclotomic divisor, local Tate duality gives \(H^2=0\), so reduction on \(H^1\) is surjective after choosing separated crystalline character lifts; every residual extension there has an ordinary regular crystalline lift. Choose such a point with \(t\ne1\) on every horizontal or non-Steinberg component. On each Steinberg component choose a maximally nonsplit point with \(s\ne0\); D04-005 supplies its Borel-valued lift through the integral \(B_{\operatorname{PGL}_2}\cong\{\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\}\subset B_{\operatorname{GL}_2}\) section.

For each seed lift, take the \(p\)-torsion-free schematic closure of its generic ordinary component in the flat split parabolic fixed-type stack of COR17-008. Separated root weights give \(H^0=H^2=0\), so the rank-one local Euler characteristic gives generic stack dimension \(D\), and the entire generic component is crystalline. Its flat special fiber has the same dimension. F02-017 gives dimension \(D\) for every ambient Borel component. Since the seed point lies on only the chosen branch, the special-fiber component through it is a closed equal-dimensional irreducible subset of that branch and hence equals the branch.

There are finitely many reductive and Borel components. Selecting the finitely many Hodge types produced above proves both assertions. This proof uses the exact two-branch Steinberg geometry and does not invoke the broad extension abstractions D02-006, F01-006, or F09-010.
