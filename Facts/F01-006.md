# Statement


Let $\mathcal O$ be a complete discrete valuation ring with residue field $k$, and let $X=\operatorname{Spec}R$ be reduced, flat over $\mathcal O$, and locally Noetherian. Let $C^\bullet$ be a perfect complex of $R$-modules concentrated in degrees $[0,2]$. Put $B^2=\operatorname{im}(C^1\to C^2)$ and $H^2=H^2(C^\bullet)$, and assume that $H^2$ is set-theoretically supported on the special fiber.

Assume the following jump-codimension condition: for every $r\geq0$, the locus in the special fiber where the fiber dimension of $H^2$ is at least $r$ has codimension in $X$ at least $r+1$ whenever it is nonempty. Let $x\in X(k)$ and let $\bar e\in H^1(C^\bullet\otimes_R^{\mathbb L}k(x))$. Then there are a finite extension $\mathcal O'/\mathcal O$, a morphism $f:\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O'\to X$ carrying the closed point to $x$, and a class
\[
e\in H^1(C^\bullet\otimes_R^{\mathbb L}\mathcal O')
\]
whose reduction is $\bar e$. The generic point of $f$ may be chosen in the complement of the support of $H^2$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Represent $C^\bullet$ by a three-term complex of finite projective $R$-modules. Lift $\bar e$ first to a degree-one cochain $c\in C^1$. Its coboundary $b=d(c)$ lies in the maximal-ideal fiber of $C^2$, because $\bar e$ is a cocycle at $x$.

Since $X$ is reduced, $B^2\subset C^2$ is torsion free. Blow up $X$ along an ideal supported in the special fiber so that the torsion-free quotient of the pullback of $B^2$ is locally free. Denote the blowup by $\pi:\widetilde X\to X$, the resulting locally free coboundary sheaf by $\widetilde B^2$, and the pullback of $b$ by $\widetilde b$. The closed fiber of $\widetilde b$ above $x$ is zero.

Apply the standard section lemma to the inclusion $\widetilde B^2\hookrightarrow\widetilde C^2$. If $\widetilde b$ had no zero above $x$, successively quotienting by the nowhere-zero section would produce, on the rank-$r$ stratum of the cokernel, a nonempty zero locus of a section of a rank-$r$ vector bundle whose codimension is at once at most $r$ and at least $r+1$. The jump-codimension hypothesis excludes this. Hence $\widetilde b$ vanishes at some point $\widetilde x$ above $x$, and the value of the pulled-back cochain is a degree-one cocycle there.

The blowup remains flat over $\mathcal O$. Choose an integral trait $\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O'\to\widetilde X$ through $\widetilde x$ whose generic point avoids the support of $H^2$. After one further blowup if necessary, the torsion cokernel $H^2$ is supported on an effective Cartier divisor in the special fiber. The chosen trait meets that divisor properly, so all higher derived pullbacks of $H^2$ vanish. The base-change spectral sequence
\[
L_{-p}f^*H^q(C^\bullet)\Longrightarrow
H^{p+q}(f^*C^\bullet)
\]
therefore gives degree-one base change. The cocycle at $\widetilde x$ lifts to a class in $H^1(f^*C^\bullet)$, and its reduction is the original class $\bar e$.
