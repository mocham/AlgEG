# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite with residue field $\kappa_F$, and let $(G,B,T)$ be split pinned reductive with dual pinned group $(\bG,\bB,\bT)$; assume $p>h_{\bG}$. Let $U$ be the unipotent radical of $\bB$ and fix a height-compatible total ordering on $\Phi(\bB,\bT)^+$, with least and greatest roots $\alpha_{\min}$ and $\alpha_{\max}$. For a root $\alpha$ of height $h$, let $U_{\leq\alpha}$ be the quotient of $U/U_{h+1}$ retaining root groups through $U_{\alpha^\vee}$ in the fixed order, and let $U_{<\alpha}$ be its quotient by $U_{\alpha^\vee}$. Let $\cX_{\leq\alpha}$ and $\cX_{<\alpha}$ be the partially reduced Emerton-Gee stacks for $U_{\leq\alpha}\rtimes\bT$ and $U_{<\alpha}\rtimes\bT$, and let $\cX_{\leq\alpha}^+\to\cX_{\leq\alpha}$ be the rootwise additive torsor chart. Since $\cX_{\bT}=\coprod[\bT/\bT]$, define $X_{<\alpha_{\min}}=X_{\bT}:=\coprod\bT$, with components indexed by the Serre weights of $\GL_1(\kappa_F)$. Recursively, once $X_{<\alpha}$ is defined as a destackification of $\cX_{<\alpha}$, set
\[
X_{\leq\alpha}:=X_{<\alpha}\times_{\cX_{<\alpha}}\cX_{\leq\alpha}^+.
\]
The Borel destackification is defined to be $X_{\bB}:=X_{\leq\alpha_{\max}}$.
