# Statement


Let \(F/\mathbb Q_p\) be finite, put \(d_F=[F:\mathbb Q_p]\), and let \(\mathbf G\) be the split pinned connected reductive group of type \(F_4\) with connected center in the manuscript's standing setup. Assume \(p>12\), fix \(\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T\), and use the manuscript convention that a digit string labels a positive root \(\alpha\), while the corresponding root subgroup of the dual group is \(U_{\alpha^\vee}\).

Every corrected fine cone stratum has dimension at most \(24d_F\), and the reduced Borel-valued Emerton--Gee stack is equidimensional of dimension \(24d_F\). If \(F\ne\mathbb Q_p\), its irreducible components are exactly the usual full-unipotent components indexed by \(\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal W_{\mathbf B})\).

If \(F=\mathbb Q_p\), let \(S_j\) be the complete fine stratum, including its torus Serre component and all nonvanishing conditions, associated with row \(j\in\{\mathrm I,\mathrm {II},\mathrm {III},\mathrm {IV}\}\) of D11-001. The non-full irreducible components are precisely
\[
\overline Z,
\qquad
Z\in\coprod_{j=\mathrm I}^{\mathrm {IV}}\operatorname{Irr}_{24}(S_j),
\]
where \(\operatorname{Irr}_{24}\) denotes the irreducible components of stack-adjusted dimension 24. Thus the certificate gives four exceptional configuration types, not four individual irreducible components. In each row \(\Psi_2\subseteq\Delta_K\), so every fixed-component row presentation has no higher-root obstruction equation and is smooth.

For split simple \(F_4\), row I occurs for exactly the \(p-1\) labels
\[
(a,1,0,1),\qquad a\in\mathbb Z/(p-1)\mathbb Z,
\]
and rows II--IV occur once each, with labels \((1,1,0,1)\), \((0,1,0,1)\), and \((0,1,0,0)\), respectively. Hence the exact number of exceptional Borel components is
\[
(p-1)+3=p+2.
\]
In particular, at \(p=17\) there are exactly 19. After adjoining a split central torus of rank \(z\), the exact count is multiplied by its \((p-1)^z\) tame Serre labels, giving \((p+2)(p-1)^z\), and at \(p=17\) giving \(19\cdot16^z\).

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

COR15-001, COR11-002, and D11-001 give the exhaustive cone-to-fine dimension bound. COR7-005 then gives equidimensionality and identifies irreducible components with closures of irreducible top-dimensional fine pieces. For \(F\ne\mathbb Q_p\), D11-001 leaves only the full-unipotent records.

For \(F=\mathbb Q_p\), D11-001 leaves exactly rows I--IV as non-full equality records. The torus stack is a coproduct of open-and-closed Serre components, so the rows must first be refined by those clopen components. Since \(\Psi_2\subseteq\Delta_K\), each fixed-label row presentation has no higher-root obstruction equation and is a nonempty open in its irreducible affine/Laurent-linear presentation. It is therefore smooth and irreducible, and its closure is one exceptional component. Distinct clopen Serre labels cannot have the same closure.

In row I the tame simple-root labels are \((a,1,0,1)\) for all \(a\bmod p-1\). The coordinate \(t_{1000}\) is a free unramified coordinate. For any fixed value of \(a\), every additional tame zero or one condition that would put an extra root into \(\Psi_0\) or \(\Psi_2\) cuts out one of finitely many proper divisors in the \(t_{1000}\)-line. Choosing \(t_{1000}\) away from their union keeps exactly the row-I root sets and proves that every one of the \(p-1\) labels occurs. Conversely these exhaust the tame Serre labels for the free simple-root coordinate, so row I contributes exactly \(p-1\) components.

Rows II--IV have no free tame label and contribute exactly the displayed labels \((1,1,0,1)\), \((0,1,0,1)\), and \((0,1,0,0)\). Thus the simple-group count is \((p-1)+3=p+2\), equal to 19 when \(p=17\). A split central torus of rank \(z\) contributes independently one of \(p-1\) tame Serre labels in each central coordinate, so Cartesian product of labels multiplies the count by \((p-1)^z\).
