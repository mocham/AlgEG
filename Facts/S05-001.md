# Statement

Let $(A,\mathfrak m)$ be a complete local Noetherian ring with residue field $k$, put $S=\operatorname{Spec}A$, let $G$ be a smooth affine $S$-group scheme, and let $H$ be an $S$-group scheme of multiplicative type. Every special-fiber homomorphism $f_0:H_k\to G_k$ lifts to an $S$-homomorphism $f:H\to G$. Any two lifts are conjugate by an element of $\ker(G(A)\to G(k))$. If $f_0$ is a closed immersion, every such lift is a closed immersion.

This is Conrad, Reductive Group Schemes, Corollary B.3.5, itself identified there as a special case of SGA3, Expose IX, 7.3. Its hypotheses are exactly that the base is complete local Noetherian, $G$ is smooth and affine over $S$, and $H$ is of multiplicative type. Its conclusions are the existence of a lift, conjugacy by an element reducing to the identity, and preservation of closed immersions, exactly as asserted here.
