# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be a finite extension, let $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_p$, and let $\bar\varepsilon_F:G_F\to k^\times$ be the mod-$p$ cyclotomic character. Put $R=k[t,t^{-1}]$, and let $M$ be a rank-one etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $\mathbf E_{F,R}$ whose $k$-valued fibers correspond to unramified characters $\chi_a:G_F\to k^\times$. For every such fiber,
\[
\dim_k H^1_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M_a)=[F:\mathbb Q_p]+\mathbf 1_{\chi_a=1}+\mathbf 1_{\chi_a=\bar\varepsilon_F}.
\]
Let $Z^1_{\mathrm{unr}}(G_F,\chi_a)$ be the one-dimensional space of continuous Galois one-cocycles that vanish on inertia, viewed under the Herr-to-Galois comparison as the normalized unramified cocycle line. Then
\[
Z^1_{\mathrm{unr}}(G_F,\chi_a)=B^1(G_F,\chi_a)\quad\text{if }\chi_a\ne1,
\qquad
Z^1_{\mathrm{unr}}(G_F,1)\cap B^1(G_F,1)=0.
\]
Thus the unramified line produces the $H^0$-driven jump at the trivial character, while the separate cyclotomic indicator records the possible simultaneous $H^2$ contribution.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The local Euler characteristic F01-021 gives
\[
\dim H^1(G_F,\chi_a)=[F:\mathbb Q_p]+\dim H^0(G_F,\chi_a)+\dim H^2(G_F,\chi_a).
\]
Because $\chi_a$ is one-dimensional, its invariant space has dimension $1$ exactly when $\chi_a=1$ and otherwise has dimension $0$. By local Tate duality D02-002,
\[
\dim H^2(G_F,\chi_a)=\dim H^0(G_F,\chi_a^{-1}\bar\varepsilon_F)=\mathbf 1_{\chi_a=\bar\varepsilon_F}.
\]
The Herr-to-Galois comparison gives the displayed Herr dimension formula.

For the cocycle assertion, choose an arithmetic Frobenius $\mathrm{Fr}_F$ and write $\lambda=\chi_a(\mathrm{Fr}_F)$. A cocycle vanishing on inertia is determined by its value at $\mathrm{Fr}_F$, so $Z^1_{\mathrm{unr}}(G_F,\chi_a)$ is one-dimensional. The value at Frobenius of the coboundary of $x\in k$ is $(\lambda-1)x$. If $\lambda\ne1$, this map is an isomorphism and the unramified cocycle line consists entirely of coboundaries. If $\lambda=1$, every coboundary is zero while the unramified cocycle line is nonzero. Since an unramified character is trivial exactly when $\lambda=1$, this proves the two alternatives. This basis-independent line is what the source's constant-cocycle calculation is intended to represent.
