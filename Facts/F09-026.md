# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $G$ be a reductive group over $K$, write ${}^LG$ for its L-group, and fix an inertial type $\tau$ and Hodge type $\lambda$. Under the nonabelian Shapiro equivalence $\operatorname{Sha}:\mathcal X_{K,{}^LG}\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal X_{\mathbb Q_p,{}^L\operatorname{Res}_{K/\mathbb Q_p}G}$, the fixed-type substack $\mathcal X_{K,{}^LG}^{\tau,\lambda}$ is identified with $\mathcal X_{\mathbb Q_p,{}^L\operatorname{Res}_{K/\mathbb Q_p}G}^{\operatorname{Sha}(\tau),\operatorname{Sha}(\lambda)}$.
