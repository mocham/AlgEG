* Title: Local Tate duality for algebraic mod-p coefficients

# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be a finite extension, let $k$ be an algebraic extension of a finite field of characteristic $p$, and let $V$ be a finite-dimensional continuous $k$-representation of $G_F=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline F/F)$ obtained by scalar extension from a representation over a finite subfield of $k$. Put $V^\vee(1)=\operatorname{Hom}_k(V,k)(1)$. Cup product and the invariant isomorphism $H^2(G_F,k(1))\cong k$ induce perfect pairings \[H^i(G_F,V)\times H^{2-i}(G_F,V^\vee(1))\longrightarrow k\qquad(0\leq i\leq2).\] In particular, $H^2(G_F,V)\cong H^0(G_F,V^\vee(1))^\vee$ and $\dim_k H^2(G_F,V)=\dim_k H^0(G_F,V^\vee(1))$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Choose a finite subfield $k_0\subset k$ and a $k_0$-representation $V_0$ with $V=V_0\otimes_{k_0}k$. For $M_0=\mathbb D_{k_0}(V_0)$, the cited source defines the Tate pairing on Herr complexes using cup product and $H^2(\mathcal C^\bullet(\mathbb A_{F,k_0}(1)))\cong k_0$. Its duality proposition gives a quasi-isomorphism $\mathcal C^\bullet(M_0)\simeq\operatorname{RHom}_{k_0}(\mathcal C^\bullet(M_0^*),k_0)[-2]$, and the comparison theorem identifies this with Galois cohomology compatibly with cup products and duality. Thus the asserted pairings are perfect over $k_0$. Continuous cochains commute with extension from $k_0$ to $k$: locally constant cochains form a filtered colimit of finite products, and tensoring with a field is exact. Tensoring the finite-level perfect pairings with $k$ therefore gives the displayed pairings, the invariant isomorphism, and the degree-two specialization over $k$.
