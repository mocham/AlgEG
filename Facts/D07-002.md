# Statement


Let $R$ be a principal ideal domain, let $u\in R$ be irreducible, put $k=R/(u)$, and let $P^\bullet$ be a bounded complex of finite projective $R$-modules concentrated in degrees $0,1,2$. Suppose $b\in P^2$ is a cocycle whose cohomology class generates a direct summand $R/(u^m)\subset H^2(P^\bullet)$ for some $m\geq1$, and suppose $c\in P^1$ satisfies $d(c)=u^m b$. Then $c\bmod u$ is a degree-one cocycle, and its image under the universal-coefficient edge map
\[
 H^1(P^\bullet\otimes_R k)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(H^2(P^\bullet),k)
\]
has nonzero component in $\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(R/(u^m),k)$. More precisely, under the canonical identification of that Tor group with the $u$-torsion in $R/(u^m)$, the component is $u^{m-1}[b]$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Because $d(c)=u^m b$ and $m\geq1$, reduction modulo $u$ makes $c$ a cocycle. Multiplication by $u$ is injective termwise on the finite projective complex, so
\[
0\longrightarrow P^\bullet\xrightarrow{u}P^\bullet\longrightarrow P^\bullet\otimes_R k\longrightarrow0
\]
is a short exact sequence of complexes. Its connecting homomorphism sends $[c\bmod u]$ to the class of $d(c)/u=u^{m-1}b$ in $H^2(P^\bullet)$. The class $u^{m-1}[b]$ is nonzero because $[b]$ has exact annihilator $(u^m)$ in the stated direct summand.

For a complex of projective modules over a PID, the degree-one universal-coefficient edge identifies its Tor target with $H^2(P^\bullet)[u]$, and this identification is the connecting homomorphism above. Hence the component of the edge image in $\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(R/(u^m),k)$ is exactly the nonzero element $u^{m-1}[b]$.
