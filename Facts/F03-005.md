# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, put $\dF=[F:\Q_p]$, and let $\bar\rho^{\mathrm{ss}}:\Gal_F\to\bT(\bFp)$ be a torus-valued Galois representation. Let $\Psi_0,\Psi_2\subset\Phi^+$ satisfy
\[
H^2(\Gal_F,U_{\alpha^\vee}(\bFp))\ne0\Longleftrightarrow\alpha\in\Psi_2,\qquad H^0(\Gal_F,U_{\alpha^\vee}(\bFp))\ne0\Longleftrightarrow\alpha\in\Psi_0.
\]
Let $\delta=\deg(\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$, where $\delta$ is the least positive $\sum_i n_i$ for a relation $\alpha=\sum_i n_i\alpha_i$ with $n_i\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$, $\alpha\in\Psi_0$, and $\alpha_i\in\Psi_2$, and set $\delta=+\infty$ if no such relation exists. If $\delta<+\infty$, then
\[
[F(\mu_p):F]\mid\delta
\]
and
\[
\dF\ge\frac{p-1}{\gcd(p-1,\delta)}\ge\frac{p-1}{\delta};
\]
equivalently, $\dF\delta\ge p-1$. If $\delta=+\infty$, these hypotheses impose no field-degree bound through the minimal degree.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

### Faithful original proof

```tex
Let $\bar\epsilon:\Gal_F\to\bFp^\times$ be the mod $p$ cyclotomic
character and put
\[
m=\#\bar\epsilon(\Gal_F)=[F(\mu_p):F].
\]
By local Tate duality, the character on $U_{\alpha_i^\vee}(\bFp)$ is
$\bar\epsilon$ for every $\alpha_i\in\Psi_2$, whereas the character on
$U_{\alpha^\vee}(\bFp)$ is trivial for every $\alpha\in\Psi_0$.

Suppose that
\[
\alpha=\sum_i n_i\alpha_i,
\qquad
\alpha\in\Psi_0,
\quad
\alpha_i\in\Psi_2.
\]
Evaluating $\bar\rho^{\mathrm{ss}}$ on this identity gives
\[
1=\bar\epsilon^{\sum_i n_i}.
\]
Thus $m\mid\sum_i n_i$. Taking a relation realizing the minimum gives
$m\mid\delta$. Since also $m\mid p-1$, we have
$m\leq\gcd(p-1,\delta)$.

Let $E=F\cap\mathbb Q_p(\mu_p)$. Then
$[E:\mathbb Q_p]=\frac{p-1}{m}.$
As $E\subset F$, it follows that
\[
\dF=[F:\mathbb Q_p]
\geq\frac{p-1}{m}
\geq\frac{p-1}{\gcd(p-1,\delta)}.
\]
The final inequality follows from $\gcd(p-1,\delta)\leq\delta$.
```
