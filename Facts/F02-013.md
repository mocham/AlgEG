# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite and let $B=B_{\PGL_2}\cong\G_a\rtimes\G_m$ be the Borel subgroup represented inside $\GL_2$ by matrices $\left[\begin{smallmatrix}* & *\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right]$. Let $\cX_B$ and $\cX_{\G_m}$ be the corresponding partially reduced mod-$p$ Emerton-Gee stacks. For the universal rank-one etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $\cX_{\G_m}$, let $C^0_{\Herr}\to C^1_{\Herr}\to C^2_{\Herr}$ be its Herr complex and let $Z^1_{\Herr}=\ker(C^1_{\Herr}\to C^2_{\Herr})$. Then $\cX_B\to\cX_{\G_m}$ is relatively represented by the groupoid $[Z^1_{\Herr}/C^0_{\Herr}]$, where $C^0_{\Herr}$ acts through Herr coboundaries.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

### Proof

Fix a test algebra A and an A-point M of cX_{G_m}. A B-valued etale (phi,Gamma)-module above M is an extension of the trivial rank-one module by M, because B consists of matrices with diagonal characters (chi,1) and additive root subgroup G_a. After choosing the fixed diagonal reduction, the upper-right entries of phi and of a topological generator of Gamma form a Herr 1-cochain. The commutation relation defining an etale (phi,Gamma)-module is exactly the Herr cocycle equation, so the allowable extension data are Z^1_Herr(M_A).

Changing the unipotent splitting by an upper-triangular gauge element is a Herr 0-cochain a in C^0_Herr(M_A), and it changes the extension cocycle by the coboundary d(a). Morphisms and stabilizers are therefore exactly those in the action groupoid [Z^1_Herr(M_A)/C^0_Herr(M_A)]. This construction is functorial in A and in the base point M, hence gives the asserted relative presentation over cX_{G_m}.
