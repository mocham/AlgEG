# Statement


Let $T$ be a torus over $\mathbb Q_p$ and let $\lambda\in X^*(T)$ be any character. There exists a potentially crystalline character $T(\mathbb Q_p)\to\mathcal O_{\overline{\mathbb Q}_p}^\times$ of Hodge type $\lambda$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

### Reviewed completion

Choose a finite splitting field $L/\mathbb Q_p$. The diagonal map gives $T(\mathbb Q_p)\hookrightarrow T(L)=(\operatorname{Res}_{L/\mathbb Q_p}T)(\mathbb Q_p)$, and $\lambda$ defines the algebraic evaluation character $f_0=\operatorname{ev}(\lambda)$ on $T(L)$. The algebraic-on-integral-points criterion F09-027, together with parameter descent under F09-025, shows that the restriction of $f_0$ to $T(\mathbb Q_p)$ is potentially crystalline of Hodge type $\lambda$.

The raw evaluation need not be unit-valued. Let $C\subset T(\mathbb Q_p)$ be a compact open subgroup on which $f_0$ is unit-valued. The homomorphism $v_p\circ f_0$ vanishes on $C$, so its image is a finitely generated subgroup of $\mathbb Q$ and hence is cyclic. Choose a homomorphism from that image to $\overline{\mathbb Q}_p^\times$ having the opposite valuation, and compose it with $v_p\circ f_0$. The resulting character $\eta$ is trivial on $C$, hence smooth and potentially unramified, and has Hodge type zero. Thus $f=\eta f_0$ takes values in $\mathcal O_{\overline{\mathbb Q}_p}^\times$, remains potentially crystalline, and has Hodge type $\lambda$.
