# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $\mathbf G$ be a connected reductive group of type $F_4$, assume $p>12$, let $\mathbf B$ be a Borel subgroup, and let $\Phi^+$ be the corresponding set of positive roots. Then the reduced Emerton-Gee stack $\mathcal X_{\mathbf G,\mathrm{red}}^{\mathrm{EG}}$ is equidimensional of dimension $[F:\mathbb Q_p]\#\Phi^+$, and its irreducible components are identified with the irreducible components of the mod-$p$ Borel-valued Weil-Deligne stack $\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put \(D=[F:\mathbb Q_p]\#\Phi^+\). COR19-002 applies in the corrected \(F_4\) ranges and proves that the reduced reductive stack is equidimensional of dimension \(D\), with every irreducible component the closure of a full maximally nonsplit Borel image indexed by \(\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal W_{\mathbf B})\). This already gives the asserted component comparison when \(F\ne\mathbb Q_p\).

Suppose \(F=\mathbb Q_p\). COR10-001 gives the same exhaustive usual-component classification and identifies every possible exceptional image as the image of a Serre-indexed realization of one of the four row types in COR18-001. For split simple \(F_4\), COR18-001 gives exactly \(p+2\) exceptional Borel components, equal to 19 at \(p=17\); a split central torus of rank \(z\) multiplies that Borel count by \((p-1)^z\). COR8-002 rotates every one of these Serre-indexed realizations componentwise into a usual full maximally nonsplit locus without changing its reductive image. Hence each exceptional image lies in a usual component closure and none creates an irreducible reductive component.

Thus in every stated \(F_4\) case the reductive stack is equidimensional of dimension \(D\), and its irreducible components are exactly those indexed by \(\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal W_{\mathbf B})\), as claimed.
