# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $A$ be an $\mathbb F_p$-algebra,
and let $\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$ be a split pinned Borel with
$p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Let $K=U_{\Phi_K}$ be $\mathbf T$-stable and
normal, choose a root order compatible with $K$-height, and fix
$\alpha\in\Phi_K$. Suppose a lower pair with logarithms
$F_{<\alpha},G_{<\alpha}$ and torus parts $t_f,t_g$ is a cocycle
modulo $U_{\alpha^\vee}$. Put

$$P_{<\alpha}=\operatorname{Ad}(t_f)\varphi(G_{<\alpha}),\qquad
Q_{<\alpha}=\operatorname{Ad}(t_g)\gamma(F_{<\alpha}),$$

$$D_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}=\pi_\alpha\left(
\operatorname{BCH}(G_{<\alpha},Q_{<\alpha})-
\operatorname{BCH}(F_{<\alpha},P_{<\alpha})\right).$$

The lower pair extends across $U_{\alpha^\vee}$ if and only if
$[D_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}]=0$ in
$H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(U_{\alpha^\vee}(\mathbf E_{F,A}))$.

More explicitly, write
$F_{<\alpha}=\sum f_\rho e_\rho$,
$G_{<\alpha}=\sum g_\rho e_\rho$,
$P_{<\alpha}=\sum p_\rho e_\rho$, and
$Q_{<\alpha}=\sum q_\rho e_\rho$. After choosing one orientation
$(\beta,\delta)$ of every unordered decomposition
$\alpha=\beta+\delta$, the bracket-length-two part is

$$D_{\alpha,2}^{\mathrm{BCH}}=
\frac12\sum_{\alpha=\beta+\delta}N_{\beta,\delta}
\left(g_\beta q_\delta-g_\delta q_\beta
-f_\beta p_\delta+f_\delta p_\beta\right).$$

All bracket-length-three and higher terms are the homogeneous BCH
classes from the Bernoulli recursion. In particular, neither
quadratic ordering may be discarded without a separate
cochain-homotopy argument.

## Expanded self-contained proof

COR14-001 identifies the exact top-root equation. Since
$U_{\alpha^\vee}$ is central in the quotient through $\alpha$, the
unknown top coordinates enter through the root-valued Herr
differential. Such coordinates exist exactly when the lower BCH
projection is a coboundary, which proves the cohomological
criterion.

The homogeneous class $Z_2(X,Y)=[X,Y]/2$ gives
$([G_{<\alpha},Q_{<\alpha}]-
[F_{<\alpha},P_{<\alpha}])/2$. Pairing the terms indexed by
$(\beta,\delta)$ and $(\delta,\beta)$ and using
$N_{\delta,\beta}=-N_{\beta,\delta}$ gives the displayed formula;
it retains both ordered cochain representatives. The classes
$Z_n$ for $n\geq3$ then follow from the complete Bernoulli
recursion in COR14-001, including both cubic mixed terms and every
higher mixed word.
