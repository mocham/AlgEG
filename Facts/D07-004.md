# Statement


Let $X$ be a noetherian algebraic stack with a dimension function, let $D$ be an integer, and suppose every irreducible component of $X$ has dimension at least $D$. Assume $X$ has a finite stratification $X=\coprod_{i=1}^r S_i$ by irreducible locally closed substacks satisfying $\dim S_i\leq D$. Then every irreducible component of $X$ has dimension $D$, the closure of each $D$-dimensional stratum is an irreducible component of $X$, and every irreducible component of $X$ is the closure of a $D$-dimensional stratum.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let $Z$ be an irreducible component of $X$. The finitely many locally closed subsets $Z\cap S_i$ cover $Z$, so the dimension of $Z$ is the maximum of the dimensions of their closures in $Z$. Each has dimension at most $\dim S_i\leq D$, hence $\dim Z\leq D$. The assumed lower bound gives $\dim Z=D$.

If $S_i$ has dimension $D$, its closure is irreducible and lies in some irreducible component $Z$. Both have dimension $D$. A proper irreducible closed substack of the noetherian irreducible stack $Z$ has strictly smaller dimension, so $\overline{S_i}=Z$. Conversely, the generic point of an irreducible component $Z$ lies in one of the finitely many strata, say $S_j$. Then $S_j\cap Z$ is dense in $Z$, so $\dim S_j=D$ and the preceding argument gives $\overline{S_j}=Z$.
