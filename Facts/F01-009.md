# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be a finite extension, let $\bG$ be a connected reductive group, let $\bB$ be a Borel subgroup, and let $\bT$ be its torus quotient. Write $\cX_{\bG}^{\EG}$ for the $\bG$-valued Emerton--Gee stack. Define
\[
\cX_{\bT}:=\cX_{\bT,\red,\bFp}^{\EG}
\]
to be the reduced torus-valued Emerton--Gee stack, and define
\[
\cX_{\bB}:=\cX_{\bB,\bFp}^{\EG}\underset{\cX_{\bT,\bFp}^{\EG}}{\times}\cX_{\bT}
\]
to be the partially reduced Borel-valued Emerton--Gee stack.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

This is notation, so no proof is required. The fiber-product formula has the expected moduli interpretation: an S-point of X_B is a B-valued mod-p Emerton--Gee object together with a factorization of its induced T-valued object through the reduced torus stack X_T. Thus only the torus base is reduced; the Borel stack is not being replaced by its full reduction. The two maps in the fiber product are the map induced by B -> T and the canonical closed immersion X_T -> X_{T,Fp}^{EG}.
