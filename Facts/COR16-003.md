# Statement


Let $\mathcal O$ be a complete discrete valuation ring with uniformizer $\varpi$, residue characteristic $p$, and fraction field $E$. Let $R$ be a $\varpi$-adically complete and separated $\mathcal O$-algebra topologically of finite type, put $X=\operatorname{Spf}R$, and let $\Gamma$ be a finite group with $|\Gamma|\in\mathcal O^\times$ acting continuously and $\mathcal O$-linearly on $R$. Let $I$ be a finite $\Gamma$-set. For every $i\in I$, let $L_i$ be a rank-one finite projective $R$-module and let $f_i\in R$. Assume that $f_i$ is a nonzerodivisor in $R$, that its image $\bar f_i$ is a nonzerodivisor in $\bar R=R/\varpi R$, and that there are semilinear isomorphisms $\gamma^*L_i\simeq L_{\gamma i}$ satisfying the cocycle condition and carrying multiplication by $f_i$ to multiplication by $f_{\gamma i}$. Put $M_i=L_i/f_iL_i$. Every $M_i$ is finitely presented and has projective dimension at most one over $R$.

For a $\varpi$-adically complete $R$-algebra $S$, define $\mathcal F(S)=\prod_{i\in I}\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(M_i,S)$. If $\widehat{\mathbf V}_R(L_i)$ denotes the $\varpi$-adic completion of the vector group $\operatorname{Spec}\operatorname{Sym}_R(L_i^\vee)$, then $\mathcal F$ is represented by the affine formal scheme
\[
\mathfrak K=\prod_{i\in I, X}\ker\bigl([f_i]:\widehat{\mathbf V}_R(L_i)\longrightarrow\widehat{\mathbf V}_R(L_i)\bigr).
\]
The formal scheme $\mathfrak K$ is topologically of finite presentation over $X$, carries the induced $\Gamma$-action, and the quotient $[\mathfrak K/\Gamma]\to[X/\Gamma]$ is the effective equivariant descent of $\mathfrak K\to X$. The atlas $X\to[X/\Gamma]$ is finite etale of degree $|\Gamma|$, and the rigid generic fiber of the quotient is canonically $[\mathfrak K_E/\Gamma]$. Finally, if $\bar M_i=M_i\otimes_R\bar R$, then the special fiber $\mathfrak K_{\bar R}$ represents $S_0\mapsto\prod_{i\in I}\operatorname{Tor}_1^{\bar R}(\bar M_i,S_0)$ on $\bar R$-algebras, and the special fiber of $[\mathfrak K/\Gamma]$ is $[\mathfrak K_{\bar R}/\Gamma]$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Because $L_i$ is locally free and $f_i$ is a nonzerodivisor, multiplication by $f_i$ is injective on $L_i$. Thus
\[
0\longrightarrow L_i\xrightarrow{f_i}L_i\longrightarrow M_i\longrightarrow0
\]
is a length-one finite-projective resolution. Tensoring it with a complete $R$-algebra $S$ gives the natural identification
\[
\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(M_i,S)=\ker(f_i:L_i\otimes_RS\longrightarrow L_i\otimes_RS).
\]
Finite projectivity makes the ordinary and completed tensor products agree here.

The completed vector group $\widehat{\mathbf V}_R(L_i)$ represents $S\mapsto L_i\otimes_RS$ on the big affine $\varpi$-adic formal site. Its fiber product with the zero section along multiplication by $f_i$ therefore represents the displayed kernel. Taking the finite fiber product over $X$ represents the product over $I$. Completed symmetric algebras of finite projective modules are topologically of finite presentation, and imposing the finite set of linear kernel equations preserves that property.

The cocycle-compatible semilinear maps carry the resolution indexed by $i$ to the resolution indexed by $\gamma i$. They consequently act on the representing kernels and permute their factors, giving a $\Gamma$-action on $\mathfrak K$. The quotient groupoid $\mathfrak K\times\Gamma\rightrightarrows\mathfrak K$ defines $[\mathfrak K/\Gamma]$, and its pullback along $X\to[X/\Gamma]$ is $\mathfrak K$ with the given descent datum. This is effective fpqc descent as a formal stack. The same quotient groupoid shows that the atlas is a finite etale $\Gamma$-torsor of degree $|\Gamma|$, which is prime to $p$, and applying rigid generic fiber termwise gives $[\mathfrak K_E/\Gamma]$.

Reduction of $L_i$ is finite projective over $\bar R$. The nonzerodivisor hypothesis on $\bar f_i$ makes the reduced sequence
\[
0\longrightarrow\bar L_i\xrightarrow{\bar f_i}\bar L_i\longrightarrow\bar M_i\longrightarrow0
\]
exact. Repeating the Tor-kernel argument over $\bar R$ proves the special-fiber representability. Fiber products and the finite quotient groupoid commute with this base change, yielding $\mathfrak K_{\bar R}$ and $[\mathfrak K_{\bar R}/\Gamma]$ as asserted.

## Post-proof remarks

Verified. Every assertion follows from the explicit length-one line-bundle resolutions, the universal property of completed vector groups, and termwise base change of the finite quotient groupoid. The lemma deliberately does not assert that a universal Herr $H^2$ module has the form $L/fL$, that a proposed destackification carries the required finite equivariant atlas, or that a reduced Tor functor is a particular mod-$p$ Weil-Deligne stack; those are application hypotheses, not conclusions of this formal-algebra lemma.
