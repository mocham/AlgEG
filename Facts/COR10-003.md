# Statement


Let $l$ be a prime, let $\mathcal O$ be the complete ring of integers of a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_l$, and let $A=\mathcal O[[X_1,\ldots,X_n]]/I$. Let $x$ and $x'$ be classical points of the rigid generic fiber $(\operatorname{Spf}A)^{\mathrm{rig}}$. If $x$ and $x'$ lie on one irreducible component of $(\operatorname{Spf}A)^{\mathrm{rig}}$, then their corresponding closed points in $\operatorname{Spec}A[1/l]$ are contained in one irreducible component of $\operatorname{Spec}A[1/l]$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Removing nilpotents and $l$-power torsion does not change either generic fiber, so replace $A$ by its reduced $l$-torsion-free quotient. The complete power-series quotient is excellent, hence its normalization $A\to\widetilde A$ is finite. By S04-001, classical rigid points correspond bijectively to closed points of the algebraic generic fiber with unchanged residue fields, the map
\[
(\operatorname{Spf}\widetilde A)^{\mathrm{rig}}\longrightarrow(\operatorname{Spf}A)^{\mathrm{rig}}
\]
is the rigid normalization, and $x,x'$ have lifts $\widetilde x,\widetilde x'$ on one connected component of the normal rigid space.

Suppose the corresponding points of $\operatorname{Spec}\widetilde A[1/l]$ were in different connected components. An idempotent of $\widetilde A[1/l]$ would separate them. Pullback gives an idempotent global analytic function separating $\widetilde x$ and $\widetilde x'$, contradicting that they lie on one connected rigid component. Thus both algebraic lifts lie on one connected component of $\operatorname{Spec}\widetilde A[1/l]$. A connected component of a normal noetherian scheme is irreducible, and its image under the finite map to $\operatorname{Spec}A[1/l]$ is contained in one irreducible component. The images of $x$ and $x'$ therefore lie on that component.
