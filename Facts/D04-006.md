# Statement


Let $F_q/F$ be an unramified extension of degree $q$ prime to $p$, let $R$ be the coefficient ring of a universal unramified-twist family, and let $M_q$ be the corresponding rank-one etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $F_q$. Let $U_{\Balpha}$ be its relative-root module over $F$, obtained by unramified induction along an orbit $\Balpha$ of cardinality $q$. Put $d_F=[F:\mathbb Q_p]$ and $d_{k_F}=[k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}:\mathbb F_p]$. Then the finite-free part of $H^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(U_{\Balpha})$ has $R$-rank
\[q(d_F+d_{k_F}).\]

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Unramified Shapiro descent identifies the modified Herr cohomology of the induced relative-root module over $F$ with the modified Herr cohomology of $M_q$ over $F_q$. The degree and residue-field terms scale by $q$: $[F_q:\mathbb Q_p]=q d_F$ and $[k_{F_{q,\mathrm{cyc}}}:\mathbb F_p]=q d_{k_F}$. The rank-one modified-Herr formula over $F_q$ therefore gives rank $q(d_F+d_{k_F})$. Equivalently, in a common unramified splitting extension the regular Galois-module decomposition of D02-004 and descent mechanism of F04-011 yield $q$ absolute-root coordinate blocks. The source uses this factor in F04-020 but its displayed twisted gauge labels do not spell out the $q$ blocks.
