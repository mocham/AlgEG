# Statement


Let $S'/S$ be a finite unramified Galois splitting cover with group $\Gamma$, acting by compatible permutations of the absolute roots, root cochains, obstruction equations, and coordinate variables of a height-filtered polynomial presentation. On a simple-root nonvanishing chart, form the square absolute Jacobian block $M_{\mathrm{abs}}$ by carrying out the source-leaf pivots and terminal-core maximal minors orbitwise, with rows the obstruction roots and columns the matched penultimate-height variables. Set $\Delta_{\mathrm{abs}}:=\det M_{\mathrm{abs}}$. Suppose this determinant is the unit monomial
\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{abs}}=u\prod_{\delta,i}x_{\delta,i}^{e_{\delta,i}},\qquad u\in\mathcal O_{S'}^\times,
\]
where $\Gamma$ permutes the coordinate factors and their exponents. The determinant $\Delta_{\mathrm{abs}}$, viewed in the determinant line of the Jacobian map, is $\Gamma$-invariant: simultaneous row and matched-column permutations contribute cancelling signs, and compatible root-vector or cochain rescalings contribute only the cancelling determinant-line transition unit. Equivalently, in any scalar trivialization its orbit norm is an invariant unit monomial. Hence the determinant descends and is invertible on every relative nonvanishing chart descended from such an orbit chart. If the presentation and its higher-height remainders are $\Gamma$-equivariant and triangular for the absolute-root height filtration, the height-ordered elimination defined by $M_{\mathrm{abs}}^{-1}$ also descends.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

For an absolute obstruction root $\alpha$ at height $h+1$, the derivative of its leading equation in a matched height-$h$ variable has the form $N_{\beta,\delta}x_{\delta,i_\delta}$ with $\alpha=\beta+\delta$ and $\delta$ simple. The source-leaf contractions use these entries as pivots. On every terminal core, the maximal minor supplied by the absolute root-poset calculation is likewise a nonzero coefficient times a monomial in the selected simple-root coordinates. Multiplying the pivot and core blocks over all heights gives the displayed $\Delta_{\mathrm{abs}}$; the complete BCH remainder does not involve the matched penultimate-height block, so it does not alter this determinant.

Let $\gamma\in\Gamma$. Equivariance writes the transformed Jacobian block as
\[
\gamma(M_{\mathrm{abs}})=D_TP_T M_{\mathrm{abs}}P_V^{-1}D_V^{-1},
\]
where $P_T,P_V$ permute the obstruction rows and matched variable columns and $D_T,D_V$ record units from compatible root-vector and cochain bases. Order every matched row-column pair together. The orbitwise construction then gives $\operatorname{sgn}(P_T)=\operatorname{sgn}(P_V)$, so the permutation signs cancel. The Chevalley coefficient in each pivot and the corresponding coordinate transform by the inverse basis factors, exactly cancelling $\det(D_T)\det(D_V)^{-1}$ in the determinant-line trivialization. Thus the determinant section is fixed. With unrelated orderings or basis trivializations the same calculation multiplies its scalar representative by the associated transition unit; the product of its distinct Galois translates is an exactly invariant monomial with unit coefficient and represents the same nowhere-vanishing descended section.

A relative simple-root class is nonzero precisely on the union of charts obtained by choosing a nonzero cochain coordinate and all of its Galois translates. On each such orbit chart the selected coordinate factors in $\Delta_{\mathrm{abs}}$ are units, so the invariant determinant descends as a unit. Its adjugate divided by this unit gives a $\Gamma$-equivariant inverse coefficient map. Solving the matched block and proceeding by height therefore commutes with Galois descent. This applies to the corrected twisted presentation because COR14-003 makes every BCH class and recursive remainder equivariant, while COR10-005 supplies the absolute unit-monomial elimination.
