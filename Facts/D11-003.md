# Statement


Let $\mathcal O$ be the complete ring of integers of a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_p$, with residue field $k$ and fraction field $E$, and put $A=\mathcal O\langle T^{\pm1}\rangle$. On the big affine formal site over $\operatorname{Spf}A$, the presheaf on $p$-adically complete $A$-algebras
\[
S\longmapsto\operatorname{Tor}_1^A(A/(T-1),S)
\]
is represented by
\[
\operatorname{Spf}A\langle Y\rangle/(Y(T-1)).
\]
This formal scheme has exactly two irreducible components, defined by $Y=0$ and $T-1=0$. Its reduced special fiber is the union of $\operatorname{Spec}k[T^{\pm1}]$ and $\operatorname{Spec}k[Y]$, and its rigid generic fiber is the union of $\operatorname{Sp}E\langle T^{\pm1}\rangle$ and $\operatorname{Sp}E\langle Y\rangle$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The element $T-1$ is a nonzerodivisor in $A$. The free resolution $0\to A\xrightarrow{T-1}A\to A/(T-1)\to0$ gives
\[
\operatorname{Tor}_1^A(A/(T-1),S)=\{s\in S:(T-1)s=0\}.
\]
A continuous $A$-algebra map from $A\langle Y\rangle/(Y(T-1))$ to $S$ is equivalently a choice of such an element as the image of $Y$, proving representability exactly as in F05-001.

The ambient ring $A\langle Y\rangle$ is a domain, and $Y$ and $T-1$ are nonassociate prime elements. Hence the quotient by their product has exactly the minimal primes $(Y)$ and $(T-1)$. The corresponding quotients are $A$ and $\mathcal O\langle Y\rangle$. Reduction modulo the uniformizer and passage to the rigid generic fiber commute with these two closed components, giving the two stated axes. This result is conditional only in the sense that an application must first identify its coefficient-module cohomology with $A/(T-1)$; it makes no such Herr-cohomology assertion.
