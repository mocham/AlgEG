# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, set $d_F=[F:\Q_p]$, let $F_{\cyc}=F(\mu_{p^\infty})^{\Delta_F}$ with residue field $k_{F_{\cyc}}$, and set $d_{k_F}=[k_{F_{\cyc}}:\F_p]$. Let $B=B_{\PGL_2}$, let $\cX_B$ be the partially reduced Borel-valued Emerton-Gee stack with destackification $X_B$, and let $\cW_B$ be its mod-$p$ Weil-Deligne stack. Then $\cW_B$ is equidimensional of dimension $0$, and there is a canonical morphism $\cX_B\to\cW_B$. For every irreducible component $C\subset\cW_B$, the morphism
\[
C\times_{\cW_B}X_B\longrightarrow C
\]
is a genuine $\G_a^{d_F+d_{k_F}}$-torsor.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

By F02-014, $\mathcal W_B$ is the relative first-Tor space of the universal rank-one Herr $H^2$. The component descriptions in F02-015, the simple cyclotomic torsion D04-002, and the corrected axis calculation COR7-002 make its destackification equidimensional of dimension one; quotienting by the one-dimensional torus gives $\dim\mathcal W_B=0$. The universal-coefficient edge F01-018 and the cocycle presentation F02-013 define the canonical morphism $\mathcal X_B\to\mathcal W_B$.

Fix an irreducible component $C$. The modified complex F01-023 and its corrected free-kernel rank in COR1-001 identify $X_B(C)\to C$ as a surjective pseudo-$\mathbb G_a^{d_F+d_{k_F}}$-torsor. On a non-Steinberg component, the zero extension gives a global section, so the pseudo-torsor is genuine.

For the Steinberg component, use the minimal standard cochain $c_{\mathrm{std}}$ from F02-018 in place of the source's unexplained choice of a tres-ramifie cocycle. Write $u=t-1$ and let $m$ be its minimal pole order. D07-002 identifies the universal-coefficient edge of $[c_{\mathrm{std}}\bmod u]$ with the nonzero terminal $u$-torsion class. Therefore scaling $c_{\mathrm{std}}$ by the Steinberg coordinate induces a nonzero additive group homomorphism $\mathbb G_a\to\mathbb G_a$ on the Steinberg axis. By D06-002 this homomorphism is finite faithfully flat, and after that cover the scaled cocycle is a section of the pseudo-torsor. Hence $X_B(C)\to C$ is a genuine $\mathbb G_a^{d_F+d_{k_F}}$-torsor also on the Steinberg component.

## Faithful GrobV14 proof

```tex
\begin{proof}
Note that $\cX_{\G_m}$
is a disjoint union of
$[\G_m/\G_m]$
where $\G_m$ acts trivially on $\G_m$, indexed by Serre weights for $\GL_1$.
Irreducible components
of $X_{\G_m}$
are isomorphic to $\G_m$
and the universal family over each $\G_m$
is the family of unramified twists of a Galois character
$\Gal_F\to \bFp^\times$.

As a consequence, $W_B$ is equidimensional of dimension $1$ by
Lemma \ref{fact:COR7-002}.
So, $\cW_B=[W_B/\G_m]$ is equidimensional of dimension $0$.
The morphism $X_B\to W_B$
is defined and shown to be surjective
by Lemma \ref{fact:F01-018},
and clearly descends to $\cX_B\to \cW_B$.

Let $C\subset \cW_B$ be an irreducible component.
By Lemma \ref{fact:COR1-001} and Lemma \ref{fact:F01-018},
$X_B(C)\to C$
is surjective and is a pseudo $\G_a^{\dF+\dkF}$-torsor:
we can define a group action
$\G_a^{\dF+\dkF}\times_C X_B(C)
\cong X_B(C)\times_C X_B(C)$.
\EDITXIV{The construction uses the $\Gamma$-invariant generator of
Lemma~\ref{fact:COR6-002} and the quotient-torsor argument of
Lemma~\ref{fact:F02-002}.}
In general, a pseudo $\G_a$-torsor does not have to be a genuine
$\G_a$-torsor.
If we can construct a section
$C\to X_B(C)$ fpqc locally, then
$X_B(C)\to C$
is a genuine $\G_a^{\dF+\dkF}$-torsor.
If $C\subset \cW_B$ is not a Steinberg component,
then there exists the trivial section
$C\to X_B(C)$ that corresponds to the zero extension classes
$
\begin{bmatrix}
* & 0 \\ & *
\end{bmatrix}
$.

It remains to consider the Steinberg component 
$[\Spec \bFp[s]/\G_m]\cong C_{\st}\subset \cW_B$.
We complete the proof by explicitly constructing
a local section in flat topology.
$C_{\st}$ maps to the cyclotomic character point 
in $\cX_{\G_m}$;
write $M_{\cyc}$ for the \'etale $(\varphi, \Gamma)$-module
that corresponds to the mod $p$ cyclotomic Galois character.
\EDITXIV{Let $(c_f,c_g)$ be the minimal standard cocycle of
Lemma~\ref{fact:F02-018}.  Its image under the universal coefficient
edge map to the $\Tor_1$ line is nonzero.}
So, we do get a section
$\Spec \bFp[s] \to X_B(C_{\st})$
corresponding to the $1$-cocycle
$(s~c_f, s~c_g) \in Z^1(M_{\cyc}\otimes_{\bFp}\bFp[s], \bFp[s])$.
We don't know if
the composition
$[\Spec \bar\F_p[s]/\G_m] \to
X_B(C_{\st})
\to C_{\st}
\cong [\Spec \bar\F_p[s]/\G_m]$
is an isomorphism.
\EDITXIV{It therefore induces a nonzero group scheme homomorphism}
$\Spec \bar\F_p[s]\to \Spec \bar\F_p[s]$.
Group scheme homomorphisms
$\Spec \bar\F_p[s]\to \Spec \bar\F_p[s]$
are classified by polynomials
$s\mapsto \sum_{i=0}^N a_i s^{p^i}$
for $a_0,\dots, a_N\in \bFp$,
and every nonzero such homomorphism is finite and flat.
Thus $X_B(C_{\st})\to C_{\st}$ is indeed an fppf $\G_a^{\dF+\dkF}$
-torsor, and we are done.
\end{proof}
```
