# Statement


Let O be the complete ring of integers of a finite extension of Q_l, let A=O[[X_1,...,X_n]]/I, and replace A by its reduced l-torsion-free quotient. Let A_tilde be its finite normalization. Then closed points of (Spf A)^rig map injectively to closed points of Spec A[1/l] with unchanged residue fields, and (Spf A_tilde)^rig -> (Spf A)^rig is the normalization of the rigid space. In particular, two points on one irreducible component of (Spf A)^rig admit lifts on one connected component of (Spf A_tilde)^rig.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let $\pi$ be a uniformizer of $\mathcal O$. Since $l=u\pi^e$ for a unit $u\in\mathcal O^\times$, the reduced $l$-torsion-free quotient is $\pi$-torsion-free and $A[1/l]=A[1/\pi]$. Thus S04-001 applies to $A$. It gives a functorial bijection between points of $(\operatorname{Spf}A)^{\mathrm{rig}}$ and closed points of $\operatorname{Spec}A[1/l]$, with unchanged residue fields, which in particular gives the stated injection. It also identifies $(\operatorname{Spf}\widetilde A)^{\mathrm{rig}}\to(\operatorname{Spf}A)^{\mathrm{rig}}$ as the normalization and identifies every rigid irreducible component as the image of one connected component of the normalized space. Therefore two points on one irreducible component admit lifts on that connected component.
