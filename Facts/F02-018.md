# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, let $R=\bFp[t^{\pm1}]$, and let $U_{\gamma^\vee}(\bfE_{F,R})$ be the universal rank-one root-valued etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module whose specialization at $t=1$ is $\bFp(1)$. Choose
\[
b_{\std}\in C^2_{\Herr}(U_{\gamma^\vee}(\bfE_{F,R/(t-1)}))
\]
with $[b_{\std}]_{t=1}=1$ under $H^2(\Gal_F,\bFp(1))\cong\bFp$, and view it over $R$. Suppose
\[
b_{\std}=\frac{1}{(t-1)^{n_0}}d^1_{\Herr}(c_{\std})
\]
for $c_{\std}\in C^1_{\Herr}(U_{\gamma^\vee}(\bfE_{F,R}))$, with $n_0$ the smallest possible integer. Then $[c_{\std}]_{t=1}\neq0$ in $H^1(\Gal_F,\bFp(1))$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

### Faithful original proof

```tex
\begin{proof}
If $[c_{\std}]_{t=1}=0$,
then $c_{\std}$ is a $1$-coboundary at $t=1$.
After conjugation by an element of
$U(\bFp)$,
we can assume $c_{\std}$ vanishes (as a cochain) at $t=1$.
Since
$\bfE_{F, \bFp[t^{\pm1}]}/(t-1)
=\bfE_{F, \bFp[t^{\pm1}]/(t-1)}$,
we conclude that
$c_{\std}=(t-1)c'$
for some $c'\in C^1_{\Herr}$,
contradicting the minimality of $n_0$.
\end{proof}
```

### Reviewer completion

The pole order $n_0$ is positive. Indeed, a negative value is impossible after reduction modulo $t-1$, while $n_0=0$ would make the chosen nonzero class $b_{\std}$ a coboundary at the cyclotomic point. Hence reducing $d^1(c_{\std})=(t-1)^{n_0}b_{\std}$ shows that the specialization of $c_{\std}$ is a one-cocycle. By D06-001 its class in Galois cohomology is its Herr-cohomology class. If that class vanished, choose a degree-zero cochain at the special fiber whose Herr coboundary equals the specialization, lift it to the coefficient ring, and subtract its coboundary from $c_{\std}$. This leaves $d^1(c_{\std})$ unchanged because $d^1d^0=0$ and makes the resulting degree-one cochain specialize to zero. The base-change identity in the source then gives $c_{\std}=(t-1)c'$ in the degree-one Herr module. Coefficient-linearity of the Herr differential yields

$$b_{\std}=\frac{1}{(t-1)^{n_0-1}}d^1_{\Herr}(c'),$$

contradicting minimality of $n_0$. Thus the specialized class is nonzero.
