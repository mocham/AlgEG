# Statement


Let k be an algebraically closed field of characteristic p. Every homomorphism of k-group schemes f:G_a -> G_a is defined by an additive polynomial f(T)=sum_{i=0}^r a_i T^(p^i). If f is nonzero, then f is finite, faithfully flat, and surjective. Moreover, let H be an affine group scheme, let q:X -> G_a be an H-pseudo-torsor in the sense that H x X -> X x_(G_a) X is an isomorphism, and suppose there is a morphism ell:G_a -> X with q ell=f for a nonzero group homomorphism f. Then q becomes a genuine H-torsor after the finite faithfully flat cover f:G_a -> G_a.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The primitive elements of the Hopf algebra k[T] are exactly the additive polynomials, so a group-scheme homomorphism has the displayed form. If a_r is the last nonzero coefficient, the coordinate map k[Y] -> k[T], Y |-> f(T), presents k[T] as k[Y,T]/(f(T)-Y). After dividing by a_r, the relation is monic of degree p^r in T, so k[T] is finite free of rank p^r over k[Y]. The map is therefore finite and flat; its nonempty fibers, equivalently faithful finite freeness, make it faithfully flat and hence surjective. For the torsor assertion, ell is a section of the pullback X x_(G_a),f G_a -> G_a. The pseudo-torsor identity then identifies that pullback with H x G_a, so q is an H-torsor after the stated fppf cover.

## Post-proof remarks

No internal issues. A Hopf-algebra map for G_a is determined by a primitive polynomial, and the primitive polynomials in k[T] are exactly finite sums of a_i T^(p^i). If a_r is the highest nonzero coefficient, k[T] is k[Y,T]/(f(T)-Y), free of rank p^r over k[Y] because the relation is monic after scaling; this also covers r=0. Hence every nonzero additive map is finite faithfully flat and surjective. The given lift ell is a section after pullback along f, and the pseudo-torsor identity then identifies that pullback with H times G_a, proving fppf local triviality. The lemma is self-contained, has no dependencies, and correctly remains conditional on nonvanishing rather than claiming the missing nonvanishing in parent F02-016.
