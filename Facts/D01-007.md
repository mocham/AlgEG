# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $G/K$ be reductive, and let $A^{\circ}$ be a finite flat $\overline{\mathbb Z}_p$-algebra. Under the nonabelian Shapiro equivalence for $A^{\circ}$-valued L-parameters, a parameter for ${}^LG$ becomes potentially semistable of Hodge type $\lambda$ and inertial type $\tau$ after inverting $p$ if and only if its Shapiro image for ${}^L\operatorname{Res}_{K/\mathbb Q_p}G$ becomes potentially semistable of the transported Hodge type $\operatorname{Sha}(\lambda)$ and inertial type $\operatorname{Sha}(\tau)$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

By F09-025, nonabelian Shapiro identifies a parameter over $G_K$ with the induced parameter over $G_{\mathbb Q_p}$ valued in the L-group of $\operatorname{Res}_{K/\mathbb Q_p}G$. After choosing coset representatives, the induced dual group is a product of conjugate copies of the original dual group, indexed by the embeddings of $K$ into $\overline{\mathbb Q}_p$. After restriction to a finite extension over which the original parameter is semistable, and enlarging it to contain all conjugates, the Shapiro parameter is therefore the product of the corresponding conjugate semistable parameters. Fontaine's semistable period functor commutes with finite products, scalar extension, and conjugation, so potential semistability is equivalent in both directions. Restriction to inertia gives exactly the induced inertial datum $\operatorname{Sha}(\tau)$, while the product decomposition of de Rham periods carries the embedding-indexed filtration to the transported cocharacter $\operatorname{Sha}(\lambda)$. These constructions commute with extension of the finite flat coefficient algebra $A^{\circ}$, proving the asserted equivalence with both types fixed.
