# Statement


Assume $p>h_{\bG}$. Let $\bG$ have root system of type $F_4$, $E_6$, $E_7$, or $E_8$. Let $\bT\subsetneq\bM\subsetneq\bG$ be a Levi subgroup containing the fixed maximal torus, and let $w\in N_{\bM}(\bT)$ be elliptic. Form each relative height-layer graph $\fB(\BPhi^+,\BDelta,h)$. Use the full-source convention of the cited non-Borel computation: delete every individually non-obstructing target, contract source leaves in the resulting full layer, retain an arbitrary set of the remaining targets together with every surviving source, contract source leaves again, discard isolated sources, and take the nonempty connected components of the resulting core. Call such a component $\fC$ an obstructing-cycle candidate. A target set is non-obstructing when the union of its target $w$-orbits contains two roots whose nonzero difference lies in $\Phi_{\bM}$. For an associated absolute bipartite graph with sources $S$ and targets $T$, call it admissible when the discriminant family over the simple-root torus cut out by $$\sum_{\beta\to\alpha}N_{\beta,\alpha-\beta}x_{\alpha-\beta}y_\beta=0\qquad(\alpha\in T)$$ is smooth of relative dimension $\#S-\#T$. Then, for every relative height $h$: (1) if $\bG$ has type $F_4$, there is no obstructing-cycle candidate; equivalently every cycle in this full-source sense is non-obstructing; (2) if $\bG$ has type $E_6$, $E_7$, or $E_8$, every obstructing-cycle candidate $\fC$ has admissible associated absolute bipartite graph $\fC_{\abs}$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The two ambient restrictions are forced by the cited context. The appendix is explicitly the non-Borel computation, and the generator enumerates all nonempty proper subsets of the simple roots while skipping the empty Levi subset. It also uses the full-source target-selection convention stated above. For every such Levi, it identifies each connected Cartan component, enumerates one representative of every elliptic Weyl conjugacy class, constructs the relative root orbits and height layers, and computes all singleton and pair witnesses for non-obstruction. Non-obstruction is existential in two absolute roots, so these singleton and pair tests detect it completely. Any target set not detected is enumerated. The verifier contracts source leaves before and after target restriction. Every component surviving those contractions is therefore one of the stated obstructing-cycle candidates. In type $F_4$, the immutable certificate records 19 configurations, 10 initial relative cores, and two obstructing target subsets; both contract, leaving no absolute core. In types $E_6,E_7,E_8$, respectively, it records 68, 145, and 304 configurations and 1, 12, and 22 surviving absolute cores. For each distinct absolute core, the verifier forms the displayed relative Jacobian and exhibits a maximal minor that is a nonzero monomial. Source-leaf contraction preserves admissibility because the equation at the deleted target has a unit coefficient $N_{\beta,\alpha-\beta}x_{\alpha-\beta}$ on the simple-root torus and uniquely eliminates the deleted source variable. The certified monomial coefficients are units under $p>h_{\bG}$. Hence every surviving Jacobian has full row rank everywhere, its kernel is a vector bundle of rank $\#S-\#T$, and the associated discriminant family is smooth of the required relative dimension.
