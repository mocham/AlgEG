# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, let $B=B_{\PGL_2}$, let $\cW_B\to\cX_{\G_m}$ be the mod-$p$ Weil-Deligne stack, and let $W_B$, $X_B$, and $\cX_B$ be the corresponding destackified Weil-Deligne space, Borel destackification, and Borel-valued Emerton-Gee stack. For an irreducible component $C\subset\cW_B$, let $\bar C$ be its image in $\cX_{\G_m}$. The component $C$ is called Steinberg when $\bar C$ is not an irreducible component of $\cX_{\G_m}$. Define
\[
\cX_B(C):=C\times_{\cW_B}\cX_B,\qquad X_B(C):=C\times_{\cW_B}X_B.
\]
Equivalently, a Steinberg component is isomorphic to $[\Spec\bFp[s]/\G_m]$, while a non-Steinberg component is isomorphic to $[\Spec\bFp[t^{\pm1}]/\G_m]$.
