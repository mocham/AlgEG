# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be a finite extension and let $R$ be a commutative ring of finite type over $\bFp$. If $\bfE_F^+$ is the positive characteristic-$p$ period ring with parameter $T_F$, define
\[
\bfE_{F,R}:=\left(\varprojlim_n(\bfE_F^+/T_F^n)\otimes_{\Fp}R\right)[1/T_F].
\]
An *etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $\bfE_{F,R}$* is a finite projective $\bfE_{F,R}$-module $M$ equipped with semilinear actions of $\Gamma$ and $\varphi$ such that the linearized Frobenius map $\varphi^*M\to M$ is an isomorphism.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

This is a definition, so no proof is required. Here Gamma and T_F are the cyclotomic and field-of-norms data fixed in F01-013. The inverse limit is the displayed T_F-adic completion with coefficients in R, after which T_F is inverted. For a semilinear Frobenius phi_M, the linearization is

  phi^*M = E_{F,R} tensor_{phi,E_{F,R}} M -> M,   a tensor m |-> a phi_M(m).

The requirement that this map be an isomorphism is the etaleness condition. As usual in the term (phi,Gamma)-module, the two semilinear actions are understood to be compatible with the corresponding commuting actions on the period ring.

The period-ring formula and module conditions agree verbatim with References/GrobV13/grob-v13.tex:914-918; the cyclotomic notation is at lines 906-913.
