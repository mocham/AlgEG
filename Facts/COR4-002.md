# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, put $d_F=[F:\mathbb Q_p]$, and let $\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$ be the Borel of a split pinned reductive group over $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_p$, with $p$ larger than its Coxeter number. Let $K\subset U$ be a $\mathbf T$-stable normal subgroup with root set $\Phi_K$, $K$-height $\operatorname{Ht}_K$, and $K$-simple roots $\Delta_K$. Fix a height-compatible total order on $\Phi_K$.

In a recursive gauge chart, let $r_\alpha$ be the coefficient of the standard degree-two obstruction class for a root $\alpha$ of $K$-height greater than one. Choose the blockwise local-duality normal forms of COR17-017 and write
\[
\langle\vec x_\beta,\vec x_\gamma\rangle_{\beta,\gamma}
=x_{\beta,\mathrm{reg}}^{\mathsf T}J_{\beta,\gamma}x_{\gamma,\mathrm{reg}}
+x_{\beta,d_F+1}s_\gamma+x_{\gamma,d_F+1}s_\beta,
\]
where $J_{\beta,\gamma}$ is a unit multiple of the identity on a distinct-dual block and a unit multiple of the symplectic antidiagonal matrix on a self-dual block. Let $\mathcal D_\alpha$ contain one orientation $(\beta,\delta)$ of every unordered decomposition $\alpha=\beta+\delta$ for which at least one summand lies in $\Delta_K$. Then
\[
r_\alpha=s_\alpha(t_\alpha-1)
+\sum_{(\beta,\delta)\in\mathcal D_\alpha}
N_{\beta,\delta}\langle\vec x_\beta,\vec x_\delta\rangle_{\beta,\delta}
+Q_\alpha,
\]
where $Q_\alpha$ is independent of every coordinate attached to a root of $K$-height $\operatorname{Ht}_K(\alpha)-1$. On a hyperbolic slice, the leading Jacobian block differs from the former identity-pairing block only by coordinate permutations and unit row or column scalings.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The quadratic BCH contribution of an unordered pair is the half-sum of its two orders. Swapping degree-one cochains changes the cup sign, while swapping root vectors changes the Chevalley sign, so the signs cancel and one oriented copy remains. COR17-017 supplies the correct perfect-pairing tensor: identity on distinct-dual blocks and symplectic antidiagonal on self-dual blocks. Its hyperbolic-slice calculation preserves every leading rank witness.

If a monomial in the root-$\alpha$ obstruction contains a coordinate of height $\operatorname{Ht}_K(\alpha)-1$, root-height additivity forces all remaining root weight to have height one. It is therefore one of the displayed quadratic terms; no cubic or higher BCH term contains that coordinate block. The remainder $Q_\alpha$ is independent of the entire block.
