# Statement


Let $k$ be a field of characteristic zero, let $G$ be a smooth affine group scheme over $k$, let $(A,\mathfrak m_A)$ be an Artin local $k$-algebra, and let $I\subset A$ be an ideal such that $I\mathfrak m_A=0$. If $D_A$ is a $G$-torsor and its reduction $D_{A/I}$ carries an exact tensor filtration $\mathcal F_{A/I}^{\bullet}$, then this filtration lifts to an exact tensor filtration on $D_A$. The set of lifts is a torsor under $I\otimes_{A/\mathfrak m_A}(\operatorname{ad}D_{A/\mathfrak m_A}/\mathcal F^0_{A/\mathfrak m_A}(\operatorname{ad}D_{A/\mathfrak m_A}))$. In particular, forgetting the filtration is formally smooth with this relative tangent space.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

By D09-002, the exact tensor filtration over the Artin local ring $A/I$ is split by a cocharacter $\lambda_{A/I}:\mathbb G_m\to\operatorname{Aut}_G(D_{A/I})$. The inner form $\operatorname{Aut}_G(D_A)$ is smooth. Applying D09-001 to the nilpotent thickening $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}A$ lifts $\lambda_{A/I}$ to a cocharacter over $A$, hence lifts the filtration.

Any two lifted cocharacters are conjugate by an element reducing to the identity. Since $I\mathfrak m_A=0$, the infinitesimal kernel is $I\otimes_{A/\mathfrak m_A}\operatorname{ad}D_{A/\mathfrak m_A}$. Such conjugation preserves the lifted filtration exactly when its tangent vector lies in $I\otimes\mathcal F^0(\operatorname{ad}D)$. Quotienting by this stabilizer gives the displayed vector space, which acts simply transitively on the lifts. Existence for every square-zero extension and the tangent-torsor description give formal smoothness.
