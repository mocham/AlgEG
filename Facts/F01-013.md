# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be a finite extension and let $F(\mu_{p^\infty})$ be its cyclotomic $p$-power extension. Write
\[
\Gal(F(\mu_{p^\infty})/F)\cong\Delta_F\times\Gamma,
\]
where $\Delta_F$ is the torsion factor and $\Gamma\cong\Z_p$. Define
\[
F_{\cyc}:=F(\mu_{p^\infty})^{\Delta_F}.
\]
If $k_{F_{\cyc}}$ is the residue field of $F_{\cyc}$ and $T_F$ is the field-of-norms parameter, then the characteristic-$p$ period field is denoted
\[
\mathbf E_F=k_{F_{\cyc}}(\!(T_F)\!).
\]
