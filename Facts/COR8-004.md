# Statement


In the twisted-root setup for a connected reductive group $\mathbf G$, assume $p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Let $w$ be the relevant Weyl element, let $\boldsymbol\alpha$ be a $w$-orbit of roots split by the unramified extension $F_n/F$, and put $\mathbb G_{m,\boldsymbol\alpha}:=\prod_{\alpha\in\boldsymbol\alpha}\mathbb G_{m,\alpha}$. Let $\mathcal W_{U_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\rtimes\mathbb G_{m,\boldsymbol\alpha}\langle w\rangle}\to\mathcal X_{U_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\rtimes\mathbb G_{m,\boldsymbol\alpha}\langle w\rangle}$ be the relative mod-$p$ Weil-Deligne stack representing the first-Tor presheaf of $H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}$ over $X_{\mathbb G_{m,\boldsymbol\alpha}\langle w\rangle}$. Then $p\nmid n$ and $\mathcal W_{U_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\rtimes\mathbb G_{m,\boldsymbol\alpha}\langle w\rangle}=\left(\mathcal W_{F_n,U_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\rtimes\mathbb G_{m,\boldsymbol\alpha}}\times_{\mathcal X_{F_n,\mathbb G_{m,\boldsymbol\alpha}}}\mathcal X_{\mathbb G_{m,\boldsymbol\alpha}\langle w\rangle}\right)^{\operatorname{Gal}(F_n/F)}$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The orbit degree $n$ divides the order of the Weyl group. Under $p>h_{\mathbf G}$, the standard Weyl-order lemma gives $p\nmid n$. F04-010 identifies the twisted etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $F$ with its base change to $F_n$ equipped with $\operatorname{Gal}(F_n/F)$-descent data. Because $n$ is invertible on every mod-$p$ coefficient algebra, the Reynolds idempotent makes invariants exact. Equivariant finite-projective resolutions therefore identify the first-Tor functor downstairs with the invariants of the split first-Tor functor after the displayed base change. The two stacks represent these functors, so Yoneda gives the fixed-locus identity.
