# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $l$ be a prime, and let $E/\mathbb Q_l$ have ring of integers $\mathcal O$ and residue field $\mathbb F$. Let $G$ be a smooth affine group scheme over $\mathcal O$ whose geometric fibers are reductive and whose center is smooth, and let $\bar\rho:G_K\to G(\mathbb F)$ be residual. Write $G^{\mathrm{ab}}$ and $G^{\mathrm{der}}$ for the abelianization and derived subgroup of $G$, let $\mathrm{ab}:G\to G^{\mathrm{ab}}$ be the quotient, and fix a character $\psi:G_K\to G^{\mathrm{ab}}(\mathcal O)$ whose reduction satisfies $\bar\psi=\mathrm{ab}\circ\bar\rho$. Fix an inertial type $\tau$ and, if $l=p$, a $p$-adic Hodge type $\mathbf v$; let $P_{\mathbf v}$ be the parabolic conjugacy class stabilizing the tensor filtration defined by $\mathbf v$, and put $\delta_{l=p}=1$ if $l=p$ and $0$ otherwise. If $R_{\bar\rho}^{\square,\tau,\mathbf v}\ne0$, then $\operatorname{Spec}R_{\bar\rho}^{\square,\tau,\mathbf v}[1/l]$ has a dense regular open and a Zariski-dense set of very smooth points, is reduced and locally a complete intersection, and is equidimensional of dimension
\[
\dim_EG+\delta_{l=p}\dim_E((\operatorname{Res}_{E\otimes K/E}G)/P_{\mathbf v}).
\]
The fixed-character generic fiber $\operatorname{Spec}R_{\bar\rho}^{\square,\tau,\mathbf v,\psi}[1/l]$ has a dense regular open and a Zariski-dense set of very smooth points and is equidimensional of dimension
\[
\dim_EG^{\mathrm{der}}+\delta_{l=p}\dim_E((\operatorname{Res}_{E\otimes K/E}G)/P_{\mathbf v}).
\]
A closed point is very smooth if its restriction to every finite extension $K'/K$ is a regular point of the corresponding fixed-type deformation space.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put $X=\operatorname{Spec}R_{\bar\rho}^{\square,\tau,\mathbf v}[1/l]$. By F09-018, the natural map from $X$ to the fixed-inertial-type Weil-Deligne stack is flat and its completed morphism at every closed point is formally smooth. By D01-002, the target has a dense smooth locus, dense very smooth points, and a framed presentation that is reduced, equidimensional, and locally a complete intersection. Flat pullback of the dense smooth locus and completed formal smoothness give a dense regular open in $X$; the same argument after every finite extension gives the dense very smooth set. Pulling back the framed Weil-Deligne presentation gives a smooth cover of $X$ whose completed local rings are power-series rings over reduced complete-intersection rings, proving reducedness and the local-complete-intersection assertion.

At a regular closed point, D01-004 identifies the unframed tangent problem with the Weil-Deligne problem when $l\ne p$ and with the filtered $(\varphi,N)$ problem when $l=p$. Framing contributes $\dim_EG$. In the latter case COR7-004 shows that lifting a filtration of type $\mathbf v$ is formally smooth with tangent dimension $\dim_E((\operatorname{Res}_{E\otimes K/E}G)/P_{\mathbf v})$. These constant tangent dimensions give the first equidimensionality formula.

For deformations satisfying $\mathrm{ab}\circ\rho=\psi$, the same argument is performed in the fixed fiber of the abelianized character. Its infinitesimal change-of-framing space is $\operatorname{Lie}G^{\mathrm{der}}$, while the filtration term is unchanged. This gives the fixed-character formula and the same regular/very-smooth conclusions. This is the fixed-determinant repetition indicated in the source at lines 3121-3125.
