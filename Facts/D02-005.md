# Statement


Let $k$ be a field, let $A$ be a finite-type $k$-algebra whose irreducible components all have dimension at least $d$, and let $f\in A$. Then every irreducible component of $\operatorname{Spec}(A/(f))$ has dimension at least $d-1$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let $\mathfrak q$ be a minimal prime over $(f)$ and choose a minimal prime $\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathfrak q$ of $A$. Minimality of $\mathfrak q$ over $(f)$ implies that $\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p$ is minimal over the image of $f$ in $A/\mathfrak p$. Stacks Project Tag 00KV gives $\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p)\leq1$. The affine domain $A/\mathfrak p$ is catenary and satisfies the dimension formula, so \[\dim A/\mathfrak q=\dim A/\mathfrak p-\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p)\geq d-1.\] This applies to every irreducible component of the principal hypersurface, including the height-zero case when $f$ vanishes on an ambient component.
