# Statement


Let $R$ be a commutative ring, let $u\in R$ be a nonzerodivisor, and let $C^\bullet$ be an $R$-linear cochain complex such that $H^2(C^\bullet)\cong R/(u)$. Fix $b\in Z^2(C^\bullet)$ corresponding to $1\in R/(u)$ and $c_{\mathrm{std}}\in C^1$ with $d(c_{\mathrm{std}})=ub$. For every $B\in Z^2(C^\bullet)$ there is a unique class $\bar a\in R/(u)$ such that $[B]=\bar a[b]$. For any lift $a\in R$ of $\bar a$, there is $c\in C^1$ with $B=ab-dc$. If $a'=a+uq$, then the same equality holds with $c'=c+qc_{\mathrm{std}}$. Consequently, in any finite obstruction equation $r=su+\sum_j a_jm_j$, changing lifts to $a_j+uq_j$ changes the equation only by the triangular coordinate translation $s\mapsto s-\sum_jq_jm_j$; the resulting hypersurface is isomorphic over the other coordinates.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The chosen class $[b]$ identifies $H^2(C^\bullet)$ with $R/(u)$, so $[B]$ has a unique coefficient $\bar a$ in that quotient. For a lift $a$, the class of $B-ab$ vanishes, hence $B-ab$ is a coboundary; changing the sign of its primitive gives $B=ab-dc$. If $a'=a+uq$, then

$$a'b-d(c+qc_{\mathrm{std}})=ab+uqb-dc-qub=ab-dc=B.$$

For finitely many coefficients put $Q=\sum_jq_jm_j$. The equation formed from the new lifts is $r'=su+\sum_j(a_j+uq_j)m_j=(s+Q)u+\sum_ja_jm_j$. The polynomial-ring automorphism sending $s$ to $s-Q$ carries $r'$ to $r$, proving the final assertion.
