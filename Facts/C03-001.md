# Statement


Let $p=3$, put $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_3$, let $F=\mathbb Q_3(\mu_3)$, and put $R=k[t,t^{-1}]$. Let $M$ be the rank-one etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $\mathbf E_{F,R}$ whose fibers are the unramified characters with Frobenius parameter $t$. At $t=1$, the fiber character is both trivial and equal to the mod-$3$ cyclotomic character of $G_F$. Consequently $[F:\mathbb Q_3]=2$ and
\[
\dim_k H^1_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M_{t=1})=2+1+1=4.
\]
Thus the $H^0$ and cyclotomic $H^2$ contributions occur simultaneously in this fiber.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The extension $F=\mathbb Q_3(\mu_3)$ is totally ramified of degree $2$, so $[F:\mathbb Q_3]=2$. Since $\mu_3\subset F$, the mod-$3$ cyclotomic character is trivial on $G_F$. The fiber at $t=1$ is also the trivial unramified character. Applying COR3-001 therefore gives
\[
\dim_k H^1_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M_{t=1})=[F:\mathbb Q_3]+\mathbf 1_{1=1}+\mathbf 1_{1=\bar\varepsilon_F}=2+1+1=4.
\]
Equivalently, local Tate duality gives $h^2(G_F,k)=h^0(G_F,k(1))=1$, while $h^0(G_F,k)=1$, and the local Euler characteristic gives the same value. The calculation shows why the cyclotomic $H^2$ indicator is required in addition to the unramified $H^0$ jump.
