# Statement


The correct one-root nonabelian Herr obstruction is the root projection of a difference of two full BCH series. Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $A$ be an $\mathbb F_p$-algebra, write $\mathbf E_{F,A}$ for the characteristic-$p$ coefficient period ring, and let $\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$ be a split pinned Borel in a reductive group $\mathbf G$, with $p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Let $K=U_{\Phi_K}\subset U$ be $\mathbf T$-stable and normal, choose a root order compatible with $K$-height, and fix $\alpha\in\Phi_K$. Suppose the torus parts $f^{\mathrm{ss}},g^{\mathrm{ss}}$ satisfy their torus cocycle equation and the lower pair is a cocycle modulo $U_{\alpha^\vee}$. In logarithmic coordinates put

$$F_{<\alpha}=\sum_{\beta<\alpha}c_{f,\beta},\qquad G_{<\alpha}=\sum_{\beta<\alpha}c_{g,\beta},$$

$$P_{<\alpha}=\operatorname{Ad}(f^{\mathrm{ss}})\varphi(G_{<\alpha}),\qquad Q_{<\alpha}=\operatorname{Ad}(g^{\mathrm{ss}})\gamma(F_{<\alpha}).$$

For $c_{f,\alpha},c_{g,\alpha}\in U_{\alpha^\vee}(\mathbf E_{F,A})$, set $c_{f,\leq\alpha}=\exp(F_{<\alpha}+c_{f,\alpha})$ and $c_{g,\leq\alpha}=\exp(G_{<\alpha}+c_{g,\alpha})$. Then the extended pair is a cocycle if and only if

$$\bigl(c_{f,\alpha}-\operatorname{Ad}(g^{\mathrm{ss}})\gamma(c_{f,\alpha})\bigr)-\bigl(c_{g,\alpha}-\operatorname{Ad}(f^{\mathrm{ss}})\varphi(c_{g,\alpha})\bigr)=D^{\mathrm{BCH}}_\alpha,$$

where

$$D^{\mathrm{BCH}}_\alpha=\pi_\alpha\left(\operatorname{BCH}(G_{<\alpha},Q_{<\alpha})-\operatorname{BCH}(F_{<\alpha},P_{<\alpha})\right).$$

Here $\pi_\alpha$ is root projection. The torus actions are rootwise: the $\beta$-component of $P_{<\alpha}$ is $\beta(f^{\mathrm{ss}})\varphi(c_{g,\beta})$, and the $\beta$-component of $Q_{<\alpha}$ is $\beta(g^{\mathrm{ss}})\gamma(c_{f,\beta})$.

This obstruction is computed class by class, including all mixed words. In the universal enveloping algebra, for $H=\operatorname{ht}(\alpha)$,

$$\pi_\alpha\operatorname{BCH}(X,Y)=\pi_\alpha\sum_{m=1}^{H}\frac{(-1)^{m+1}}{m}\left(\sum_{\substack{r,s\geq0,\ 0<r+s\leq H}}\frac{X^rY^s}{r!s!}\right)^m.$$

Equivalently, write $\operatorname{BCH}(X,Y)=\sum_{n=1}^{H}Z_n$, where $Z_n$ has bracket length $n$, $Z_1=X+Y$, and for $n\geq2$,

$$nZ_n=\frac12[X-Y,Z_{n-1}]+\sum_{1\leq p\leq(n-1)/2}\frac{B_{2p}}{(2p)!}\sum_{\substack{k_1+\cdots+k_{2p}=n-1\\k_i\geq1}}[Z_{k_1},[\cdots,[Z_{k_{2p}},X+Y]\cdots]].$$

In particular, $Z_2=\frac12[X,Y]$ and $Z_3=\frac1{12}[X,[X,Y]]+\frac1{12}[[X,Y],Y]$. Positive-root height makes the recursion finite. The paper needs at most $H=11$, the highest positive-root height in type $F_4$; the standing bound $p>12$ makes every displayed coefficient invertible there.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Cancel the common torus part in the group cocycle equation. The unipotent equation is exactly

$$\exp(F_{<\alpha}+c_{f,\alpha})\exp(\operatorname{Ad}(f^{\mathrm{ss}})\varphi(G_{<\alpha}+c_{g,\alpha}))=\exp(G_{<\alpha}+c_{g,\alpha})\exp(\operatorname{Ad}(g^{\mathrm{ss}})\gamma(F_{<\alpha}+c_{f,\alpha})).$$

Applying the truncated logarithm gives equality of the two BCH expressions. The root group $U_{\alpha^\vee}$ is central in the quotient through $\alpha$, so its four top-coordinate contributions occur linearly. Moving them to the left gives exactly the displayed sign and leaves the difference of the two lower BCH root projections on the right.

For the expansion, use $\operatorname{BCH}(X,Y)=\log(\exp X\exp Y)$ and $\log(1+R)=\sum_{m\geq1}(-1)^{m+1}R^m/m$, with $R=\sum_{r,s\geq0,r+s>0}X^rY^s/(r!s!)$. Root projection to $\alpha$ kills every term of total positive-root height greater than $\operatorname{ht}(\alpha)$, proving the finite signed factorial formula. The Bernoulli recurrence is the homogeneous primitive form of the same identity. It gives both cubic terms with coefficient $1/12$ and all higher mixed terms.

The focused Sage calculation independently implements this recurrence through class 11. In type $B_2$ it obtains the lower products $(5,19/3,10/3,2)$ and $(5,19/3,10/3,-2)$ and hence obstruction $-4e_d$, while the incomplete one-sided series gives $-e_d/9$. It also verifies the symbolic class-three polynomial and the full corrected top-coordinate solution.
