# Statement


Let $\mathbf G$ be a connected reductive group with connected center, let $\mathbf T\subset\mathbf G$ be a maximal torus, and let $\Delta$ be a set of simple roots. Then the pairing map
\[
X_*(\mathbf T)\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^{\Delta},\qquad
\lambda\longmapsto(\langle\lambda,\delta\rangle)_{\delta\in\Delta}
\]
is surjective. Equivalently, for every collection of integers $(m_\delta)_{\delta\in\Delta}$ there is $\lambda\in X_*(\mathbf T)$ with $\langle\lambda,\delta\rangle=m_\delta$ for all $\delta\in\Delta$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let $Q=\mathbb Z\Delta\subset X^*(\mathbf T)$ be the root lattice. Connectedness of the center is equivalent to $X^*(\mathbf T)/Q$ being torsion-free. This quotient is therefore a finitely generated free abelian group, so the exact sequence $0\to Q\to X^*(\mathbf T)\to X^*(\mathbf T)/Q\to0$ splits as a sequence of abelian groups. Applying $\operatorname{Hom}(-,\mathbb Z)$ gives a surjection $X_*(\mathbf T)=\operatorname{Hom}(X^*(\mathbf T),\mathbb Z)\to\operatorname{Hom}(Q,\mathbb Z)$. Since $\Delta$ is a basis of $Q$, the target is $\mathbb Z^{\Delta}$, and the restriction map is exactly the displayed simple-root pairing map.
