# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $A$ be an $\mathbb F_p$-algebra,
and write $\mathbf E_{F,A}$ for the characteristic-$p$ coefficient
period ring. Let $\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$ be a split pinned
Borel in a reductive group $\mathbf G$, with $p>h_{\mathbf G}$, and
let $K=U_{\Phi_K}$ be $\mathbf T$-stable and normal. For $j\geq1$,
let $K_{\geq j}$ be generated by the roots of $K$-height at least
$j$, and put

$$V_{h+1}=K_{\geq h+1}/K_{\geq h+2}
=\bigoplus_{\operatorname{Ht}_K(\alpha)=h+1}U_{\alpha^\vee}.$$

Suppose $f_h=\exp(F_h)t_f$ and $g_h=\exp(G_h)t_g$ form a
nonabelian Herr cocycle valued in $(K/K_{\geq h+1})\rtimes\mathbf T$.
Put

$$P_h=\operatorname{Ad}(t_f)\varphi(G_h),\qquad
Q_h=\operatorname{Ad}(t_g)\gamma(F_h),$$

and, for every $\alpha$ of $K$-height $h+1$, define

$$D_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}
=\pi_\alpha\left(\operatorname{BCH}(G_h,Q_h)
-\operatorname{BCH}(F_h,P_h)\right),$$

where every BCH class through the ordinary root height of $\alpha$
is included. Then $(f_h,g_h)$ extends to a cocycle valued in
$(K/K_{\geq h+2})\rtimes\mathbf T$ if and only if

$$(D_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}})_{
\operatorname{Ht}_K(\alpha)=h+1}
\in B^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}
\left(V_{h+1}(\mathbf E_{F,A})\right).$$

Equivalently, the class of this vector vanishes in
$H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(V_{h+1}(\mathbf E_{F,A}))$. No reduction to a
single ordered half-cup representative is part of the criterion.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The layer $V_{h+1}$ is central in $K/K_{\geq h+2}$ and is the
direct sum of its root groups. Apply COR14-001 to every root
$\alpha$ in that layer. The unknown top-root coordinates occur
linearly, and their left sides together are precisely the Herr
differential of a $V_{h+1}$-valued $1$-cochain. The remaining
lower-root term is the displayed vector of BCH projections.
Therefore top-root coordinates exist exactly when that vector is a
Herr coboundary.

The computation is class by class. Its quadratic part is the full
projection of $([G_h,Q_h]-[F_h,P_h])/2$, with both root orders.
For bracket length at least three, the Bernoulli recursion in
COR14-001 retains every mixed word. Positive-root height makes the
expansion finite. Thus no cocycle assumption on an individual
height-$h$ coordinate and no unproved cochain homotopy deleting the
reversed quadratic order are needed.
