# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let $\mathbf G$ be split connected reductive with connected center, with $p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Let $\boldsymbol\alpha\subset\Phi^{\mathbf M+}$ be a $w$-orbit, and take $U_{\boldsymbol\alpha}$ in its relative-height associated graded quotient. Let $C=\operatorname{Spec}A$ be a connected affine component of the torus-normalizer destackification. Assume that over the prime-to-$p$ unramified splitting field the universal root family is the normalized family $\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}\operatorname{ur}_{t_{\boldsymbol\alpha}}$, that $u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}=t_{\boldsymbol\alpha}-1$ is a nonzerodivisor, and that the recursive chart and descent hypotheses of COR17-018 hold.

If $C$ contains a specialization with $t_{\boldsymbol\alpha}=1$, then after choosing a root coordinate the destackified rootwise Weil--Deligne scheme is
\[
W_{\boldsymbol\alpha,C}\cong
\operatorname{Spec}A[s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}]/(s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}).
\]
Its Steinberg closed subscheme is
\[
\operatorname{Spec}(A/(u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}))[s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}],
\]
and its irreducible components are called Steinberg components. The other branch is $V(s_{\boldsymbol\alpha})\cong C$. If $C$ has no such specialization, then the rootwise $H^2$ vanishes and $W_{\boldsymbol\alpha,C}\cong C$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The hypothesis \(p>h_{\mathbf G}\) makes the orbit-splitting degree prime to \(p\). COR13-001 and COR13-002 identify the corrected cyclotomic character of the stabilizer and its Hodge type, while COR2-002 places the orbit module in the correct associated graded. D04-002 computes its universal top Herr cohomology scheme-theoretically as the exponent-one quotient \(A/(u_{\boldsymbol\alpha})\); D04-001 represents the resulting first-Tor functor by the two-branch locus \(s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}=0\). COR8-004 carries this calculation through the prime-to-\(p\) twisted fixed locus. Away from the cyclotomic divisor, local Tate duality and base change give vanishing.

For an orbit of arbitrary relative height, COR2-002 places the root module in the correct graded quotient. Over the splitting field, D04-002 and COR8-006 identify the normalized universal top Herr cohomology with the exponent-one quotient $A/(u_{\boldsymbol\alpha})$. COR17-018 shows that the absolute classes form one transitive orbit, so prime-to-$p$ descent leaves one such quotient. D04-001 represents its first-Tor functor by $s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}=0$, and COR8-004 and F04-015 descend this presentation. If there is no specialization with $u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}=0$, local Tate duality and finite-type base change make the family $H^2$ module zero.

W_{U_{\Balpha}\rtimes\G_{m,\Balpha}\langle w\rangle}\big|_C

\[

## Post-proof remarks

The V14 definition is stated for an arbitrary relative-root orbit only under the normalized-family, nonzerodivisor, recursive-chart, and prime-to-p descent hypotheses printed in the manuscript.  Without those hypotheses, the narrower relative-height-one statement remains the available unconditional form.
