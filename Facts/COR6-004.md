# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $\bG$ be connected reductive over $\overline{\mathbb F}_p$, and let $\bar\rho:\operatorname{Gal}_F\to\bG(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$ be semisimple in the sense of $\bG$-complete reducibility. Up to $\bG(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$-conjugation, there are a Levi subgroup $\bM\subset\bG$ minimal among Levi subgroups containing the image, a maximal torus $\bT\subset\bM$, and an elliptic element $w\in N_{\bM}(\bT)/\bT$ such that the connected centralizer of $\bar\rho(I_F)$ in $\bM$ is $\bT$ and the image of $\bar\rho$ lies in the inverse image of $\langle w\rangle$ in $N_{\bM}(\bT)$. Once $\bM$ is chosen minimally, $\bT$ is uniquely determined. After choosing a pinning of $\bM$ containing $\bT$ and writing $\dot w$ for the resulting Tits representative, the factorization can be written $$\bar\rho(\operatorname{Gal}_F)\subset\langle\bT(\overline{\mathbb F}_p),\dot w\rangle.$$ The generated subgroup is independent of the chosen lift of $w$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Choose a Levi $\bM$ of minimal dimension containing the image. If the image lay in a proper parabolic of $\bM$, complete reducibility in $\bG$ would place it in a smaller Levi, contradicting minimality; hence it is $\bM$-irreducible. By F09-019, wild inertia is trivial, so the inertia image is finite cyclic of order prime to $p$ and consists of semisimple elements. Put $C=Z_{\bM}(\bar\rho(I_F))^\circ$. It is reductive, Frobenius normalizes it, and inertia lies in $Z_C(C)$. The subgroup generated by the Frobenius image and $Z_C(C)$ contains the full Galois image and is therefore $\bM$-irreducible. F09-020 forces $C$ to be a torus. Every maximal torus containing inertia lies in $C$, so $C=\bT$ is the unique such maximal torus, and Frobenius normalizes it. Thus the Weyl image is generated by one class $w$. If $w$ fixed a nonzero vector in the real root space, a Weyl-invariant inner product would give a noncentral rational cocharacter $\lambda$ fixed by $w$. The torus and a lift of $w$ would centralize $\lambda$, placing the image in the proper Levi $Z_{\bM}(\lambda)$, contrary to minimality. Hence $w$ is elliptic. Finally, any two lifts of $w$ differ by an element of $\bT$, so adjoining either lift to $\bT$ gives the same subgroup; a chosen pinning singles out the Tits lift $\dot w$.
