# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite and let $(G,B,T)$ be split pinned reductive with dual pinned group $(\bG,\bB,\bT)$; assume $p>h_{\bG}$. Let $U$ be the unipotent radical of $\bB$, fix a height-compatible total ordering of the positive roots, and fix a positive root $\alpha$ of height $h$. In $U/U_{h+1}$, let $U_{\leq\alpha}$ and $U_{<\alpha}$ be the successive subquotients fitting into $1\to U_{\alpha^\vee}\to U_{\leq\alpha}\to U_{<\alpha}\to1$, and let $\cX_{\leq\alpha}$ be the partially reduced Emerton-Gee stack for $U_{\leq\alpha}\rtimes\bT$. Put $F_{\cyc}=F(\mu_{p^\infty})^{\Delta_F}$ and let $k_{F_{\cyc}}$ be its residue field. Define $\cX^+_{\leq\alpha}$ over the ramified locus by $\cX_{\leq\alpha}\times\G_a^{[k_{F_{\cyc}}:\F_p]}$; over the unramified locus, its objects over a morphism $\Spec A\to\cX_{\leq\alpha}$ are pairs $(P'_A,\xi)$, where $P'_A$ is the corresponding etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-structured $(U_{\leq\alpha}\rtimes\bT)$-torsor lifting its induced $(U_{<\alpha}\rtimes\bT)$-torsor $P_A$, and $\xi:P_A\to P'_A$ is a $\Gamma$-stable section. Then
\[
[\cX^+_{\leq\alpha}/\G_a^{\oplus[k_{F_{\cyc}}:\F_p]}]\cong\cX_{\leq\alpha}.
\]

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

### Universal pullback reduction

Over the ramified locus the assertion is immediate from the definition $\mathcal X^+_{\leq\alpha}=\mathcal X_{\leq\alpha}\times\mathbb G_a^d$, where $d=[k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}:\mathbb F_p]$. It remains to treat the unramified locus.

Put $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_p$, $k_0=k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}$, and $R=k[t^{\pm1}]$. Let $P'_R\to P_R$ denote the exact rootwise projection in the universal unramified-character family and let $\mathscr S_R$ be its sheaf of sections. Its action is $(\gamma\cdot\xi)=\gamma_{P'_R}\circ\xi\circ\gamma_{P_R}^{-1}$. Under the clarified universal construction, a coefficient map $R\to A$ pulls back this projection, its section sheaf, the displayed $\Gamma$-action, differences of sections, and translation to the corresponding data over $A$. Thus it is enough to construct the fixed section and torsor identity once over the universal Laurent family; no new basis or obstruction argument is needed at each $A$-point.

### Universal Gamma-stable section

Definition extra-Herr makes the relevant identification explicit. After choosing a $\Gamma$-stable generator $e$ of the universal unramified rank-one module $M_R$, an element has an expansion in powers of $T_F$, and its constant term lies in $R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_0$. GrobV13 lines 1405-1413 identify a section itself with a $\Gamma$-stable element having the prescribed constant term. This identifies the universal geometric fiber, not merely the linear module of differences of two already chosen sections.

Let $a\in R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_0$ be the prescribed universal constant coefficient. COR6-002, using S03-001, gives $\gamma(e)=e$ and shows that $a$ is fixed by $\Gamma$. Therefore
\[
 x_a:=a e,\qquad \gamma(x_a)=\gamma(a)\gamma(e)=a e=x_a,\qquad \operatorname{ct}(x_a)=a.
\]
Under the source's geometric identification, $x_a$ is the required actual section $\xi_{\mathrm{univ}}\in\mathscr S_R^\Gamma$. In particular, prescribed coefficient zero gives the distinguished zero-constant-term section when only a base point is required.

This explicitly kills the affine obstruction from D12-002. If $\xi_0$ is any ordinary section, put $m=\xi_{\mathrm{univ}}-\xi_0$ and $c_\gamma=\gamma(\xi_0)-\xi_0$. Then
\[
 c_\gamma+\gamma(m)-m=\gamma(\xi_{\mathrm{univ}})-\xi_{\mathrm{univ}}=0.
\]
Hence $o_R=[c]\in H^1(\Gamma,M_R)$ is zero. D12-002 remains the correct warning for an arbitrary affine $\Gamma$-torsor; here its criterion is satisfied because the extra-Herr geometric identification supplies the point $\xi_{\mathrm{univ}}$, rather than because an invariant linear generator alone is being treated as a point.

### Acting group and quotient

The difference of two $\Gamma$-stable sections lies in $M_R^\Gamma$, and translation by every element of $M_R^\Gamma$ preserves stability and acts simply transitively. COR6-002 and S03-001 give
\[
 M_R^\Gamma=(R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_0)e.
\]
Choose once and for all an $\mathbb F_p$-basis $\epsilon_1,\ldots,\epsilon_d$ of $k_0$. Translation by $\sum_i a_i\epsilon_i e$ identifies this invariant vector group with the constant group $\mathbb G_{a,R}^{\oplus d}$. Thus the universal fixed-section morphism is a genuine $\mathbb G_{a,R}^{\oplus d}$-torsor and its quotient is the universal unramified rootwise chart.

All constructions and the section $x_a=a e$ commute with coefficient base change, so the universal torsor identity pulls back to every unramified chart point. Together with the defining product on the ramified open-and-closed locus, this proves
\[
 [\mathcal X^+_{\leq\alpha}/\mathbb G_a^{\oplus[k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}:\mathbb F_p]}]\cong\mathcal X_{\leq\alpha}.
\]

## Faithful GrobV14 proof

```tex
\begin{proof}
By construction $\cX_{\le \alpha}^+$
is a pseudo $\G_a^{\oplus [k_{F_{\cyc}}:\F_p]}$-torsor over
$\cX_{\le \alpha}$.
We still need to show that it is a genuine $\G_a^{\oplus [k_{F_{\cyc}}:\F_p]}$-torsor over the unramified locus.
This is equivalent to the statement that
all $P_A'\to P_A$
admits a $\Gamma$-stable section fppf locally
in $\Spec A$.
Since $P_A$ is affine
and $\Res_{\bfE_{F,A}/A}\G_a$ is quasi-coherent, the coherent cohomology
$H^1(P_A, \Res_{\bfE_{F,A}/A}\G_a)=0$,
and $P_A'$ is indeed a trivial $\Res_{\bfE_{F,A}/A}\G_a$-torsor
over $P_A$.

\EDITXIV{Let $v_1$ be the $\Gamma$-fixed vector in the specialization at
$t=1$.  In the universal unramified twist, base change gives the
$\Gamma$-fixed vector $1\otimes v_1$.  For a prescribed constant
$a\in A\otimes_{\F_p}k_{F_{\cyc}}$, take
\[
x_a=a(1\otimes v_1).
\]
Lemma~\ref{fact:COR6-002} shows that $x_a$ is $\Gamma$-stable and that
all differences of such sections are obtained uniquely in this way.
Thus the pseudo-torsor is a genuine
$\G_a^{\oplus[k_{F_{\cyc}}:\F_p]}$-torsor.}
\end{proof}
```
