# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $l$ be a prime, and let $E/\mathbb Q_l$ have ring of integers $\mathcal O$ and residue field $\mathbb F$. Let $G$ be a smooth affine group scheme over $\mathcal O$ whose geometric fibers are reductive and whose center is smooth, and let $\bar\rho:G_K\to G(\mathbb F)$ be residual. Fix an inertial type $\tau$ and, if $l=p$, a $p$-adic Hodge type $\mathbf v$; let $P_{\mathbf v}$ be the parabolic conjugacy class defined by $\mathbf v$, put $\delta_{l=p}=1$ if $l=p$ and $0$ otherwise, and write $G^{\mathrm{der}}$ and $G^{\mathrm{ab}}$ for the derived subgroup and abelianization. If $R_{\bar\rho}^{\square,\tau,\mathbf v,N=0}\ne0$, then $R_{\bar\rho}^{\square,\tau,\mathbf v,N=0}[1/l]$ is regular and equidimensional of dimension $\dim_EG+\delta_{l=p}\dim_E((\operatorname{Res}_{E\otimes K/E}G)/P_{\mathbf v})$. Now fix a homomorphism $\psi:G_K\to G^{\mathrm{ab}}(\mathcal O)$ whose reduction is $\operatorname{ab}\circ\bar\rho$, and let $R_{\bar\rho}^{\square,\tau,\mathbf v,N=0,\psi}$ denote the quotient on which $\operatorname{ab}\circ\rho=\psi$. If this quotient is nonzero, then $R_{\bar\rho}^{\square,\tau,\mathbf v,N=0,\psi}[1/l]$ is regular and equidimensional of dimension $\dim_EG^{\mathrm{der}}+\delta_{l=p}\dim_E((\operatorname{Res}_{E\otimes K/E}G)/P_{\mathbf v})$. Here $N=0$ means potentially crystalline when $l=p$ and potentially unramified when $l\ne p$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Restrict the fixed-type deformation-to-Weil-Deligne morphism to the closed condition $N=0$. By D05-001, the resulting monodromy-zero Weil-Deligne stack is smooth and equidimensional of stack dimension zero: its deformation complex has only degrees $0$ and $1$. By F09-018, the completed fixed-type morphism is formally smooth at every closed point, and formal smoothness is preserved by the $N=0$ base change. Thus every completed local ring of the nonzero monodromy-zero generic fiber is formally smooth over a smooth local target and is regular.

The tangent calculation uses D01-004 to compare framed fixed-type Galois deformations with the corresponding Weil-Deligne or filtered-module deformations. When $l=p$, COR7-004 shows that the filtration of type $\mathbf v$ contributes the constant relative dimension $\dim_E((\operatorname{Res}_{E\otimes K/E}G)/P_{\mathbf v})$; when $l\ne p$ this contribution is zero. Framing contributes $\dim_EG$, giving the first displayed dimension. These dimensions are constant at all closed points, so the regular generic fiber is equidimensional.

For fixed $\psi$, take the base change fixing the abelianized representation. The same formal-smoothness argument applies. Its tangent condition removes the $\dim_EG^{\mathrm{ab}}$ abelianized directions from the unrestricted calculation, leaving $\dim_EG^{\mathrm{der}}$, while the filtration contribution is unchanged. This gives the second formula and regular equidimensionality whenever the fixed-$\psi$ quotient is nonzero.
