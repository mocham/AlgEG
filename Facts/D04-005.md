# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite. Every continuous representation $\bar\rho:G_F\to\operatorname{PGL}_2(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$ admits a $\operatorname{PGL}_2$-valued crystalline lift of some regular Hodge type and trivial inertial type. If $\bar\rho$ takes values in a Borel subgroup $B\subset\operatorname{PGL}_2$, it admits such a lift taking values in $B$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

This is the $\operatorname{PGL}_2$ specialization of MAIN-002. For a Borel-valued residual representation, the integral group-scheme section $B_{\operatorname{PGL}_2}\to\operatorname{GL}_2$ normalizes an upper-triangular projective matrix by its lower-right entry. EG23 then lifts the resulting chosen extension as an upper-triangular regular crystalline $\operatorname{GL}_2$ representation, and projectivization preserves the Borel, crystallinity, trivial inertial type, and regularity. If the projective image is in no Borel, it is $\operatorname{PGL}_2$-irreducible and Lin22's regular crystalline lifting theorem applies directly. No homomorphic lift of the non-Borel projective representation to $\operatorname{GL}_2$ is used.

Verified through MAIN-002. The biquadratic example only rules out a global reduction of every PGL2 representation to GL2.
