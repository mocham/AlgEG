# Statement


Let F/Q_p be finite, let k be an algebraic extension of a finite field of characteristic p, and let V be a finite-dimensional continuous k-representation of Gal_F obtained by scalar extension from a finite subfield k_0 of k. If D(V) is its associated etale (phi,Gamma)-module, then there are natural isomorphisms H^i(Gal_F,V) = H^i(C_Herr(D(V))) for i=0,1,2. The comparison is functorial in V and compatible with extension of the coefficient field. In particular, it applies to the cyclotomic representation over the algebraic closure of F_p.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

For a finite coefficient field k_0, apply the Herr-Galois comparison theorem in EG23, Theorem at lines 21025-21040, with A=k_0. It gives the asserted functorial isomorphisms in every degree for V_0 and D(V_0). If k/k_0 is algebraic and V=V_0 tensor k, write k as the filtered union of its finite subextensions. A continuous cochain from the compact profinite space Gal_F^n to a discrete scalar extension has finite image, so it is defined over one finite subextension. Thus the continuous Galois cochain complex is the filtered colimit of the finite-coefficient complexes. The Herr complex has only three terms and its coefficient extension is likewise the corresponding filtered colimit. Filtered colimits of modules are exact, so the finite-level comparison isomorphisms pass to cohomology over k. Functoriality and coefficient compatibility pass through the same colimit.
