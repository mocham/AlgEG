# Statement


Let $\Phi^+$ be the positive roots of type $F_4$, written in Bourbaki coordinates. Define four triples $(C,\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$, where $C$ is the complement of the root set of the associated root-subgroup product, by
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{I}:&\ (\{0010\},\{0010\},\{0001,0011,0100,0110,0120\}),\\
\mathrm{II}:&\ (\{0010\},\{0010\},\{0001,0011,0100,0110,0120,1000\}),\\
\mathrm{III}:&\ (\{0010,1000\},\{0010,1000\},\{0001,0011,0100,0110,0120,1100,1110,1120\}),\\
\mathrm{IV}:&\ (\{0001,0010,0011,1000\},\{0001,0010,0011,1000\},\{0100,0110,0111,0120,0121,0122,1100,1110,1111,1120,1121,1122\}).
\end{aligned}
\]
For I, II, and IV choose rotation root $0010$, and for III choose rotation root $1000$. For each triple, form the rational rotational-deformation polynomial system by conjugating along the chosen negative-root one-parameter subgroup, traversing every complete root string with coefficient recurrence
\[
c_{n+1}=c_n\,N_{-\delta_{\mathrm{rot}},\alpha+(n+1)\delta_{\mathrm{rot}}}/(n+1),
\]
and taking the constant terms of the divided first-order deformation equations together with $\sum_\delta\lambda_\delta=1$. Each resulting square system has a rational specialization and rational solution at which every equation vanishes. In the order I, II, III, IV, the Jacobian determinants are
\[
432,\qquad704,\qquad15360,\qquad895795200,
\]
and the complete prime supports of the specialization denominators, required nonzero coordinates, point coordinates, and determinants are
\[
\{2,3\},\qquad\{2,3,5,11\},\qquad\{2,3,5\},\qquad\{2,3,5\}.
\]
Consequently each rational solution spreads to a smooth solution in every characteristic $p>12$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The implementation generates the type-$F_4$ Chevalley constants, follows each complete root string with the displayed divided-power recurrence, and constructs the divided first-order equations directly. For each of the four explicitly listed triples it searches deterministic small rational specializations and points, evaluates every equation exactly, and evaluates the complete square Jacobian determinant.

The immutable SageMath 10.9 certificate records 4, 4, 7, and 12 zero equation values for I-IV. It records rotation roots $0010,0010,1000,0010$ and determinant values $432,704,15360,895795200$. Factoring all specialization values required to remain nonzero, their denominators, all point coordinates, and each determinant gives exactly the four prime supports in the Statement. The root-string coefficients occurring here are integral. The certificate SHA-256 is `c8a59f32de7f28eeaa88905900a53114863e7134f4dd2c5c5662ebb946f8a159`, matching the cited manifest. A fresh read-only SageMath 10.9 execution reproduces the same roots, determinants, supports, and success flag.

After inverting the recorded primes, the rational coordinates define a section of each polynomial system and its Jacobian determinant is a unit. F05-018 therefore makes the section smooth. Every recorded prime is at most 12, so reduction gives the asserted smooth solution for every $p>12$. This pointwise proof does not use either the manuscript's $I+(J)$ Groebner-basis comparison or the later saturation-based general pass criterion.
