# Statement

Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $G$ be a possibly nonsplit reductive group over $K$ that splits over a finite extension $L/K$, and write ${}^LG=\check G\rtimes\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$. Nonabelian Shapiro gives an equivalence of groupoids between parameters $\operatorname{Gal}_K\to{}^LG$ and parameters $\operatorname{Gal}_{\mathbb Q_p}\to{}^L(\operatorname{Res}_{K/\mathbb Q_p}G)$.

# Proof or Examples

## Expanded self-contained proof

### Faithful full quotation

```text
3.1. Non-abelian Shapiro lemma Let G be a possibly non-split reductive group over K that splits over L and let LG := G-check semidirect Gal(L/K). There is an equivalence of groupoids

{parameters Gal_K -> LG} <-> {parameters Gal_Qp -> L Res_{K/Qp} G},

see, for example, [GHS, Lemma 9.4.1] for unramified groups and [St10] for the general case. As a consequence, we will assume K = Qp from now on as all results for Qp automatically imply the same statement for general K.
```

### Verification argument

Put $\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}_{\mathbb Q_p}$, $H=\operatorname{Gal}_K$, and $A=\check G$ with its continuous $H$-action; as implicit in $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, take a finite Galois splitting extension. A parameter whose projection to the finite Galois group is the canonical quotient is the same as a continuous nonabelian cocycle in $Z^1(H,A)$, and conjugacies of parameters are exactly the morphisms in the action groupoid $[Z^1(H,A)/A]$.

The dual group of $\operatorname{Res}_{K/\mathbb Q_p}G$ is the induced $\Gamma$-group $\operatorname{Ind}_H^\Gamma A$: after choosing coset representatives it is a product of one copy of $A$ for each embedding of $K$ into $\overline{\mathbb Q}_p$, with the natural permutation-and-twist action of $\Gamma$. Nonabelian Shapiro is an equivalence of action groupoids
\[
[Z^1(H,A)/A]\simeq[Z^1(\Gamma,\operatorname{Ind}_H^\Gamma A)/\operatorname{Ind}_H^\Gamma A].
\]
Indeed, a cocycle groupoid is equivalently the groupoid of equivariant torsors; induction sends an $H$-equivariant $A$-torsor to a $\Gamma$-equivariant $\operatorname{Ind}_H^\Gamma A$-torsor, while evaluation at the identity coset is a quasi-inverse, on both equivariant torsors and equivariant morphisms. Translating the right-hand cocycle groupoid back into parameters for the restriction-of-scalars L-group proves the claimed equivalence.
