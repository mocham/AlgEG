# Statement

Let K/Q_p be finite, let P=U semidirect M be a parabolic in a connected reductive group, and let rho:Gal_K->M(Qpbar) be de Rham with P-minus-dominant Hodge type. Every P-valued extension rho' of rho is de Rham, and its possible Weil-Deligne types are parameterized by H^2(Gal_K,U(Qpbar)).

# Proof or Examples

## Expanded self-contained proof

### Rigorous proof

Choose a finite extension L/K over which rho becomes semistable; this is possible because rho is de Rham. The P-minus-dominance condition is unchanged after restriction. For any lift rho prime: Gal_K -> P(Qpbar), its restriction to Gal_L is a P-valued extension of the semistable representation rho restricted to Gal_L. Corollary 4 (cited lines 534-539), which is the semistable consequence of Theorem 8, therefore makes rho prime restricted to Gal_L semistable. Thus rho prime is potentially semistable, hence de Rham.

For the type assertion, Theorem 8(2)-(3) identifies the possible Weil-Deligne data in the semistable case, and Corollary 4 states the resulting parameter space as H^2(Gal_K,U(Qpbar)). Theorem 9 shows that the comparison used in Theorem 8 is equivariant under Gal(L/K) (lines 576-594), so the classification descends from L and is independent of the chosen semistabilizing extension. Corollary 6 then explicitly says that Theorem 8 and Corollary 4 remain valid with semistable replaced by de Rham (lines 595-598). The reduction from general finite K to Q_p is the non-abelian Shapiro equivalence recorded in lines 550-556. Consequently every P-valued extension is de Rham and its possible Weil-Deligne types are parameterized by the asserted H^2 set.

### Faithful quotation

It then proves in Theorem 9 (lines 576-594) that the comparison is compatible with the descent action, and states in Corollary 6 (lines 595-598): `Theorem 8 and Corollary 4 both hold after replacing semistable by de Rham.`
