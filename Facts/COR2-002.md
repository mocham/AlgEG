# Statement


Let $\mathbf G$ be a split connected reductive group over a field $k$, fix a maximal torus and positive roots, and let $\mathbf M\subsetneq\mathbf G$ be a Levi subgroup. Let $U^{\mathbf M}$ be the unipotent radical of the corresponding parabolic and let $\Phi^{\mathbf M+}$ be its roots. For $\gamma=\sum_{\delta}n_\delta\delta$, put $\operatorname{Ht}^{\mathbf M}(\gamma)=\sum_{\delta\notin\Delta_{\mathbf M}}n_\delta$, and let $U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge h}$ be the subgroup generated by the root groups $U_{\gamma^\vee}$ with $\operatorname{Ht}^{\mathbf M}(\gamma)\ge h$. Let $w\in N_{\mathbf M}(\mathbf T)$ and let $\overline\alpha=\langle w\rangle\alpha\subset\Phi^{\mathbf M+}$ be the orbit of a root of relative height $h$. In the graded quotient $\operatorname{gr}^{\mathbf M}_h U:=U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge h}/U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge h+1}$, define the relative-root module by $U_{\overline\alpha}:=\prod_{\gamma\in\overline\alpha}\overline U_{\gamma^\vee}$, where $\overline U_{\gamma^\vee}$ is the image of $U_{\gamma^\vee}$. This product is independent of order, is a smooth commutative unipotent subgroup of the graded quotient, and is stable under $w$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

A reflection in a simple root of $\mathbf M$ changes only the coefficients on $\Delta_{\mathbf M}$. Hence $w$ preserves $\operatorname{Ht}^{\mathbf M}$, so every root in $\overline\alpha$ has the same relative height $h$. The Chevalley commutator formula shows that a commutator between root groups of relative heights $r$ and $s$ has factors only in roots of relative height at least $r+s$. Thus the subgroups $U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge h}$ form a central filtration, and root groups of height $h$ commute modulo $U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge h+1}$ because $2h\ge h+1$.

Standard ordered-root factorization identifies $\operatorname{gr}^{\mathbf M}_h U$ with the direct product of the images of all root groups of relative height $h$. The factors indexed by $\overline\alpha$ therefore form a coordinate vector subgroup, so their product is a subgroup and does not depend on an ordering. Finally, $w$ permutes these factors, proving stability. The type-$G_2$ calculation in D04-007 shows why the same bare product need not be a subgroup before passing to the graded quotient.
