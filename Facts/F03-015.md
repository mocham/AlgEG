# Statement


Let $\fB$ be a directed graph. It is contractible if it has a vertex $v$ with no incoming edge and exactly one outgoing edge $v\to v'$. Deleting both $v$ and $v'$ gives the contraction $\fB-\{v,v'\}$. A directed bipartite graph is called a cycle if it is connected, nonempty, and not contractible.
