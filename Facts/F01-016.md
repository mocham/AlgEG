# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be a finite extension, let $R$ be a commutative finite-type $\bFp$-algebra, and let $M$ be an etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $\bfE_{F,R}$. Although $Z^i(M,-)$, $B^i(M,-)$, and $H^i(M,-)$ are generally only presheaves of $\cO_{\Spec R}$-modules on the big affine site over $\Spec R$, the degree-two Herr cohomology presheaf $H^2(M,-)$ is a coherent sheaf over $\Spec R$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Apply F09-009 with A=R (and coefficient exponent a=1). Its hypotheses hold because a finite-type F_p-algebra is Noetherian and countable, and M is finite projective by the definition of an etale (phi,Gamma)-module. The theorem represents the Herr complex by a perfect complex P of R-modules concentrated in degrees 0 through 2. Hence H^2(P)=H^2(M,R) is a finite R-module. For every finite-type R-algebra S, the base-change part of F09-009 gives H^2(M,S) = H^2(P tensor_R^L S) isomorphic to H^2(P) tensor_R S; the last equality holds because P has no cohomology above degree 2. Therefore the presheaf H^2(M,-) is the restriction to the big affine site of the quasi-coherent sheaf associated to the finite R-module H^2(M,R). Since R is Noetherian, that sheaf is coherent.
