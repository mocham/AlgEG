# Statement


Let $\fB$ be a directed bipartite graph. A bipartite matching is a collection of directed edges $\{\beta_i\to\alpha_i:i\in I\}$ such that the vertices occurring as the $\alpha_i$ and $\beta_i$ are all distinct; equivalently, $\{\alpha_i,\beta_i:i\in I\}$ has exactly $2\#I$ vertices.
