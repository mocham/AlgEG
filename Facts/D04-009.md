# Statement


Let A be a countable Noetherian Jacobson algebra over a finite field, let M be a projective etale (phi,Gamma)-module with A-coefficients, and let B be a finite-type A-algebra. Then the natural map C_Herr(M) tensor_A^L B to C_Herr(M_B) is a quasi-isomorphism, and H^2_Herr(M) tensor_A B is naturally isomorphic to H^2_Herr(M_B). In particular, if A is finite type over the countable algebraic closure of a finite field, both isomorphisms hold for every finite-type A-algebra B.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The perfectness part of COR8-006 applies to A and B because both are countable Noetherian coefficient algebras. Form the natural base-change map and its cone; both sides and the cone are perfect B-complexes. For every maximal ideal m of B, Jacobsonness of A and finite type of B over A imply that B/m is finite as an A-module. The finite-base-change case of COR8-006 identifies the source after derived tensor with B/m with C_Herr(M_{B/m}). Applying the same finite-base-change case over B identifies the target fibre with the identical complex. Thus every maximal residue-field fibre of the perfect cone is acyclic, so the cone is acyclic. The H^2 assertion follows because the Herr complex has no cohomology above degree two. A finite-type algebra over an algebraic closure of a finite field is countable, Noetherian, and Jacobson, giving the final specialization.
