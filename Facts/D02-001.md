# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be a finite extension, let $k$ be a finite field of characteristic $p$, and let $V$ be a finite-dimensional $k$-vector space with a continuous $k$-linear action of $G_F=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline F/F)$. Then $H^i(G_F,V)$ is finite-dimensional, it vanishes for $i>2$, and \[\sum_{i=0}^2(-1)^i\dim_k H^i(G_F,V)=-[F:\mathbb Q_p]\dim_k V.\]

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Apply Proposition \ref{prop: Herr complex Tate duality and Euler char} of the cited source to the etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module $M=\mathbb D_k(V)$. The source states that its Herr complex is perfect, concentrated in degrees $[0,2]$, and has Euler characteristic $-[F:\mathbb Q_p]\operatorname{rank}(M)$. The subsequent Herr-to-Galois comparison theorem identifies $H^i(\mathcal C^\bullet(M))$ functorially with $H^i(G_F,V)$. Since $\operatorname{rank}(M)=\dim_k V$, these statements give the asserted finiteness, vanishing, and Euler-characteristic identity.
