# Statement


Let $\bG$ be a split pinned reductive group with root system $\Phi$. Fix a standard Chevalley basis $\{e_\eta\}_{\eta\in\Phi}$ for $\Lie(\bG)$. For roots $\beta,\delta\in\Phi$ such that $\alpha=\beta+\delta\in\Phi$, define the integral root-system structure constant $N_{\beta,\delta}\in\mathbb Z$ by
\[
[e_\beta,e_\delta]=N_{\beta,\delta}e_\alpha.
\]
