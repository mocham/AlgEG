# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $\mathbf G$ be a split connected reductive group over $\overline{\mathbb F}_p$ with connected center and Coxeter number $h_{\mathbf G}$, and assume $p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Let $\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$ be a Borel subgroup of $\mathbf G$, let $\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}$ be its mod-$p$ Weil--Deligne stack, and for an irreducible component $C\subset\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}$ let $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}(C)$ denote the corresponding locus of the Borel-valued Emerton--Gee stack. Define $\Psi_2(C)$ to be the positive roots whose root characters are pointwise cyclotomic on $C$, and $\Psi_0(C)$ those whose root characters are pointwise trivial on $C$. Then $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}(C)$ contains the top-dimensional irreducible component
\[
\overline{\dot\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}\langle\Psi_0(C),\Psi_2(C)\rangle}.
\]
If a pair $\Psi=(\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$ is not $(\Psi_0(C),\Psi_2(C))$ for any irreducible component $C\subset\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}$, then $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}[U]\langle\Psi\rangle$ is nowhere dense in $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

F01-001 supplies the connected-center and Coxeter-bound hypotheses. Fix an irreducible component $C\subset\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}$ and put $\Psi(C)=(\Psi_0(C),\Psi_2(C))$. By COR10-005, the full-unipotent fine stratum attached to $\Psi(C)$ is smooth, and its map to the corresponding Weil--Deligne fine locus has geometrically irreducible affine-space fibers and induces a bijection on irreducible components. For a component-indexing pair, the cyclotomic roots are spanned by the simple roots that are cyclotomic on $C$, so
\[
\dim\operatorname{Span}_{\mathbb Q}\Psi_2(C)=\dim\operatorname{Span}_{\mathbb Q}(\Psi_2(C)\cap\Delta).
\]
The correction term in the COR10-005 dimension formula therefore vanishes, and the stratum is irreducible over the dense irreducible locus of $C$ of dimension $[F:\mathbb Q_p]\#\Phi^+$. The top-stratum purity statement of COR7-005 now identifies
\[
\overline{\dot{\mathcal X}_{\mathbf B}\langle\Psi_0(C),\Psi_2(C)\rangle}
\]
as a top-dimensional irreducible component of $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}(C)$.

Conversely, suppose that a full-unipotent fine stratum $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}[U]\langle\Psi\rangle$ is dense in an irreducible component of $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}$. Its generic Weil--Deligne image is then dense in an irreducible component $C\subset\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}$, and at the generic point its pointwise trivial and cyclotomic root sets are exactly $(\Psi_0(C),\Psi_2(C))$. Thus density forces $\Psi=(\Psi_0(C),\Psi_2(C))$. If $\Psi$ arises from no such $C$, its closure contains no irreducible component of $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}$, so the stratum is nowhere dense.
