# Statement


Let \(K/\mathbb Q_p\) be finite, let \({}^LB=U\rtimes{}^LT\) be a Borel Langlands dual group, let \(U_2=[U,U]\), and put
\[
\overline U=U/U_2=\prod_{\alpha\in\Delta}U_{\alpha^\vee}.
\]
Fix a \(B^-\)-dominant Hodge type \(\lambda\) and work on the weakly admissible fixed-type locus. The natural morphism of reduced rigid analytic Weil--Deligne stacks
\[
\mathrm{WD}_{K,{}^LB}^{L}(\lambda)
\longrightarrow
\mathrm{WD}_{K,\overline U\rtimes{}^LT}^{L}(\lambda)
\]
is an isomorphism. Under the fixed-type Borel Weil--Deligne morphism, the crystalline locus is the Cartesian inverse image of the closed zero-monodromy substack
\[
\mathrm{WD}_{K,\overline U\rtimes{}^LT}^{L,N=0}(\lambda)
\hookrightarrow
\mathrm{WD}_{K,\overline U\rtimes{}^LT}^{L}(\lambda).
\]
Thus the difference between potentially semistable and crystalline fixed-type Borel loci is entirely the \(N=0\) base change on the height-one truncation.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Lin25 Lemma 12 proves that the first morphism is a surjective closed immersion of reduced rigid analytic Artin stacks and hence an isomorphism. The weak admissibility condition is essential: it determines all higher-root monodromy data from the simple-root quotient.

Fontaine's criterion says that a potentially semistable representation is crystalline exactly when its Weil--Deligne monodromy is zero (with the fixed trivial crystalline inertial type). Through the displayed isomorphism, the full Borel monodromy is zero exactly when its height-one image has \(N=0\). Therefore imposing crystallinity on the fixed-type source is the Cartesian pullback of the closed \(N=0\) substack of the truncated target. The rank-one construction in the proof of Lin25 Theorem 11 explicitly defines the crystalline chart by the trivial-monodromy map, and Grob V13 records the same closed \(N=0\) specialization for integral fixed-type stacks.

No second upper-central-series argument is involved: Lin25 has already identified the full weakly admissible Weil--Deligne base with its height-one quotient before the \(N=0\) base change is made.
