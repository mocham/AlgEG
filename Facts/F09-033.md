# Statement


Let \(G\) be a connected reductive group over an algebraically closed field and let \(\Gamma\subset G\) be a subgroup.

The subgroup \(\Gamma\) is \(G\)-completely reducible if, whenever \(\Gamma\) is contained in a parabolic subgroup \(P\subset G\), it is contained in a Levi subgroup of \(P\). It is \(G\)-irreducible if it is not contained in any proper parabolic subgroup of \(G\). In particular, every \(G\)-irreducible subgroup is \(G\)-completely reducible.

Let \(\rho:\Pi\to G\) be any representation, choose a parabolic \(P=U^M\rtimes M\) minimal among parabolic subgroups containing \(\rho(\Pi)\), and let \(\rho_M:\Pi\to M\) be its Levi projection. Then \(\rho_M\) is \(M\)-irreducible. Equivalently, \(\rho\) is a \(U^M\)-extension of an \(M\)-irreducible, hence \(M\)-completely reducible, representation. Thus the definitions classify every non-\(G\)-irreducible representation by a proper minimal parabolic and an irreducible Levi projection.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The first two assertions are Lin22's definitions. A \(G\)-irreducible subgroup satisfies complete reducibility vacuously because no proper parabolic contains it, while the only remaining parabolic is \(G\) itself.

For the minimal-parabolic assertion, suppose that the Levi projection \(\rho_M(\Pi)\) were contained in a proper parabolic \(Q\subsetneq M\). The inverse image \(U^M\rtimes Q\) is then a proper parabolic subgroup of \(G\), strictly contained in \(P\), and contains \(\rho(\Pi)\). This contradicts minimality of \(P\). Hence \(\rho_M\) is \(M\)-irreducible. The factorization through \(U^M\rtimes M\) is exactly the asserted extension description.
