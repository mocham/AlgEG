# Statement


Let $\Phi$ be a reduced root system with positive roots $\Phi^+$ and simple roots $\Delta$, over $\bFp$ in the manuscript's standing range $p>h_\Phi$. For an integer $h$, let $\fB(\Phi^+,\Delta,h)$ be the directed bipartite graph on roots of heights $h$ and $h+1$, with $\beta\to\alpha$ when $\alpha-\beta\in\Delta$. Every induced subgraph $\fB\subset\fB(\Phi^+,\Delta,h)$ that contains every height-$h$ root is admissible: its discriminant variety $\fD_{\fB}\to\G_m^{\Delta_{\fB}}$ is smooth of relative dimension $\#\fB^{\src}-\#\fB^{\tar}$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let $T$ be the selected set of height-$(h+1)$ targets. By F03-018, it is enough to prove that the target-by-source Jacobian has full row rank everywhere over the simple-root torus. A source-leaf contraction eliminates one equation together with its leaf variable: the relevant coefficient is $N_{\beta,\alpha-\beta}x_{\alpha-\beta}$, which is a unit in the standing characteristic range. An isolated source contributes a free variable. Thus successive contractions preserve smoothness and the asserted relative dimension.

For types $A_n$, $B_n$, $C_n$, and $G_2$, COR12-001 records that every complete adjacent-height layer contracts to the empty graph. The same contraction order clears every target-induced subgraph: deleting targets can only turn the next source leaf into another leaf or an isolated source. Hence these cases are handled entirely by elimination.

For type $D_n$, no separate Type D theorem from the manuscript is needed. COR12-001 supplies the complete stable table of terminal cores, including the omitted alternating odd tails, and COR12-002 treats every target subset. It shows that the terminal graph is either empty or $K_{n,m}$ and, in the latter case, that
\[
\det J_{K_{n,m}}=\epsilon\,2x_{\alpha_{n-2m-1}}\prod_{i=n-m}^{n}x_{\alpha_i},\qquad \epsilon\in\{1,-1\}.
\]
This determinant is a unit because $p>h_{D_n}$, so every Type D graph is admissible uniformly in the rank.

For $F_4,E_6,E_7,E_8$, D11-002 exhausts all target subsets and supplies an invertible monomial maximal minor for every nonempty contracted core; its coefficients are units under $p>h_\Phi$. Consequently the exceptional graphs are admissible as well. Finally, for a reducible root system the graph, equations, and Jacobian split over the irreducible components. Smoothness is preserved under products and the relative dimensions add, proving the assertion for every reduced $\Phi$.
