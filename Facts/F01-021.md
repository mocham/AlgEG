# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be a finite extension and let $\chi$ be a one-dimensional continuous mod-$p$ representation of $\Gal_F$ over a field. Then its local Galois cohomology satisfies
\[
\dim H^1(\Gal_F,\chi)=\dim H^0(\Gal_F,\chi)+\dim H^2(\Gal_F,\chi)+[F:\Q_p].
\]

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let $k$ be the coefficient field of $\chi$. Under the standard meaning of a mod-$p$ representation, $k$ has characteristic $p$ and carries the discrete topology. The image of the continuous character $\chi:G_F\to k^\times$ is compact and discrete, hence finite. Its values are roots of unity, so they generate a finite subfield $k_0\subset k$, and $\chi$ is obtained by extension of scalars from a one-dimensional $k_0$-representation $\chi_0$. For every degree, the continuous cochain complex commutes with this field extension: writing locally constant cochains as a filtered colimit of cochains on finite quotients gives \[C^\bullet(G_F,\chi_0)\otimes_{k_0}k\cong C^\bullet(G_F,\chi).\] Since field extension is flat, $H^i(G_F,\chi_0)\otimes_{k_0}k\cong H^i(G_F,\chi)$. Applying D02-001 to the rank-one representation $\chi_0$ gives \[\dim H^0(G_F,\chi)-\dim H^1(G_F,\chi)+\dim H^2(G_F,\chi)=-[F:\mathbb Q_p].\] Rearranging proves the displayed formula.
