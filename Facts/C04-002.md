# Statement


Let $p=5$, $F=\mathbb Q_5$, $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_5$, and let $\mathbf G=\mathrm{GL}_{3,k}$ with upper-triangular Borel $\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$. Denote its positive-root subgroups by $U_{12}$, $U_{23}$, and $U_{13}$. Put $K=U_{12}U_{13}$. Let $\chi:G_F\to\mathbb F_5$ be the nonzero unramified additive character with $\chi(\operatorname{Frob}_F)=1$, and define $\bar\rho(g)=I+\chi(g)E_{12}$ with trivial torus component. Then $K$ is the minimal $\mathbf T$-stable normal subgroup of $U$ through which a $\mathbf B(k)$-conjugate of $\bar\rho$ factors. Although $K_2=[K,K]=1$ and the projected cocycle has $c_{13}=0$, the root $13$ is not removable: the product of the other root groups in $K$ is $U_{12}$, which is not normal in $U$. Thus the example lies outside a nonvanishing assertion restricted to removable height-one roots and demonstrates why that restriction is necessary.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The standing characteristic condition holds because the Coxeter number of $\mathrm{GL}_3$ is $3$ and $5>3$; its center is connected. The subgroup $K$ is $\mathbf T$-stable and abelian. It is normal in $U$ because the only relevant commutator is
\[
 [I+yE_{23},I+xE_{12}]=I-xyE_{13}\in U_{13}\subset K,
\]
and $U_{13}$ is central in $U$. The map $\chi$ is the continuous composite $G_F\twoheadrightarrow\widehat{\mathbb Z}\twoheadrightarrow\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z=\mathbb F_5$, and $E_{12}^2=0$, so $\bar\rho(gh)=\bar\rho(g)\bar\rho(h)$.

To check minimality, let $b=tu\in\mathbf B(k)$ and suppose $b\bar\rho b^{-1}$ factors through a $\mathbf T$-stable normal subgroup $L\subset U$. In the abelianization $U/[U,U]\cong U_{12}\times U_{23}$, conjugation by $u\in U$ is trivial, while conjugation by $t\in\mathbf T$ rescales the nonzero $U_{12}$ coordinate by an invertible scalar. Thus the conjugated image still has nonzero $U_{12}$ projection. The $\mathbf T$-weight decomposition forces $U_{12}\subset L$, and normality under $U_{23}$ then forces $U_{13}=[U_{23},U_{12}]\subset L$. Therefore $K\subset L$, while $K$ itself contains the image, proving minimality.

Finally $K/K_2=K$, so both roots $12$ and $13$ have $K$-height one, while $c_{12}=\chi$ and $c_{13}=0$. This does not permit the minimality argument to remove $U_{13}$: the remaining subgroup $U_{12}$ is not normal in $U$, precisely because $[U_{23},U_{12}]=U_{13}$. Hence root $13$ lies outside the removable-root scope of COR-001.

## Post-proof remarks

Reclassified after human review from a counterexample to an example of obvious source sloppiness. The group and cocycle calculations are correct, but the original counterexample interpretation ignored that deleting $U_{13}$ leaves $U_{12}$, which is not a $\mathbf T$-stable normal subgroup of the ambient $U$. The example therefore motivates, rather than contradicts, the corrected fork COR-001. Statement and proof were rechecked after reclassification.
