# Statement


In the setting of the rigid fixed-type component correspondence, let $\Phi^{\mathbf M}$ be the set of roots in the unipotent radical $U^{\mathbf M}$. The special fiber of $\mathcal X_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle w\rangle}^{\underline\lambda_w,\mathrm{tame}}$ is equidimensional of dimension $[F:\mathbb Q_p]\#\Phi^{\mathbf M}$. Its number of irreducible components is at least the number of irreducible components of the mod-$p$ Weil-Deligne stack $\mathcal W_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle w\rangle}$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put
\[
\mathfrak X=
\mathfrak X_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}^{\underline\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}},\mathrm{tame}},
\qquad
\mathfrak W=
\mathfrak W_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle},
\qquad
D=[F:\mathbb Q_p]\#\Phi^{\mathbf M}.
\]
Work throughout in the corrected split pinned, fixed-Tits-representative, elliptic-Weyl-image, \(P^-\)-dominant cyclotomic, orbit-normalizable setting of COR17-006, after descent to a complete coefficient DVR \(\mathcal O\).

There is an integral formal morphism
\[
f:\mathfrak X\longrightarrow\mathfrak W.
\]
Indeed, on an affine torus-normalizer coefficient chart, each universal relative-height-one extension class has the universal-coefficient edge map to
\(\operatorname{Tor}_1(H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}},-)\). The product of these edge maps is represented by the universal-coefficient formal Tor stack of COR13-003. The maps commute with change of root-coordinate trivialization and with the common prime-to-\(p\) orbit action; the effective quotient descent supplied there through COR16-003 therefore descends the chartwise maps to the displayed integral formal morphism. Its rigid generic fiber is the fixed-type deformation-to-Weil--Deligne morphism used in COR17-006, so it gives the componentwise-surjective bijection on generic irreducible components required below.

COR17-008 makes \(\mathfrak X\) Noetherian and \(\mathcal O\)-flat. COR17-007 gives finite type for its underlying reduction. Every integral component is horizontal, and COR17-006 gives every generic component dimension \(D\). The special fiber is the effective Cartier divisor cut out by the uniformizer. Applying F09-007 componentwise on a smooth formal atlas gives every special-fiber component dimension \(D\). Thus \(\mathfrak X_s\) is equidimensional of dimension \(D\).

The target \(\mathfrak W\) has the persistent integral label system required by COR19-001. Away from a cyclotomic component, top Herr cohomology vanishes and the label is the integral formal torus component. At a cyclotomic crossing the exponent-one universal-coefficient equation is
\[
s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}=0;
\]
its horizontal label is \(V(s_{\boldsymbol\alpha})\), and its Steinberg label is \(V(u_{\boldsymbol\alpha})\). Both are integral and flat in the generic and special fibers. For a product of crossings, the component labels are the maximal branch loci obtained by choosing horizontal or Steinberg equations and then decomposing simultaneous character fibers into torus translates. The relevant torus component groups and root-orbit descent groups have order prime to \(p\), so reduction preserves the maximal-label poset and effective quotient descent replaces labels by the same orbit set in both fibers.

All hypotheses of COR19-001 now hold for \(f\): the target labels persist, COR17-006 gives the componentwise-surjective generic bijection, every generic source component has dimension \(D\), and the special source is equidimensional of dimension \(D\). It follows that the reduced closure of each generic source component has a special-fiber component dominating the equally labelled component of \(\mathfrak W_s\), and distinct target labels give distinct ambient source components. Hence
\[
\operatorname{Irr}(\mathfrak W_s)\hookrightarrow
\operatorname{Irr}(\mathfrak X_s).
\]
COR13-003 identifies \(\mathfrak W_s\) with
\(\mathcal W_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}\), proving the asserted component lower bound together with the equidimensionality statement.

## Faithful GrobV14 proof

```tex
\begin{proof}
\EDITXIV{Put $d=[F:\Q_p]\#\Phi^{\bM}$.  For an integral component
$\sC$ of $\sW_{U^{\bM}\rtimes\bT\langle\wt w\rangle}$, consider
\[
\cX_{U^{\bM}\rtimes\bT\langle\wt w\rangle}
^{\underline\lambda_{\cyc},\tame}(\sC)
:=
\cX_{U^{\bM}\rtimes\bT\langle\wt w\rangle}
^{\underline\lambda_{\cyc},\tame}
\times_{\sW_{U^{\bM}\rtimes\bT\langle\wt w\rangle}}\sC.
\]
Its rigid generic fiber has a nonempty component of dimension $d$ by
Theorem~\ref{fact:COR17-006}.  Take its schematic closure $\cY$, with
its $p$-power torsion removed.  It is flat, and its special fiber is
nonempty by adic completeness.  Krull's Hauptidealsatz gives
\[
\dim(\cY\otimes\bar\F_p)=d.
\]
Componentwise specialization for this formal morphism shows that an
irreducible component of this special fiber maps onto
$C:=\sC\otimes\bar\F_p$, and that different $C$
give different components of the potentially semistable special fiber.
Applying the same closure and Krull argument to the whole stack gives
equidimensionality.  Definition~\ref{fact:COR13-003} identifies the
special fiber of the formal stack with the Weil--Deligne stack in the
statement.}
\end{proof}
```

## Post-proof remarks

Verified in exactly the corrected orbit-normalizable scope of COR17-006: \(\mathbf G\) is split and pinned, \(\mathbf M\) is the proper standard Levi, \(\dot w\) is the fixed Tits representative of an elliptic Weyl element in \(W_{\mathbf M}\), the Hodge type is the corrected \(P^-\)-dominant cyclotomic type, and every relative-height-one orbit is covered by orbit-normalizability. No unrestricted normalizer representative or arbitrary elliptic pair is included.

The former specialization gap is closed in this scope. The universal-coefficient edge maps give the integral formal morphism to the COR13-003 Tor model; the COR16-003 descent built into that theorem handles prime-to-\(p\) torus and orbit quotients. Integral torus labels, the two branches of each \(su=0\) crossing, and maximal labels in products persist under reduction, so COR19-001 supplies the special-component injection without assuming that closures over different target labels remain separate.
