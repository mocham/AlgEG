# Statement


Let $X=\Spec R$ be an affine scheme and let $F$ be a coherent sheaf on $X$ of projective dimension at most $1$. Then the functor on $R$-algebras
\[
S\longmapsto\Tor_1^R(F,S)
\]
is representable by a finite-type scheme over $\Spec R$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let the coherent sheaf correspond to the finitely presented `R`-module `F`. Choose a finite free surjection `P_0 -> F` and write `P_1` for its kernel. Finite presentation makes `P_1` finitely generated. Since `pd_R(F)<=1`, dimension shifting gives `Ext_R^1(P_1,N)=Ext_R^2(F,N)=0` for every `R`-module `N`; hence `P_1` is projective. Thus

`0 -> P_1 -> P_0 -> F -> 0`

is a resolution by finitely generated projective modules.

For every `R`-algebra `S`, the Tor exact sequence identifies

`Tor_1^R(F,S) = ker(P_1 tensor_R S -> P_0 tensor_R S)`

naturally in `S`. For a finitely generated projective module `P`, the functor `S |-> P tensor_R S` is represented by the vector-group scheme

`V(P)=Spec(Sym_R(P^vee))`.

The map `P_1 -> P_0` induces a morphism `V(P_1) -> V(P_0)`. Therefore the required kernel functor is represented by the fiber product

`V(P_1) x_{V(P_0)} Spec(R)`,

where `Spec(R) -> V(P_0)` is the zero section. Both vector-group schemes are affine of finite type over `R`, and the displayed fiber product is affine of finite type as well. This proves the claim.

## Faithful GrobV14 proof

```tex
\begin{proof}
Since $\operatorname{pd}_R(F) \le 1$, we have a projective resolution:
$$ 0 \to P_1 \xrightarrow{\phi} P_0 \to F \to 0. $$

For any $R$-algebra $S$, we tensor this sequence over $R$ with $S$. Since $P_0$ is projective, $\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(P_0, S) = 0$, which yields the exact sequence:
$$ 0 \to \operatorname{Tor}_1^R(F, S) \to P_1 \otimes_R S \xrightarrow{\phi \otimes \operatorname{id}_S} P_0 \otimes_R S \to F \otimes_R S \to 0 $$
From this, we can identify our functor exactly as the kernel of a map of projective modules:
$$ \mathcal{F}(S) = \operatorname{Tor}_1^R(F, S) \cong \ker(P_1 \otimes_R S \to P_0 \otimes_R S). $$

We now show this kernel is representable. For any finitely generated projective $R$-module $P$, the functor $S \mapsto P \otimes_R S$ is represented by the affine group scheme $\mathbb{V}(P^*) = \operatorname{Spec}(\operatorname{Sym}_R(P^*))$, where $P^* = \operatorname{Hom}_R(P, R)$ is the dual module.
Let $W_i = \operatorname{Spec}(\operatorname{Sym}_R(P_i^*))$ for $i = 0, 1$. The $R$-module homomorphism $\phi: P_1 \to P_0$ induces a natural transformation of functors $W_1 \to W_0$.

The presheaf $\mathcal{F}$ is the kernel of the map $W_1 \to W_0$ in the category of abelian group-valued functors. \EDITXIV{This kernel is the fiber product over the identity section:}
$$ \mathcal{F} \cong W_1 \times_{W_0} \{0\} $$
where $\{0\} \cong \operatorname{Spec}(R)$ is the zero-section of the group scheme $W_0$. 
\end{proof}
```
