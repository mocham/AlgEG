# Statement


Let $\mathbf G$ be a connected reductive group with Borel subgroup $\mathbf B=\mathbf T U$, positive roots $\Phi^+$, and simple roots $\Delta$. Assume the truncated logarithm is defined, and let $u\in U$ satisfy $\log_{\leq h_{\mathbf G}}u=\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi^+}u_\alpha$ with $u_\delta\neq0$ for every $\delta\in\Delta$. Then the centralizer $Z_{\mathbf G}(u)$ is contained in $\mathbf B$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

### Proof

Let z be a geometric point of Z_G(u), and write its Bruhat decomposition uniquely as z=b dot(w) v with b in B and v in U_w:=U intersect dot(w)^{-1}U^-dot(w). From z^{-1}uz=u one obtains

dot(w)^{-1}(b^{-1}ub)dot(w)=vuv^{-1}.

Set u_1=b^{-1}ub and u_2=vuv^{-1}. Both lie in U. Conjugation by the unipotent part of b acts trivially on U/[U,U], while its torus part acts on each simple-root quotient by a nonzero scalar. Hence every simple-root component of log(u_1) remains nonzero. The truncated logarithm is conjugation-equivariant, so the displayed equality gives

Ad(dot(w)^{-1})log(u_1)=log(u_2) in Lie(U).

If w is not the identity, the Weyl-group descent property gives a simple root delta with w^{-1}(delta) negative. The nonzero delta-component of log(u_1) is carried to a nonzero component in the negative root space g_{w^{-1}(delta)}. No other root component can cancel it because w permutes root spaces injectively. This contradicts the fact that log(u_2) lies in Lie(U), the sum of the positive root spaces. Thus w=1. Then U_w is trivial, so z=b lies in B. Therefore Z_G(u) is contained in B.

## Faithful GrobV14 proof

```tex
\begin{proof}
Let $z=b\dot wv\in Z_{\bG}(u)$ be its Bruhat decomposition, with
$b\in\bB$ and $v\in U_w$.  Then
\[
\dot w^{-1}(b^{-1}ub)\dot w=vuv^{-1}.
\]
Conjugation by $b$ preserves $U$ and acts by a nonzero scalar on each
simple-root quotient of $U/[U,U]$.  Thus every simple-root component of
$\log_{\le h_{\bG}}(b^{-1}ub)$ is nonzero, whereas the right hand side
has logarithm in $\operatorname{Lie}U$.  If $w\ne1$, there is a simple
root $\delta$ such that $w^{-1}(\delta)$ is negative.  Its nonzero
component is then carried to $\mathfrak g_{w^{-1}(\delta)}$, a
contradiction.  Hence $w=1$ and $z\in\bB$.
\end{proof}
```
