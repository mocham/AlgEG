# Statement


Let $F=\mathbb Q_5$, let $\mathbf G=\mathrm{GL}_2$ with diagonal torus $\mathbf T$, take the proper Levi $\mathbf M=\mathbf T$, and let the Weyl element be $w=1$ of order $n=1$. Put $\lambda(z)=\operatorname{diag}(z^{-1},1)$ and choose $\sigma\in G_F$ with $\chi_{\mathrm{LT}}(\sigma)\ne1$. If the twisted norm rule is evaluated on presentations $g\sigma^k$ with arbitrary $k\in\mathbb Z$, then the two presentations $1=1\sigma^0=(\sigma^{-1})\sigma^1$ receive different values. Thus a well-defined corrected construction must use unique residue-class representatives and impose compatibility between the subgroup norm of $\sigma^n$ and the chosen lift of $w^n$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

A torus is a proper Levi in $\mathrm{GL}_2$, and the identity Weyl element is elliptic in its rank-zero root system. Since $F_1=F$, every $\sigma\in G_F$ lifts the generator of the trivial quotient. Choose one with $u=\chi_{\mathrm{LT}}(\sigma)\ne1$. For $n=1$ and $w=1$, the unrestricted formula reads
\[
N_1(g\sigma^k)=\lambda(\chi_{\mathrm{LT}}(g)).
\]
The presentation $1=1\sigma^0$ gives $1$, while $1=(\sigma^{-1})\sigma^1$ gives $\lambda(u^{-1})=\operatorname{diag}(u,1)\ne1$. Equivalently, if $\psi=\lambda\circ\chi_{\mathrm{LT}}$, then $\psi(\sigma^1)\ne1=w^1$, so the descent compatibility in COR1-002 fails. Under the corrected convention for $n=1$, the only allowed exponent is $k=0$ and every element is evaluated directly as an element of $H=G_F$; the resulting map is simply the Lubin-Tate character composed with $\lambda$ and is a representation.
