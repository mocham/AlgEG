# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let $\mathbf G$ be a split pinned connected reductive group with connected center and $p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Fix the pinned maximal torus $\mathbf T$ and positive system. Let $\mathbf M\subsetneq\mathbf G$ be a proper standard Levi containing $\mathbf T$, let $U^{\mathbf M}$ be the unipotent radical of its standard parabolic, and let $\dot w\in N_{\mathbf M}(\mathbf T)$ be the fixed Tits representative whose Weyl image
$$w\in W_{\mathbf M}=N_{\mathbf M}(\mathbf T)/\mathbf T$$
is elliptic in $\mathbf M$. Assume the corrected $P^-$-dominant cyclotomic Hodge type $\underline\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}}$ and the orbit-normalizability hypothesis of COR17-006. Let $\Phi^{\mathbf M}$ be the roots in $U^{\mathbf M}$ and put
$$d=[F:\mathbb Q_p]\#\Phi^{\mathbf M}.$$

Assume every irreducible component of
$$\mathcal X_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}$$
has dimension $d$. If the number of its irreducible components does not exceed the number of irreducible components of
$$\mathcal W_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle},$$
then every $\overline{\mathbb F}_p$-point of $\mathcal X_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}$ has a potentially semistable lift of the corrected regular cyclotomic Hodge type $\underline\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}}$ and tame inertial type.

Both hypotheses hold, only within this split pinned, proper-standard-Levi, fixed-Tits-representative, elliptic-Weyl-image, orbit-normalizable setup, in each of the following cases:

1. $\mathbf G$ and the elliptic Weyl image $w$ are otherwise arbitrary and $[F:\mathbb Q_p]\geq\#\Phi^++2$.
2. $\mathbf G$ has type $F_4$ and $w\neq1$.
3. $\mathbf G$ has type $F_4$ and $F\neq\mathbb Q_p$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put
\[
X=\mathcal X_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle},
\qquad
W=\mathcal W_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle},
\]
and let \(Z\) be the special fiber of the corrected fixed-type potentially semistable stack in the orbit-normalizable setting of COR17-006. By F05-009, for every irreducible component \(C\) of \(W\) there is a closed irreducible component \(Y_C\) of \(Z\), of dimension \(d\), which maps onto \(C\); choices for distinct \(C\) lie in distinct irreducible components \(X_C\) of \(X\). Hence
\[
\#\operatorname{Irr}(X)\geq\#\operatorname{Irr}(W).
\]

The assumed reverse inequality gives equality, so the components \(X_C\) exhaust \(\operatorname{Irr}(X)\). By the expected-dimension hypothesis, \(Y_C\subset X_C\) is a closed irreducible subset of the same dimension as \(X_C\). The reduced stack \(X\) is finite type by the finite-presentation input used in F05-009, so a proper closed irreducible subset of an irreducible component has smaller dimension. Therefore \(Y_C=X_C\) for every \(C\). Thus \(Z\) and \(X\) have the same underlying support. Every geometric point of \(X\) consequently belongs to the corrected fixed-type special fiber and has a potentially semistable lift of the stated cyclotomic Hodge type and tame inertial type.

It remains to check the two hypotheses in the listed cases. COR15-004 gives the corrected large-degree Borel component calculation at \([F:\mathbb Q_p]\geq\#\Phi^++2\). COR18-001 gives the corrected type-$F_4$ Borel classification, with the exceptional loci indexed by all Serre-component realizations of the four row types rather than by four individual components. COR18-003 supplies the corresponding twisted expected-dimension and generic-regularity calculation for the fixed Tits representative \(\dot w\) whose Weyl image is elliptic in \(\mathbf M\). Combining those upper bounds and dimension statements with the lower bound of F05-009 proves both hypotheses in each listed case, under the setup imposed in the Statement.
