# Statement


Let $p=5$, $F=\mathbb Q_5$, $\mathbf G=\mathrm{GL}_4$ with diagonal torus $\mathbf T$ and upper Borel, and let $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$ be the standard simple roots. Let $\mathbf M=\mathrm{GL}_2\times\mathrm{GL}_1\times\mathrm{GL}_1$ be the standard proper Levi for $\alpha_1$, let $w$ be the order-two permutation matrix swapping the first two basis vectors, and put $\mathbf K=U_{\alpha_3}$. Then $\mathbf K\subset U^{\mathbf M}$ is $\mathbf T\langle w\rangle$-stable and has one relative root, whereas $U^{\mathbf M}$ has five positive root groups. Assume that the unique rootwise stable-section object in the recursive construction is a genuine $U_{\alpha_3}$-torsor. The one-stage fiber then has dimension $\dim\mathbf T+1=5$, while the full $U^{\mathbf M}$ fiber at the completed full-unipotent stage has dimension $\dim\mathbf T+5=9$. This illustrates the distinction between an intermediate stage and the full-unipotent stage.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The positive roots outside the Levi root system generated by $\alpha_1$ are
\[
\alpha_2,\quad\alpha_3,\quad\alpha_1+\alpha_2,\quad\alpha_2+\alpha_3,\quad\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_3,
\]
so $\dim U^{\mathbf M}=5$. Since $\alpha_1$ and $\alpha_3$ are orthogonal, $s_{\alpha_1}$ fixes $\alpha_3$, and conjugation by $w$ preserves $U_{\alpha_3}$. Thus $\mathbf K=U_{\alpha_3}$ is an allowed stable one-root subgroup. For $F=\mathbb Q_5$, one has $k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}=\mathbb F_5$, so the relevant Weil restrictions do not change dimensions. COR3-002 gives the accumulated one-stage group $U_{\alpha_3}\rtimes\mathbf T$, of dimension $1+4=5$, and gives $U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T$ only at the full final stage, of dimension $5+4=9$. The calculation concerns the formal fiber identification conditional on the genuine-torsor input; it makes no claim that the source has proved that separate input.
