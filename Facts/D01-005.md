# Statement


Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p>0$, let $G/k$ be a connected reductive group, and let $U\subset G(k)$ be a finite $p$-subgroup. Then there is a parabolic subgroup $P\subset G$ such that $U\subset R_u(P)(k)$ and $N_{G(k)}(U)\subset P(k)$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Every element of the finite $p$-group $U$ is unipotent in characteristic $p$. The asserted parabolic is the specialization of Borel-Tits, Corollaire 3.9 used in Lin22: that result places a unipotent subgroup in the unipotent radical of a parabolic whose rational-point normalizer contains no elements outside the parabolic.
