# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $\mathcal O$ be the ring of integers of a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_p$ with residue field $k$, and let $G/\mathcal O$ be a smooth affine group scheme such that $G^0$ is reductive and $G/G^0$ is finite. For every continuous $\bar\rho:G_F\to G(k)$, the universal framed deformation ring $R_{\bar\rho}^{\square}$ is a local complete intersection, flat over $\mathcal O$, of relative dimension $\dim(G_k)([F:\mathbb Q_p]+1)$. In particular, the associated derived framed Galois deformation ring is homotopy discrete, so its derived deformation functor is the left Kan extension of its classical restriction to Artinian local coefficient rings.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Paškūnas-Quast Theorem 1.1 gives the local-complete-intersection, flatness, and dimension assertions. Their obstruction-theoretic presentation has $r$ tangent variables and $s=\dim_kH^2(G_F,\operatorname{ad}\bar\rho)$ equations; the local Euler-Poincare formula gives $r-s=\dim(G_k)([F:\mathbb Q_p]+1)$, and the proved dimension equality makes the uniformizer together with the $s$ equations a regular sequence. The same source explicitly applies Galatius-Venkatesh Lemma 7.5 to conclude that the derived deformation ring is homotopy discrete. For a homotopy-discrete prorepresenting ring, evaluation on animated Artinian local rings is recovered by left Kan extension from the ordinary Artinian deformation functor, giving the final formulation.
