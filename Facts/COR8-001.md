# Statement


Let \(F/\mathbb Q_p\) be finite and let \(\mathbf G\) be a connected reductive group with connected center and \(p>h_{\mathbf G}\). Let \(\mathbf B\) be a Borel subgroup, write \(\Phi^+=\Phi^+(\mathbf G,\mathbf B)\) for the corresponding set of positive roots, let \(\mathcal X_{\mathbf G,\mathrm{red}}^{\mathrm{EG}}\) be the reduced Emerton--Gee stack, and let \(\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}\) be the mod-\(p\) Borel-valued Weil--Deligne stack. Assume either \([F:\mathbb Q_p]\geq\#\Phi^++2\), with \(\mathbf G\) arbitrary, or \(\mathbf G\) has type \(F_4\) and \(F\neq\mathbb Q_p\).

Then \(\mathcal X_{\mathbf G,\mathrm{red}}^{\mathrm{EG}}\) is equidimensional of dimension \([F:\mathbb Q_p]\#\Phi^+\), and its irreducible components are in bijection with the irreducible components of \(\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}\). The component indexed by \(C\in\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal W_{\mathbf B})\) is the closure of the reductive image of the corresponding full maximally nonsplit Borel locus.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put \(D=[F:\mathbb Q_p]\#\Phi^+\). For each irreducible component \(C\subset\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}\), choose pairwise disjoint component-generic opens
\[
C^\circ\subset C\setminus\bigcup_{C'\ne C}C'.
\]
COR15-003 identifies the corresponding full-unipotent fine stratum as the component-indexing top Borel stratum and makes every non-indexing full-unipotent pair nowhere dense. Restrict its regular maximally nonsplit locus over \(C^\circ\) to an irreducible dense open \(L_C^\circ\). The fixed-type dimension input F09-004 and COR7-001 give dimension \(D\), while COR5-001 gives zero-dimensional, and component-generically singleton, geometric fibers over its reductive image. Thus the closure \(Y_C\) of that image has dimension \(D\).

COR19-002 supplies the formerly missing global step. Its finite disjoint-union scheme-image coverage, combined with the corrected dimension inputs in the present numerical ranges, proves that every irreducible reductive component is some \(Y_C\), and at the same time proves that the ambient reductive stack is equidimensional of dimension \(D\). No prior ambient equidimensionality or top-component lemma is used.

It remains only to record distinctness of the indexing. Suppose \(Y_C=Y_{C'}\) for \(C\ne C'\). The constructible images of \(L_C^\circ\) and \(L_{C'}^\circ\) contain dense opens of the same irreducible reductive component and therefore meet at a component-generic geometric point \(y\). Choose Borel-valued preimages \(x\in L_C^\circ\) and \(x'\in L_{C'}^\circ\). COR5-001 embeds the geometric fiber in
\[
Z_{\mathbf G}(y)/Z_{\mathbf B}(y)=\{1\},
\]
so \(x=x'\). Its Weil--Deligne label would lie in both disjoint opens \(C^\circ\) and \((C')^\circ\), a contradiction. This argument uses COR15-003 before applying the singleton-fiber result and never assigns a component label at a crossing. Consequently \(C\mapsto Y_C\) is the asserted bijection.

```tex
\begin{proof}
\label{fact:COR19-002}
\EDITXIV{Put $X=\cX_{\bG,\red}^{\EG}$.  There are finitely many standard
Levi subgroups and elliptic classes in their Weyl groups.  Given
$x\in X(\bFp)$, choose a parabolic $U^{\bM}\rtimes\bM$ minimal among
those containing the corresponding Galois image.  The Levi projection is
$\bM$-irreducible, and Theorem~\ref{fact:COR6-004} conjugates it into
$\bT\langle\wt w\rangle$ for an elliptic $w\in W_{\bM}$.  Hence the
reduced scheme-theoretic image of the morphism from the finite disjoint union
\[
\coprod_{(\bM,w)}
\cX_{U^{\bM}\rtimes\bT\langle\wt w\rangle,\red}^{\EG}
\longrightarrow X
\]
is $X$, so one irreducible component of one of these stacks maps densely
to each irreducible component of $X$.  Put $D=[F:\Q_p]\#\Phi^+$.
Theorems~\ref{fact:COR15-004},
\ref{fact:COR18-001}, and~\ref{fact:COR18-003}, together with
Proposition~\ref{fact:COR8-002} in type $\mathrm F_4$, show that only the
full maximally nonsplit Borel loci can map densely to components in the
two cases of the proposition, and that their dimension is $D$.

For each $C\in\operatorname{Irr}(\cW_{\bB})$, define the maximally
nonsplit locus over $C$ by the fiber product
\[
\dot\cX_{\bB}(C):=
\dot\cX_{\bB}\langle\Psi_0(C),\Psi_2(C)\rangle
\times_{\cW_{\bB}}C.
\]
Corollary~\ref{fact:COR15-003} makes this locus irreducible and dominant
over $C$, while Lemma~\ref{fact:COR5-001} gives image dimension $D$.
The finite argument above gives all irreducible components, and applying
the same lemma over disjoint dense opens of distinct $C$ shows that their
closures are distinct.}
\end{proof}
```

## Post-proof remarks
