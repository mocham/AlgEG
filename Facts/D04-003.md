# Statement


Let $\mathbb F$ be a finite field of characteristic $p$, let $\mathcal G$ be a split connected reductive group scheme over $W(\mathbb F)$, and write $G=\mathcal G_{\mathbb F}$. If $T\subset G$ is a maximal torus defined over $\mathbb F$, then there is a maximal torus $\mathcal T\subset\mathcal G$ over $W(\mathbb F)$ whose special fiber is $T$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Choose a finite extension $\mathbb F'/\mathbb F$ that splits $T$. The extension $W(\mathbb F')/W(\mathbb F)$ is finite etale. Descend the split torus $\mathbb G_m^{\operatorname{rank}T}$ over $W(\mathbb F')$ using the same finite Galois action on its character lattice that descends $T_{\mathbb F'}$ to $T$. This gives a torus $\widetilde T$ over $W(\mathbb F)$ whose special fiber is $T$. Apply S05-001 to the closed immersion $T\hookrightarrow G$: it lifts to a closed immersion $\widetilde T\hookrightarrow\mathcal G$. Its image $\mathcal T$ is a torus of the same rank as the maximal torus $T$. Since reductive-group rank is constant under passage to the special fiber, $\mathcal T$ has maximal possible rank in $\mathcal G$ and is therefore a maximal torus. Its special fiber is the original $T$.
