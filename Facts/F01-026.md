# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be a finite extension, let $\bG$ be a connected reductive group with connected center, assume $p>h_{\bG}$ where $h_{\bG}$ is its Coxeter number, and let $\bB$ be a Borel subgroup. In the Herr-cohomological construction of the Borel-valued Emerton--Gee stack, the stackiness comes from the $H^0$ terms. Replacing $C^\bullet_{\Herr}$ by the modified complex $C^\bullet_{\Herr,+}$, whose degree-zero cohomology vanishes, uniformly eliminates this stackiness.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

For a linear cocycle groupoid presented by a cochain complex $C^0\xrightarrow{d^0}C^1\xrightarrow{d^1}C^2$, the automorphisms of a one-cocycle are the degree-zero cochains in $\ker(d^0)=H^0$. Thus the stabilizer contribution called stackiness in the source is exactly the $H^0$ term. F01-023 augments the degree-zero differential by the constant-term coordinate in the unramified case, while retaining the ordinary differential in the ramified case. COR1-001 proves, for the universal unramified-twist root families used here, that the resulting complex satisfies $H^0_{\mathrm{Herr},+}=0$. Replacing each rank-one root-coordinate Herr groupoid in the recursive Borel construction by this modified groupoid therefore removes these degree-zero stabilizers uniformly. This conclusion concerns the $H^0$ stabilizers identified in the Statement; it does not assert removal of unrelated global stabilizers.
