# Statement


Let $\bB=U\rtimes\bT$ be a split Borel and let $\Phi^+$ be its positive-root set, with root group $U_{\alpha^\vee}\subset U$ attached to $\alpha\in\Phi^+$. For $S\subset\Phi^+$, define $U_S:=\prod_{\alpha\in S}U_{\alpha^\vee}$ using a chosen ordering on $S$, and regard it as the corresponding subquotient of $U$ whenever possible. Fix a $\bT$-stable normal subgroup $K=U_{\Phi_K}\subset U$, equip $\Phi_K$ with its $K$-height $\Ht_K$, and fix a total order $\leq_K$ compatible with $\Ht_K$. For $\alpha\in\Phi_K$, define
\[
\Phi_{K,\leq\alpha}:=\{\beta\in\Phi_K:\beta\leq_K\alpha\},\qquad
K_{\leq\alpha}:=U_{\Phi_{K,\leq\alpha}},\qquad
\tr_hK:=U_{\{\beta\in\Phi_K:\Ht_K(\beta)\leq h\}}.
\]
The notation $\alpha>_K\beta$ means that $\alpha$ is greater than $\beta$ in the fixed total order.
