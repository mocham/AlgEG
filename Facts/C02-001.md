# Statement


Let $p=5$, $F=\mathbb Q_5$, $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_5$, and $\mathbf G=\mathrm{GL}_{2,k}$ with diagonal torus $\mathbf T$ and upper-unipotent root subgroup $K=U_\alpha$. Let $c:G_F\to\mathbb F_5$ be the continuous unramified additive character sending arithmetic Frobenius to $1$, and put $\rho(g)=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&c(g)\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. The torus character is trivial, so the positive-root stabilizer subgroup is $H_0=U_\alpha=K$ and its effective quotient $H_0/(H_0\cap K)$ is trivial. On the other hand, for $g$ with $c(g)=1$ and $\ell=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\in U_{-\alpha}(\mathbb F_5)$, one has $\ell\rho(g)\ell^{-1}=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\-1&2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, which is not upper triangular. Thus negative-root conjugation does not act on this fine Borel stratum, while the effective positive-root stabilizer prescription correctly produces no extra quotient dimension.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The two fine-configuration closure implications are vacuous in type $A_1$. The trivial root character gives $H^0(G_F,\mathbb F_5)=\mathbb F_5$, while local Tate duality gives $H^2(G_F,\mathbb F_5)\cong H^0(G_F,\mathbb F_5(1))^\vee=0$ because the mod-$5$ cyclotomic character of $G_{\mathbb Q_5}$ has image of order $4$. The character $c$ factors through $G_F\twoheadrightarrow\widehat{\mathbb Z}\twoheadrightarrow\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z$, so it is continuous. It is a nonzero cocycle for the trivial action, whose coboundaries vanish. Since the only unipotent root-subgroup choices are $1$ and $U_\alpha$, the representation lies in the minimal-$K$ fine stratum.

For $u=\rho(g)=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, one has $\ell^{-1}=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&0\\-1&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and direct multiplication over $\mathbb F_5$ gives $\ell u=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and $\ell u\ell^{-1}=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\-1&2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Its lower-left entry is nonzero, so negative-root conjugation leaves the Borel-valued representation locus. In contrast, the positive root group equals $K$ and therefore contributes nothing after passing to $H_0/(H_0\cap K)$.
