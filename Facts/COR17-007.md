# Statement


Let \(F/\mathbb Q_p\) be an arbitrary finite extension, let \(G\) be an arbitrary reductive group over \(F\) that splits over a finite extension \(L/F\), and let
\[
{}^LP=\check P\rtimes\operatorname{Gal}(L/F)\subset {}^LG
\]
be a parabolic subgroup with Levi factor \({}^LM\). Then the parabolic Emerton--Gee stack admits an ind-presentation
\[
\mathcal X_{F,{}^LP}\cong\varinjlim_m\mathcal X_{F,{}^LP}^{m},
\]
where every \(\mathcal X_{F,{}^LP}^{m}\) is an algebraic stack of finite presentation over \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z_p\) and every transition morphism is a nilpotent thickening. In particular, all transition morphisms induce isomorphisms on underlying reduced substacks, and
\[
\mathcal X_{F,{}^LP,\mathrm{red}}\cong
\bigl(\mathcal X_{F,{}^LP}^{m}\bigr)_{\mathrm{red}}
\]
is an algebraic stack of finite type. The same holds for the Levi stack, and the induced morphism
\[
\mathcal X_{F,{}^LP,\mathrm{red}}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal X_{F,{}^LM,\mathrm{red}}
\]
is representable by algebraic stacks and of finite type.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The ind-presentation is Theorem \(\ref{thm:rep-parabolic}\) of the source. The introduction states the same result as part (3) of Theorem \(\ref{thm:main}\).

The proof begins with the formal algebraicity and limit-preservation of \(\mathcal X_{F,{}^LP}\). The preceding parabolic and reductive representability corollaries make the stack quasi-compact, and its diagonal is affine. The source then applies the ind-algebraicity criterion cited as Stacks Project, Tag 0AJE. The resulting algebraic levels are of finite presentation and the transition maps are nilpotent thickenings, exactly as asserted in the theorem.

A nilpotent thickening induces an isomorphism after reduction. Hence the reductions of all levels in the displayed ind-system are canonically identified, and reducing the colimit gives the reduction of any level. Since a reduced closed substack of an algebraic stack of finite presentation over \(\mathbb Z_p\) is of finite type, \(\mathcal X_{F,{}^LP,\mathrm{red}}\) is of finite type. This is also the consequence used in `unobs`, Lemma 9, where the source theorem is cited to deduce quasi-compactness of the reduced parabolic Emerton--Gee stack. Applying the same result to the Levi gives a finite-type algebraic reduced Levi stack. A morphism between finite-type algebraic stacks is representable by algebraic stacks and of finite type, which proves the reduced relative assertion.

In the introduction, an ``algebraic stack over \(\operatorname{Spf}\mathbb Z_p\)'' is defined to mean an algebraic stack over \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z/p^a\) for some \(a>0\). The detailed parabolic theorem writes \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z_p\); the displayed ind-presentation above follows that detailed statement.

## Post-proof remarks

Verified against arXiv:2304.05317v2, lines 264--327 and 2391--2442 of the authorized TeX source. The nilpotent transitions preserve the reduction, so the finite-presentation levels imply that the underlying reduced parabolic and Levi stacks are finite-type algebraic stacks. Their induced morphism is therefore representable by algebraic stacks and of finite type. This is the finite-type input needed for component and dimension arguments on the special fiber.

The warning in source lines 2423--2442 concerns the morphism between the full nonreduced ind-algebraic stacks: the author does not prove that \(\mathcal X_{F,{}^LP}\to\mathcal X_{F,{}^LM}\) is relatively representable by algebraic stacks of finite presentation before reduction. It does not obstruct the finite-type assertion for the reduced morphism.
