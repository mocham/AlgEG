# Statement


Let $k$ be a field and let $X=\operatorname{Spec}k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(f_1,\ldots,f_c)$. If the Jacobian matrix $(\partial f_i/\partial x_j)$ has rank at least $c-r$ at every geometric point of $X$, then every irreducible component of $X$ has dimension at most $n-c+r$. More generally, the same conclusion holds fiberwise for a family admitting such a presentation on each fiber when $r$ is a uniform upper bound for the Jacobian corank on that fiber. This rank condition alone does not imply that a fiber is irreducible.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

At a geometric point $x$, the Zariski tangent space is the kernel of the Jacobian map $k(x)^n\to k(x)^c$, so

$$\dim T_xX=n-\operatorname{rank}J(x)\le n-c+r.$$

The local Krull dimension at $x$ is at most the embedding dimension $\dim T_xX$. Taking a closed geometric point on each irreducible component gives the stated component-dimension bound. Applying the same argument after base change to each geometric fiber proves the relative formulation when the corank bound is uniform on that fiber. Irreducibility does not follow: D01-011 gives the smooth full-rank hypersurface $x^2-1=0$ with two geometric components.
