# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, put $d_F=[F:\mathbb Q_p]$, and let $(G,B,T)$ be split pinned reductive with dual pinned group $(\mathbf G,\mathbf B,\mathbf T)$; assume $p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Let $\Phi^+=\Phi(B,T)^+$ and let $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}=\mathcal X_{\mathbf B,\overline{\mathbb F}_p}^{\mathrm{EG}}\times_{\mathcal X_{\mathbf T,\overline{\mathbb F}_p}^{\mathrm{EG}}}\mathcal X_{\mathbf T,\mathrm{red}}^{\mathrm{EG}}$. Every irreducible component of $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}$ has dimension at least $d_F\#\Phi^+$. If $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}$ admits a finite stratification by locally closed substacks, each of dimension at most $d_F\#\Phi^+$, then its irreducible components are precisely the closures of the dimension-$d_F\#\Phi^+$ irreducible components of the strata. Equivalently, after refining the stratification into irreducible locally closed strata, they are precisely the closures of the top-dimensional strata.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put $r=\#\Phi^+$, put $d_k=[k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}:\mathbb F_p]$, and set $D=d_Fr$. The torus chart has irreducible components of dimension $\dim(\mathbf T)$. Fix a height-compatible ordering of the positive roots. Applying the Verified dimension-growth theorem F02-026 once at each of the $r$ root steps shows that every irreducible component of the final chart $X_{\mathbf B}$ has dimension at least
\[\dim(\mathbf T)+(d_F+d_k)r.\]
The Verified theorem COR9-003 identifies this final chart, independently of the ordering within every completed-height layer, as a smooth surjective affine atlas of $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}$ whose geometric two-fiber is $\operatorname{Res}_{k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}/\mathbb F_p}U\rtimes\mathbf T$. This fiber is pure of dimension
\[e=\dim(\mathbf T)+d_kr.\]
For every irreducible component of $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}$, smooth surjectivity supplies an irreducible component of $X_{\mathbf B}$ dominating it, and the smooth relative-dimension formula subtracts $e$. Thus every irreducible component of $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}$ has dimension at least
\[\dim(\mathbf T)+(d_F+d_k)r-e=d_Fr=D.\]

Now assume the finite stratification. Refining each locally closed stratum into its finitely many irreducible components gives a finite stratification by irreducible locally closed substacks, all of dimension at most $D$. Hence $\dim\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}\le D$. Together with the first paragraph, every irreducible component of $\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}$ has dimension exactly $D$. The generic point of each ambient irreducible component lies in one refined stratum; the closure of that stratum is the ambient component and the stratum has dimension $D$. Conversely, the closure of any dimension-$D$ irreducible stratum is an irreducible closed subset of maximal ambient dimension and therefore is an irreducible component. This proves both formulations.
