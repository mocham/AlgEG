# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, let $\bG$ be a connected reductive group with connected center and Coxeter number $h_{\bG}$, and assume $p>h_{\bG}$. Put $\dkF=[k_{F_{\cyc}}:\F_p]$, let $\bB=U\rtimes\bT$ be a split Borel subgroup of $\bG$ with positive roots $\Phi^+$, and let $(K,\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$ be a fine configuration. Write $\Phi_K$ for the roots of $K$, $\dot X_{K\rtimes\bT}\langle\Psi\rangle$ for the fine quasi-affine open, and $\cX_{\bB}[K]\langle\Psi\rangle$ for its locally closed image in the Borel-valued stack. Then
\[
\dim\cX_{\bB}[K]\langle\Psi\rangle=\dim\dot X_{K\rtimes\bT}\langle\Psi\rangle-\#\{\alpha\in\Phi^+-\Phi_K\mid\alpha\in\Psi_0\}-\dkF\#\Phi_K.
\]

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let $q$ be the change-of-group/destackification map from the fine quasi-affine open to its locally closed image in the Borel-valued stack. By F01-001, the standing connected-center and prime-bound assumptions required by the construction hold. COR9-003 proves that the recursive Borel charts are genuine rootwise additive torsors compatible with arbitrary base change, that the final Borel atlas is smooth and affine, and that every completed root-height layer is independent of the ordering within that layer. Base-changing those torsors to the fine quasi-affine open therefore gives the fiber calculation for $q$ without any residual atlas or ordering hypothesis.

For every $\alpha\in\Phi_K$, the rigidifying root factor is $\operatorname{Res}_{k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}/\mathbb F_p}U_{\alpha^\vee}$. It has dimension $[k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}:\mathbb F_p]=\dkF$. The completed-height ordering independence permits these factors to be assembled in any height-compatible order, and dimensions add, so they contribute $\dkF\#\Phi_K$.

For $\alpha\in\Phi^+\setminus\Phi_K$, the corresponding root direction occurs in the stabilizer exactly when its torus character is pointwise trivial. For a fine configuration this is precisely the condition $\alpha\in\Psi_0$. Each such root group is one-dimensional, so the remaining stabilizer contribution is
\[
\#\{\alpha\in\Phi^+\setminus\Phi_K\mid\alpha\in\Psi_0\}.
\]
Thus $q$ has constant relative stack dimension
\[
\dkF\#\Phi_K+\#\{\alpha\in\Phi^+\setminus\Phi_K\mid\alpha\in\Psi_0\}.
\]
Subtracting this constant relative dimension from the dimension of the fine chart gives the asserted formula.
