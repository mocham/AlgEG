# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $T/K$ be an unramified torus, and let $\bar f:T(K)\to\overline{\mathbb F}_p^\times$ be continuous. There is a continuous crystalline character $g_0:T(K)\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_p^\times$ such that $g_0$ is unit-valued on the maximal compact subgroup $\mathcal T(\mathcal O_K)$ and its reduction there equals $\bar f|_{\mathcal T(\mathcal O_K)}$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let `k=F_q` be the residue field of `K`, choose an unramified splitting extension `K_m/K` of degree `m`, and write `k_m=F_{q^m}`. Put `C=T_int(O_K)`. The kernel of `C -> T(k)` is pro-`p`, whereas `Fbar_p^x` has no `p`-torsion, so `bar f|_C` inflates from a character `bar chi` of `T(k)`.

Let `M=X^*(T)` and let `F=q pi^{-1}` be the Frobenius operator in Proposition 8. Its two exact rows identify `M/(F-1)M` and `M/(F^m-1)M` with the character groups of `T(k)` and `T(k_m)`. The vertical lattice map is `N_F=1+F+...+F^{m-1}`, and the vertical character map is pullback along the finite-field norm `N_{k_m/k}`. These maps commute because `(F-1)N_F=F^m-1`; the character map is injective, equivalently the norm on points of the finite-field torus is surjective. Choose `chi in M` representing `bar chi` in the top row.

Set `a=Frob_K pi^{-1}` and let `h=ev(chi)` be the corresponding algebraic character on `T(K_m)`. Define the multiplicative Frobenius norm `H=h a(h) ... a^{m-1}(h)`. Reduction sends `a` to `F`, so commutativity of Proposition 8 gives `bar H=N_{k_m/k}^*(bar chi)`. Moreover `a(H)H^{-1}=a^m(h)h^{-1}=1`: the torus splits over `K_m`, so `pi^m=1`, and `Frob_K^m` fixes the coefficient field `K_m`. Lemma 24 therefore descends `H` to a character `g_0:T(K)->Qpbar^x`.

On compact reductions, unramified base change is again pullback by `N_{k_m/k}`. Hence `N_{k_m/k}^*(bar g_0)=bar H=N_{k_m/k}^*(bar chi)`, and injectivity in Proposition 8 gives `bar g_0=bar chi` on `T(k)`. Algebraic evaluation on the integral model is unit-valued, so `g_0` is unit-valued on `C` and has the required reduction there. Its descended compact character is algebraic; F09-027 therefore makes `g_0` crystalline.
