# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let $T$ be an unramified torus over $K$. Every continuous character $\bar f:T(K)\to\overline{\mathbb F}_p^\times$ admits a crystalline lift $f:T(K)\to\mathcal O_{\overline{\mathbb Q}_p}^\times$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put `C=T_int(O_K)` and `A=T(K)/C`. For an unramified torus, the Kottwitz valuation identifies `A` with a finitely generated abelian lattice (the argument only needs finite generation). By D01-008 there is a crystalline character `g_0:T(K)->Qpbar^x` that is unit-valued on `C` and whose reduction there is `bar f|_C`.

The homomorphism `nu=v_p o g_0` vanishes on `C`, so it factors through `A`. Decompose `A` into its finite torsion part and a free part. The homomorphism `nu` vanishes on torsion; on a basis of the free part choose values in `Qpbar^x` having valuations `-nu`, and extend multiplicatively to a character `u:A->Qpbar^x`. Inflated to `T(K)`, `u` is trivial on `C`, hence is crystalline by F09-027. Thus `g_1=u g_0` is crystalline, is unit-valued on all of `T(K)`, and has the same reduction as `bar f` on `C`.

Now `r=bar f bar g_1^{-1}` is a residual character trivial on `C`, so it factors through `A`. Since `A` is finitely generated and `Fbar_p^x` is torsion, `r` has finite image. The Teichmuller section on `Fbar_p^x` is multiplicative, so its pointwise lift `[r]:A->O_Qpbar^x` is a continuous character. It is trivial on `C` and therefore crystalline by F09-027. The product `f=[r]g_1` is unit-valued, crystalline, and reduces to `bar f` on every element of `T(K)`.

The valuation-cancellation step is necessary: the norm-descended algebraic character supplied by Proposition 8 and Lemma 24 is only known to be integral on `C`. It makes the global reduction used in the printed Teichmuller step well-defined without adding any new hypothesis.
