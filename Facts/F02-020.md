# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, let $(G,B,T)$ be split pinned reductive with dual pinned group $(\bG,\bB,\bT)$, assume $p>h_{\bG}$, and let $U$ be the unipotent radical of $\bB$. Let $\Delta$ be the simple roots. For each $\delta\in\Delta$, let $\G_{m,\delta}\cong\G_m$ act on $U_{\delta^\vee}$ through the root $\delta$ and trivially on $U_{\delta'^\vee}$ for $\delta'\neq\delta$, and let $\cW_{U_{\delta^\vee}\rtimes\G_{m,\delta}}\to\cX_{\G_{m,\delta}}$ be the corresponding rank-one mod-$p$ Weil-Deligne stack. Using the morphism $\prod_{\delta\in\Delta}\delta:\bT\to\prod_{\delta\in\Delta}\G_{m,\delta}$ and its induced map on Emerton-Gee stacks, define
\[
\cW_{\bB}:=\cX_{\bT}\times_{\prod_{\delta\in\Delta}\cX_{\G_{m,\delta}}}\prod_{\delta\in\Delta}\cW_{U_{\delta^\vee}\rtimes\G_{m,\delta}}.
\]
