# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let
$\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$ be a split pinned Borel with
$p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Fix an ordered positive root $\alpha$ and a
lower recursive chart with coordinate ring $R$. Assume that its
universal lower logarithms $F_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}}$ and
$G_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}}$ satisfy the lower cocycle equation.
Define

$$P_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}}=
\operatorname{Ad}(t_f)\varphi(G_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}}),\qquad
Q_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}}=
\operatorname{Ad}(t_g)\gamma(F_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}}),$$

$$D_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}=\pi_\alpha\left(
\operatorname{BCH}(G_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}},
Q_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}})-
\operatorname{BCH}(F_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}},
P_{<\alpha}^{\mathrm{univ}})\right),$$

including every BCH class through $\operatorname{ht}(\alpha)$.
Assume at a Steinberg crossing that the root-$\alpha$ obstruction
module is cyclic with standard class $[b_{\alpha,\mathrm{std}}]$,
annihilator generated by $u_\alpha=t_\alpha-1$, and standard
cochain satisfying
$d(c_{\alpha,\mathrm{std}})=u_\alpha b_{\alpha,\mathrm{std}}$.
If $A_\alpha\in R$ is any lift of the coefficient of
$[D_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}]$ relative to the standard class, then
the actual next-stage logarithm, including its root-$\alpha$
coordinate and parameter $s_\alpha$, satisfies the cocycle
equation exactly on

$$r_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}
=s_\alpha(t_\alpha-1)+A_\alpha=0.$$

Replacing $A_\alpha$ by $A_\alpha+(t_\alpha-1)q$ and
$s_\alpha$ by $s_\alpha-q$ leaves the equation unchanged. At a
non-Steinberg stage there is no obstruction equation and the new
root cocycle coordinates are free. This is a conditional
root-stage assertion; it does not by itself construct a global
recursive chart or torsor.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Apply COR14-002 at the single root $\alpha$. COR14-001 supplies the
complete obstruction class, while the cyclic obstruction module
and the standard boundary relation convert its coefficient into
$s_\alpha(t_\alpha-1)+A_\alpha$. Free root cocycles have zero Herr
differential and do not change that equation. The displayed
triangular substitution proves independence of the chosen lift.

Unlike the source's pure ordered multilinear polynomial,
$A_\alpha$ contains the coefficient of every homogeneous BCH
class. The quadratic part is the oriented term from COR14-002;
both cubic terms and all higher mixed terms remain in the
remainder. The assertion concerns the next-stage logarithm, so it
also removes the source formula's literal mismatch between a
lower family independent of $s_\alpha$ and an equation involving
$s_\alpha$.
