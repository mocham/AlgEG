# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $G$ be a connected reductive group over $\mathbb Z_p$, let $k_f$ be a finite field, and let $\bar\rho:G_K\to G(k_f)$ be residual. Write $(\operatorname{Lan}\mathcal X_G)^{\#}_{\bar\rho}$ for the etale-sheafified left-Kan-extension fiber of the Emerton-Gee stack at $\bar\rho$, and $\mathcal F_{K,G,\bar\rho}$ for the derived unframed local Galois deformation functor. Then $(\operatorname{Lan}\mathcal X_G)^{\#}_{\bar\rho}\simeq\mathcal F_{K,G,\bar\rho}$. Consequently, if $\mathfrak X_G$ is the derived Laurent $F$-crystal stack, then $(\operatorname{Lan}\mathcal X_G)^{\#,\mathrm{nil}}\simeq\mathfrak X_G^{\mathrm{nil}}$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

### Complete original proof

```tex
To prove $(\Lan\calX_{G})^{\#}_{\bar \rho}\simeq\calF_{K,G,\bar\rho}$, the arguments in \cite[Proposition 3.51]{Min23} still work here. We briefly explain the strategy. The proof can be divided into three steps:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item prove $(\Lan\calX_{G})_{\bar \rho}\simeq (\Lan ^{\rm cl}\calR_{G_K,G/G}^{c})_{\bar \rho}$;
    \item prove $(\Lan^{\rm cl}\calR_{G_K,G/G}^{c})_{\bar \rho}\simeq\calR_{G_K,G/G,\bar\rho}^{c}$;
    \item prove $\calR_{G_K,G/G,\bar\rho}^{c}\simeq \calF_{K,G,\bar\rho}$.
\end{enumerate}
For Step 1, we need to use that both $\calX_G$ and $^{\rm cl}\calR_{G_K,G/G}^{c}$ are limit preserving, as well as the equivalence between the category of \'etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-modules with $G$-structure and the category of Galois representations valued in $G$ when the coefficient rings are finite Artinian local rings (cf. Proposition \ref{derivedrep-laurent}). For Step 2, we need Theorem \ref{PQ24} or more precisely the infinitesimal classicality result guaranteed by Theorem \ref{PQ24}. The Step 3 is just about the definitions of both sides.

To conclude $(\Lan\calX_{G})^{\#,\nil}\simeq \frakX_G^{\nil}$, we use Proposition \ref{GR-criterion} and the reduction process to the case of finite-field points we have already finished.
```

### Expanded argument

Fix the residual point $\bar\rho$. For the first step, limit preservation writes both $\mathcal X_G$ and the classical continuous-representation prestack as filtered colimits of finite-type affine test objects. The fibre over $\bar\rho$ of each test object is unchanged by localization and completion at the corresponding maximal ideal and is the filtered colimit of its Artinian local quotients. On those quotients, D03-003 (restricted to discrete rings) identifies etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-modules with $G$-structure and continuous $G$-valued Galois representations. Hence the Artinian overcategories, and therefore the two left-Kan-extension fibres, agree. Etale sheafification does not alter this local fibre: an etale algebra over an Artinian local ring is a finite product of Artinian local rings with finite residue fields, and both functors satisfy the corresponding descent.

For the second step, the framed continuous-representation prestack is a filtered colimit of derived affine prestacks almost of finite presentation. Its fibre at $\bar\rho$ is pro-corepresented by completed Artinian local quotients of the universal framed deformation ring. By D03-002 this ring is a local complete intersection and the derived framed deformation ring is homotopy discrete, so the derived framed deformation functor is exactly the left Kan extension of its classical restriction. Left Kan extension commutes with geometric realization; applying this to the conjugation action groupoid passes the equivalence from framed representations to the unframed quotient $\mathcal R^c_{G_K,G/G,\bar\rho}$. The third step is then definitional: geometric realization of the continuous framed representation functor under conjugation is the derived unframed local Galois deformation functor $\mathcal F_{K,G,\bar\rho}$. This proves
\[
(\operatorname{Lan}\mathcal X_G)^\#_{\bar\rho}\simeq\mathcal F_{K,G,\bar\rho}.
\]

For the global consequence, F09-023 identifies $\mathcal X_G$ with the underlying classical stack of $\mathfrak X_G$. The source verifies that the nilcompletion of the sheafified left Kan extension and $\mathfrak X_G^{\mathrm{nil}}$ admit deformation theories, are locally almost of finite type, and have actual cotangent complexes at finite-type classical points. By D03-004 it is therefore enough to compare cotangent complexes at finite-field points. At every such $\bar\rho$, the local functor comparison just proved and D03-003 identify the two deformation functors, hence their cotangent complexes. D03-004 gives the claimed global equivalence $(\operatorname{Lan}\mathcal X_G)^{\#,\mathrm{nil}}\simeq\mathfrak X_G^{\mathrm{nil}}$.
