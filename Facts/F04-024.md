# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, let $\dot X_{\BK\rtimes\bT\langle w\rangle}\langle\BPsi\rangle$ be the nonvanishing open in a twisted fine stratum, and let $r_{\Balpha}$ be its obstruction equations in the coordinates $x_{\Bbeta,1}$. Set

$$J=\left(\frac{\partial r_{\Balpha}}{\partial x_{\Bbeta,1}}\right).$$

If $J$ has full rank, then $\dot X_{\BK\rtimes\bT\langle w\rangle}\langle\BPsi\rangle$ is smooth. If $\dot W_{\BK\rtimes\bT\langle w\rangle}\langle\BPsi\rangle$ denotes its image in $W_{\BK\rtimes\bT\langle w\rangle}$, every fiber of

$$\dot X_{\BK\rtimes\bT\langle w\rangle}\langle\BPsi\rangle\longrightarrow\dot W_{\BK\rtimes\bT\langle w\rangle}\langle\BPsi\rangle$$

is irreducible of dimension

$$[F:\Q_p]\,\#\bigcup_{\Balpha\in\BPhi_{\BK}}\Balpha.$$

More generally, if $r$ is the corank of $J$, every such fiber has dimension at most

$$[F:\Q_p]\,\#\bigcup_{\Balpha\in\BPhi_{\BK}}\Balpha+r.$$

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let $\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(F_n/F)$ for the finite unramified splitting extension used in the twisted presentation. By COR14-003, after base change to the splitting cover the corrected equations are the complete absolute-root BCH equations, with a $\Gamma$-equivariant absolute-height filtration. Every restored mixed BCH term is independent of the matched penultimate-height coordinate block. Thus the block relevant to elimination is exactly the absolute leading block; descent introduces no additional derivative in that block.

Fix a geometric point of the relative nonvanishing base. For each absolute simple root $\delta$, choose a nonzero cochain coordinate $x_{\delta,i_\delta}$ on the resulting absolute chart and relabel that coordinate as index $1$. For every height $h$, let $T_h$ be the selected height-$(h+1)$ obstruction roots and let $m_h(T_h)$ be the matched height-$h$ roots obtained by the source-leaf contractions and terminal-core matching in COR10-005. The absolute square minor is
\[
M_{\mathrm{abs}}=\left(\frac{\partial r_{\alpha}^{\mathrm{BCH}}}{\partial x_{m_h(\alpha),i_{m_h(\alpha)}}}\right)_{h,\,\alpha\in T_h},
\]
ordered first by height and then by the contraction order. A source-leaf pivot is $c_{\beta,\delta}x_{\delta,i_\delta}$ for $\alpha=\beta+\delta$, with $c_{\beta,\delta}$ a unit Chevalley/BCH coefficient. Each terminal-core maximal minor is also a unit times a monomial in the selected simple-root coordinates. Consequently
\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{abs}}:=\det M_{\mathrm{abs}}=u\prod_{\delta,i}x_{\delta,i}^{e_{\delta,i}},\qquad u\in\overline{\mathbb F}_p^\times.
\]
This is the unit-triangular Jacobian minor used by the absolute elimination, not merely an unspecified full-rank minor.

The Galois action permutes absolute roots, equations, matched variables, and the monomial factors. Ordering each matched row-column pair together makes the row and column permutation signs equal, so their determinant signs cancel. A compatible rescaling of root vectors or cochain bases multiplies the equation and variable determinant lines by inverse units; equivalently, the Chevalley coefficient and its coordinate factor acquire the cancelling unit. Hence $\Delta_{\mathrm{abs}}$ is fixed as a determinant-line section. In an arbitrary scalar ordering or trivialization, the product of its distinct Galois translates is the exactly invariant orbit norm, still a unit-coefficient monomial. COR17-002 therefore descends this determinant to a unit on every relative chart obtained from the simple-root nonvanishing condition. Those charts cover $\dot X_{\BK\rtimes\bT\langle w\rangle}\langle\BPsi\rangle$.

The inverse matrix $M_{\mathrm{abs}}^{-1}=\operatorname{adj}(M_{\mathrm{abs}})/\Delta_{\mathrm{abs}}$ is Galois-equivariant. Solving its matched variables and proceeding upward by absolute height therefore descends. The complete BCH remainders are already known to involve only lower blocks, so the recursion never reintroduces an eliminated variable. After geometric base change every fiber of the relative morphism is consequently the affine space obtained by the absolute elimination in COR10-005. Its free-coordinate count is
\[
[F:\mathbb Q_p]\,\#\bigcup_{\boldsymbol\alpha\in\boldsymbol\Phi_{\mathbf K}}\boldsymbol\alpha.
\]
Finite-etale descent preserves this geometric fiber dimension, and an affine space after geometric base change is geometrically irreducible. This proves the relative elimination and the asserted irreducibility and dimension of every fiber.

The descended determinant is a unit at every geometric point, so the displayed Jacobian has full row rank everywhere on the relative nonvanishing open. After the torus, vanishing, and invariant linear-coordinate equations from COR14-003 are included, F09-008 gives smoothness. For the final clause, the only fiberwise-stable meaning of the source phrase `corank of $J$' is a uniform pointwise bound
\[
r_y=\sup_{z\in X_{\bar y}}\operatorname{corank}J(z)
\]
on a geometric fiber (or one global upper bound for all $r_y$). D09-004 then gives the displayed expected dimension plus $r_y$. Here the invariant unit minor gives $r_y=0$ for every $y$, uniformly, and the affine-space elimination gives equality. Thus the smoothness, irreducible-fiber, exact-dimension, and uniform-corank clauses all follow.
