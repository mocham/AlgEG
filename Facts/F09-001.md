# Statement

The morphisms  $\calX_G\to \calX_{d}\times \calX_{t(d)-1}$ is representable in formal schemes and locally of finite presentation. In particular, $\calX_G$ is a formal algebraic stack locally of finite presentation over $\Spf(\bZ_p)$.

# Proof or Examples

## Expanded self-contained proof

### Expanded proof

Work in the standing setup and let `X_G^circ` be the prestack of triples consisting of the two underlying etale `(phi,Gamma)`-modules and their surjective tensor-construction map. The cited paper constructs a factorization

`X_G -> X_G^circ -> X_d x X_{t(d)-1}`.

Proposition `surj1` supplies the limit-preservation input used to extend the twist description, and hence this factorization, from finite-type coefficients to all p-nilpotent coefficient rings. Lemma `surj2` proves that the second arrow is representable by formal schemes locally given by completions of finite-type affine schemes; in particular it is locally of finite presentation.

For an affine test morphism `Spec(R) -> X_G^circ`, the pullback of the first arrow is precisely `B_R^Gamma`. Proposition `main-2` proves that `B_R^Gamma` is a scheme locally of finite presentation over `R`. Thus `X_G -> X_G^circ` is representable by schemes and locally of finite presentation. Representability by formal schemes and local finite presentation are stable under base change and composition, so the given composite is representable by formal schemes and locally of finite presentation.

Finally, the rank-n Emerton-Gee stacks are formal algebraic stacks locally of finite presentation over `Spf(Z_p)`. Hence their product has the same property, and pulling back a formal smooth cover along the given representable morphism gives a formal smooth cover for `X_G`. Therefore `X_G` is a formal algebraic stack locally of finite presentation over `Spf(Z_p)`.
