# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $L/K$ be finite Galois, let $E/\mathbb Q_l$ be finite, and let $G$ be a possibly disconnected reductive group over $E$. Fix an inertial type and consider the substack of $G$-valued Weil-Deligne representations trivial on $I_L$ whose monodromy operator is fixed to be $N=0$. At an object $D$ with Frobenius $\Phi$, its tangent-obstruction theory is controlled by the two-term complex
\[
(\operatorname{ad}D)^{I_{L/K}}\xrightarrow{1-\operatorname{Ad}(\Phi)}(\operatorname{ad}D)^{I_{L/K}}
\]
concentrated in degrees $0$ and $1$. In particular, the obstruction group vanishes at every point. The fixed-type framed monodromy-zero moduli space is smooth of dimension $\dim_E G$, and its quotient by $G$ is smooth and equidimensional of dimension zero.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

BG19 gives the full Weil-Deligne tangent-obstruction double complex and its lifting interpretation. On the deformation problem in which $N$ is required to remain zero, the $N$-variation and the monodromy compatibility row disappear, leaving exactly the displayed two-term complex, as stated explicitly in the proof at lines 3193-3200. There is no degree-two term, so every infinitesimal lifting obstruction vanishes. The two terms have the same finite dimension, hence $\dim H^0=\dim H^1$. The framed tangent-space formula from the same deformation theory is $\dim G-\dim H^0+\dim H^1=\dim G$ at every point. Formal smoothness at all points of the finite-type framed presentation gives smoothness and pure dimension $\dim G$; quotienting by the change-of-framing action of $G$ gives stack dimension zero.
