# Statement


Let $\mathbf G$ be a split pinned connected reductive group and assume $p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Let $X_{\mathbf K\rtimes\mathbf T\langle w\rangle}\langle\boldsymbol\Psi\rangle$ be a twisted fine stratum, and let $f^{\mathrm{univ}},g^{\mathrm{univ}}$ be its universal etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module coordinates, satisfying $f^{\mathrm{univ}}\varphi(g^{\mathrm{univ}})=g^{\mathrm{univ}}\gamma(f^{\mathrm{univ}})$. Write the relative-root decomposition of the truncated logarithm as $\log_{\le h_{\mathbf G}}(f^{\mathrm{univ}}(f^{\mathrm{univ}})^{\mathrm{ss},-1},g^{\mathrm{univ}}(g^{\mathrm{univ}})^{\mathrm{ss},-1})=\sum_{\boldsymbol\alpha\in\boldsymbol\Phi_{\mathbf K}}c_{\boldsymbol\alpha}$. Define $\dot X_{\mathbf K\rtimes\mathbf T\langle w\rangle}$ to be the open subscheme of $X_{\mathbf K\rtimes\mathbf T\langle w\rangle}$ on which $[c_{\boldsymbol\delta}]\ne0$ in $H^1_{\mathrm{Herr}}(U_{\boldsymbol\delta})$ for every relative simple root $\boldsymbol\delta\in\boldsymbol\Delta_{\mathbf K}$. This open subscheme is quasi-affine.
