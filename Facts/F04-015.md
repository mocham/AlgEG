# Statement


Let $\bG$ be a connected reductive group with maximal torus $\bT$ and Coxeter number $h_{\bG}$. Assume $p>h_{\bG}$. The order of the Weyl group $N_{\bG}(\bT)/\bT$ is prime to $p$; equivalently, $p\nmid\#(N_{\bG}(\bT)/\bT)$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

### Expanded proof

Write the root system of the derived group as a product of irreducible root systems, with Weyl groups $W_i$, Coxeter numbers $h_i$, and fundamental invariant degrees $d_{i,1},\ldots,d_{i,r_i}$. For an irreducible crystallographic reflection group,
\[
|W_i|=\prod_{j=1}^{r_i}d_{i,j}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\max_j d_{i,j}=h_i.
\]
These are the standard degree and Coxeter-number formulas cited from Humphreys, Theorem 3.9. With $h_{\bG}=\max_i h_i$, every $d_{i,j}$ is at most $h_{\bG}$. If the prime $p$ satisfies $p>h_{\bG}$, it divides none of these degrees, and hence divides none of the products $|W_i|$. The Weyl group of $\bG$ is $\prod_iW_i$; any central torus contributes no Weyl factor. Therefore $p\nmid|N_{\bG}(\bT)/\bT|$.
