# Statement


Let $p=3$, $F=\mathbb Q_3$, $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_3$, and $R=k[t^{\pm1}]$. Let $\bar\chi:G_F\to k^\times$ be the nontrivial unramified character with $\bar\chi(\operatorname{Frob}_F)=-1$, and let $M'$ be its universal unramified twist in a $\Gamma$-stable basis $e$ with $\varphi(e)=-t e$. Then $c=[(e,0)]\in H^1_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M')$ is nonzero and satisfies $(t+1)c=0$. Thus the unnormalized coordinate places the trivial fiber and its $H^0$-driven Herr $H^1$ torsion at $t=-1$. Set $u=-t$. In the $u$-coordinate one has $\varphi(e)=u e$, the trivial fiber is $u=1$, and $R/(t+1)\cong k[u^{\pm1}]/(u-1)$. The shifted calculation is therefore the normalized trivial-fiber torsion calculation after reparameterization.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

For the rank-one Herr complex, $d^0(x)=((\varphi-1)x,(\gamma-1)x)$ and $d^1(x,y)=(\gamma-1)x-(\varphi-1)y$. Since $\gamma(e)=e$, $(e,0)$ is a one-cocycle, and
\[
d^0(e)=((-t-1)e,0)=-(t+1)(e,0).
\]
Hence $(t+1)c=0$. At $t=-1$ both $\varphi$ and $\Gamma$ act trivially. The specialized cocycle is the nonzero unramified class in $H^1(G_{\mathbb Q_3},k)$, represented by the continuous homomorphism sending Frobenius to $1$. A boundary before specialization would specialize to a boundary, so $c\ne0$.

Set $u=-t$. This is an automorphism of the Laurent polynomial coefficient ring, and the Frobenius formula becomes $\varphi(e)=u e$. Consequently $t=-1$ is the normalized point $u=1$, while the annihilator $(t+1)$ becomes a unit times $(u-1)$. The calculation therefore exhibits the torsion predicted by the normalized trivial branch; it does not contradict that branch. Local Euler characteristic gives the same check: the fiber dimension jumps at the unique trivial specialization and nowhere else.
