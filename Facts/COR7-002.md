# Statement


Let $R=\overline{\mathbb F}_p[t,t^{-1}]$ and let $M$ be a rank-$1$ etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $\mathbf E_{F,R}$. Suppose $H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M,R)\cong\prod_{i=1}^k R/(t-t_i)$ for pairwise distinct elements $t_i\in\overline{\mathbb F}_p$. Then the functor $S\mapsto\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M,R),S)$ is represented by $\operatorname{Spec}R[s_1,\ldots,s_k]/((t-t_1)s_1,\ldots,(t-t_k)s_k)$. Equivalently, this is the fiber product over $\operatorname{Spec}R$ of the schemes $\operatorname{Spec}R[s_i]/((t-t_i)s_i)$, and, because the $t_i$ are pairwise distinct, it is also their affine scheme-theoretic gluing along the common zero section $\operatorname{Spec}R$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Apply D04-001 with $A=R$ and $f_i=t-t_i$. Each $f_i$ is a nonzerodivisor, and finite products and finite direct sums of $R$-modules agree. The two-term free resolutions of the $R/(f_i)$ identify first Tor with the tuples $(s_1,\ldots,s_k)$ satisfying $f_is_i=0$, giving the displayed representing ring and the scheme fiber product. If $i\ne j$, pairwise distinctness gives $(t-t_i)-(t-t_j)=t_j-t_i\in R^\times$, so $(f_i)$ and $(f_j)$ are comaximal. The final assertion of D04-001 therefore identifies the fiber product with the affine pushout gluing of the individual axes along their common zero section.
