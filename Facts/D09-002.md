# Statement

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic zero, let $G$ be a smooth affine group scheme over $k$, and let $A$ be an Artin local $k$-algebra. If a $G$-torsor $D$ over $A$ carries an exact tensor filtration on its associated fiber functor $\operatorname{Rep}_k(G)\to\operatorname{Proj}_A$, then the filtration is split by a cocharacter $\lambda:\mathbb G_m\to\operatorname{Aut}_G(D)$.

# Proof or Examples

## Expanded self-contained proof

By the Deligne splitting theorem cited by BG19 from Saavedra, every exact tensor filtration for a smooth affine group over a characteristic-zero base is Zariski-locally splittable. The spectrum of an Artin local ring has one closed point, and any member of a Zariski cover containing that point is the whole spectrum. Hence the local splitting is global and is equivalently a tensor grading, or a cocharacter $\mathbb G_m\to\operatorname{Aut}_G(D)$ inducing the filtration.
