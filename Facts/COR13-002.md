# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite. Let $\mathbf G$ be a split connected reductive group over $\overline{\mathbb Z}_p$ with connected center, maximal torus $\mathbf T$, simple roots $\Delta$, and Coxeter number $h_{\mathbf G}$, and assume $p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Let $\mathbf M\subsetneq\mathbf G$ be the standard Levi defined by $\Delta_M\subsetneq\Delta$, let $U^{\mathbf M}$ be the unipotent radical of the corresponding standard parabolic, and choose a Tits representative $\dot w\in N_{\mathbf M}(\mathbf T)(\overline{\mathbb Z}_p)$ whose Weyl image is elliptic in $\mathbf M$. Write $n$ for the order of $\dot w$, let $q_n:G_F\to\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$ be the unramified quotient normalized by arithmetic Frobenius, and let $v:G_F\to\widehat{\mathbb Z}$ be its integral lift. For each $\delta\in\Delta\setminus\Delta_M$, choose $\mu_\delta\in X_*(\mathbf T)$ with $\langle\gamma,\mu_\delta\rangle=1$ for $\gamma=\delta$ and $0$ for every other $\gamma\in\Delta$. If $m_\delta=\#\langle w\rangle\delta$ and $\operatorname{Ad}(\dot w^{m_\delta})$ acts on the $\delta$-root line by $c_\delta$, choose $z_\delta\in\overline{\mathbb Z}_p^\times$ with $z_\delta^{m_\delta}=c_\delta^{-1}$, and put $\mu=\sum_{\delta\notin\Delta_M}\mu_\delta$ and $t=\prod_{\delta\notin\Delta_M}\mu_\delta(z_\delta)$. Let $\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}:G_F\to\mathbb Z_p^\times$ be the cyclotomic character and define $N_{\mathrm{cyc}}(g)=t^{v(g)}\,\mu(\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}(g))\,\dot w^{q_n(g)}$. Define $\underline\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}}\in X^*(\operatorname{Res}_{F/\mathbb Q_p}\mathbf T)$ to be the Hodge type of $N_{\mathrm{cyc}}$. Define $\mathcal X_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}^{\underline\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}},\mathrm{tame}}$ to be the integral moduli stack of potentially semistable $U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle$-valued Galois representations of Hodge type $\underline\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}}$ and tame inertial type, and define $\mathfrak X_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}^{\underline\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}},\mathrm{tame}}$ to be its rigid generic fiber.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The corrected cyclotomic construction proves that the required integral cocharacters exist, that the finite correction satisfies $t^n=1$, and that the displayed formula defines a continuous homomorphism into $\mathbf T(\overline{\mathbb Z}_p)\langle\dot w\rangle$. After the unramified extension of degree $n$, its finite factors vanish and its restriction is $\mu\circ\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}$, so it is crystalline there and has the stated Hodge type. The two moduli objects are then definitions: the first is the fixed-Hodge-type, tame-inertial-type potentially semistable stack, and the second is its rigid generic fiber. In particular, the label $\underline\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}}$ is attached to the corrected representation displayed in the Statement and not to the arbitrary cocharacter norm used in the old construction.
