# Statement

Let f:X->S be locally of finite presentation and let x be a point of X. If affine neighborhoods admit a presentation A=R[x_1,...,x_n]/(f_1,...,f_c) for which a c-by-c Jacobian minor is invertible at x, then f is smooth at x.

# Proof or Examples

## Expanded self-contained proof

Choose affine neighborhoods U = Spec(A) of x and V = Spec(R) of f(x) as in the hypothesis, and let q be the prime of A corresponding to x. After permuting the variables, the specified c-by-c minor is the determinant g = det(partial f_i/partial x_j)_{1 <= i,j <= c}. Saying that this minor is invertible at x means that its image in the local ring A_q is a unit, equivalently g is not in q. Thus the presentation satisfies condition (6) of Stacks Project Tag 01V9, Lemma 29.35.14 (lines 174-245). That lemma proves that condition (6) implies the existence of a standard smooth neighborhood, condition (5), and that a standard smooth morphism is smooth (lines 247-255). Hence f is smooth at x.
