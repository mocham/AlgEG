# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite with $p>3$, let $(G,B,T)$ be a pinned quasi-split connected reductive group over $K$ that splits over a finite extension $L/K$, and let ${}^LP=P\rtimes\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ for a Galois-stable standard parabolic $P=U\rtimes\check M$ of $\check G$. For a $P^{-}$-dominant Hodge type $\lambda$ and an inertial type $\tau$, both valued in ${}^LP$, the fixed-type potentially semistable stack $\mathcal X_{K,{}^LP}^{\tau,\lambda}$ exists as a Noetherian formal algebraic stack flat over $\operatorname{Spf}\overline{\mathbb Z}_p$. It is the unique flat closed substack of $\mathcal X_{K,{}^LP}$ whose points over every finite flat coefficient algebra are exactly the parameters that become potentially semistable of Hodge type $\lambda$ and inertial type $\tau$ after inverting $p$. If ${}^LP={}^LG$ is reductive, this fixed-type stack is of finite type over $\operatorname{Spf}\overline{\mathbb Z}_p$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Definition 5 on page 13 defines $\mathcal X_{K,{}^LP}^{\tau,\lambda}$ as the unique flat closed substack with the stated finite-flat point criterion. Lemma 9 first proves that the ambient parabolic Emerton--Gee stack is Noetherian. Theorem 10 on page 14 proves existence when $p>3$: it handles the reductive case using a central isogeny from products of Weil restrictions of tame groups, and in the general parabolic case realizes the desired stack as the flat quotient of the fiber product with the fixed-type Levi stack. The same theorem asserts finite type only in the reductive case ${}^LP={}^LG$.
