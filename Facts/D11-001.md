# Statement


Let $p>12$, let $\Phi^+$ be the 24 positive roots of a root system of type $F_4$, written in Bourbaki coordinates, and let $\mathbf T$ be its split maximal torus. Apply the exact grouped-cone procedure that generates every downward-closed subset $C\subseteq\Phi^+$, puts $\Phi_K=\Phi^+\setminus C$ and lets $K$ be the corresponding root-subgroup product, checks affine consistency, the forced set $\Psi_0$, and both fine-pair closure conditions, and computes ranks from integral nonzero minors. There are 105 choices of $C$, and for every resulting fine configuration $\Psi=(\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$ the certified cone-model bound is
\[
\dim X_{K,\mathbf T}\langle\Psi\rangle^{\mathrm{cone}}\le d\#\Phi^+
\]
whenever $d=[F:\mathbb Q_p]\ge2$. For $d=1$, the procedure produces 4,862 fine pairs, covers all 3,002 relevant linearly independent seeds, and its classification against the 104 choices with $K$ nontrivial has exactly four equality records. Their triples $(C,\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$ are
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{I}:&\ (\{0010\},\{0010\},\{0001,0011,0100,0110,0120\}),\\
\mathrm{II}:&\ (\{0010\},\{0010\},\{0001,0011,0100,0110,0120,1000\}),\\
\mathrm{III}:&\ (\{0010,1000\},\{0010,1000\},\{0001,0011,0100,0110,0120,1100,1110,1120\}),\\
\mathrm{IV}:&\ (\{0001,0010,0011,1000\},\{0001,0010,0011,1000\},\{0100,0110,0111,0120,0121,0122,1100,1110,1111,1120,1121,1122\}).
\end{aligned}
\]
Every integral rank witness used by the procedure remains nonzero in characteristic $p>12$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The implementation generates the 24 positive F4 roots and all 105 downward-closed complements from the Cartan matrix. For each complement, compute_field_degree_bound constructs the maximal grouped-cone coefficient matrix and an exact nonzero-minor rank witness. The largest degree not covered automatically is d=1, and every prime dividing a witness content is 2 or 3.

At d=1, certify_pair_inventory checks affine consistency, the forced Psi_0 set, and both closure conditions for every one of the 4,862 reviewed pairs. It independently generates all 3,002 relevant linearly independent seeds and verifies that each occurs in the inventory. classify_degree_one_f4 then checks the 4,862 pairs against every nonempty complement: 294,388 cases are discarded by the rigorous target-count bound and 211,260 receive exact polynomial Jacobian-rank checks. Exactly four records have epsilon zero, and they are the four triples displayed in the Statement. The separate grouped-cone certificate recomputes their matrices and ranks.

The checked SHA-256 manifests reproduce the pair inventory, exhaustive certificate, and grouped-cone table byte for byte. The exhaustive certificate records SageMath 10.9, 24 roots, 105 complements, the automatic degree bound d>=2, 4,862 pairs, complete coverage of 3,002 relevant seeds, exactly four epsilon-zero records, and uniformity for p>12. COR11-001 supplies the corrected cone definition with every linear generator common to the fine and cone models and every quadratic term retained. These exact finite checks prove the stated grouped-cone classification in that corrected model. They do not assert a comparison with actual fine-locus dimensions; downstream comparison uses the flat degeneration in COR11-002.
