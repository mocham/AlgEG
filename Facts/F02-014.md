# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, let $B=B_{\PGL_2}\cong\G_a\rtimes\G_m$, and let $\cX_{\G_m}$ be the rank-one mod-$p$ Emerton-Gee stack. Write $H^2_{\Herr}(\bfE_{F,\cO_{\cX_{\G_m}}})$ for the degree-two Herr cohomology of its universal rank-one family. Define $\cW_B$ to be the algebraic stack over $\cX_{\G_m}$ whose value after a morphism $\Spec R\to\cX_{\G_m}$ relatively represents
\[
\Tor_1^{\cO_{\cX_{\G_m}}}\bigl(H^2_{\Herr}(\bfE_{F,\cO_{\cX_{\G_m}}}),R\bigr).
\]
If $X_{\G_m}\to\cX_{\G_m}$ is the torus destackification, define the destackified Weil-Deligne space by $W_B:=\cW_B\times_{\cX_{\G_m}}X_{\G_m}$.
