# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, let $\bB=U\rtimes\bT$ be a split Borel subgroup, let $(K,\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$ be a fine configuration, and let $X_{K\rtimes\bT}\langle\Psi\rangle$ have gauge coordinates $x_{\alpha,i},s_\alpha$. Define $\dot X_{K\rtimes\bT}\langle\Psi\rangle$ to be the quasi-affine open subscheme on which, for every $\delta\in\Delta_K$,
\[
\begin{cases}(x_{\delta,1},\dots,x_{\delta,[F:\Q_p]})\ne0,&\delta\in\Delta_K-\Psi_0-\Psi_2,\\(x_{\delta,1},\dots,x_{\delta,[F:\Q_p]+1})\ne0,&\delta\in(\Delta_K\cap\Psi_0)-\Psi_2,\\(x_{\delta,1},\dots,x_{\delta,[F:\Q_p]},s_\delta)\ne0,&\delta\in(\Delta_K\cap\Psi_2)-\Psi_0,\\(x_{\delta,1},\dots,x_{\delta,[F:\Q_p]+1},s_\delta)\ne0,&\delta\in\Delta_K\cap\Psi_2\cap\Psi_0.\end{cases}
\]
Let $\cX_{\bB}[K]\langle\Psi\rangle\subset X_{\bB}$ be the locally closed image of $\dot X_{K\rtimes\bT}\langle\Psi\rangle$ under the change-of-group/destackification map. The collection
\[
\{\cX_{\bB}[K]\langle\Psi\rangle\subset X_{\bB}:(K,\Psi_0,\Psi_2)\text{ is a fine configuration}\}
\]
is called the fine stratification of $\cX_{\bB}$.

### Nearby source text

```tex
\begin{remark}
\label{rem:sq-K}
For applications,
it makes no difference to treat
$\cX_{\bB}[K]\langle\Psi\rangle\subset \cX_{\bB}$
as the scheme-theoretic image of
$X_{K\rtimes \bT}\langle\Psi\rangle$.

To be more precise,
if we write $\overline{\cX_{\bB}[K]\langle\Psi\rangle\subset X_{\bB}}$
for the scheme-theoretic image
of $\dot X_{K\rtimes \bT}\langle\Psi\rangle$,
then
$\cX_{\bB}[K]\langle\Psi\rangle\subset X_{\bB}$
is $\overline{\cX_{\bB}[K]\langle\Psi\rangle}
- \bigcup_{K'\subsetneq K}\overline{\cX_{\bB}[K']\langle\Psi\rangle}$.
\end{remark}
```
