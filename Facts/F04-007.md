# Statement


Let $\Phi^+$ be the directed positive-root graph whose edges are $\beta\to\alpha$ with $\alpha-\beta$ a simple positive root, let $w$ act on a $w$-stable collection of roots, and let $\fB$ be an induced bipartite subgraph of the quotient $w$-root poset. Define the associated absolute bipartite graph $\fB_{\abs}$ as follows:

1. its source vertices are all absolute roots lying in the $w$-relative roots that are source vertices of $\fB$;
2. its target vertices are all absolute roots lying in the $w$-relative roots that are target vertices of $\fB$; and
3. its directed graph structure is the subgraph structure induced from $\Phi^+$ on those source and target vertices.
