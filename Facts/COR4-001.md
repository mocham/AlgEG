# Statement


Let $F_n/F$ be unramified of degree $n$ prime to $p$, put $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_p$, $R=k[t^{\pm1}]$, $G=\operatorname{Gal}(F_n/F)$, $d_F=[F:\mathbb Q_p]$, and $d_{k_F}=[k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}:\mathbb F_p]$. Let $\bar\chi:G_F\to k^\times$ be a character and let $\bar\chi_n$ be its restriction to $G_{F_n}$. Choose the representative of the unramified-twist orbit so that, if $\bar\chi_n$ is unramified, then $\bar\chi_n=1$, and choose the twist coordinate so that the trivial specialization is $t=1$. Let $M'=M'_{\bar\chi_n}$ be the resulting universal unramified-twist rank-one etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $\mathbf E_{F_n,R}$. If $\bar\chi_n=1$, let $k_{\bar\chi}$ denote the one-dimensional $k[G]$-module on which $G$ acts through $\bar\chi$, made an $R$-module by $t\mapsto1$. Then, as $R[G]$-modules,
\[
H^1_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M')\cong\begin{cases}
R[G]^{\oplus d_F},&\bar\chi_n\ne1,\\
R[G]^{\oplus d_F}\oplus k_{\bar\chi},&\bar\chi_n=1.
\end{cases}
\]
For the modified Herr complex obtained by adjoining $R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_{F_{n,\mathrm{cyc}}}$ in degree one and using the constant-term map in the unramified case, one has in both cases
\[
H^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M')\cong R[G]^{\oplus d_F}\oplus\bigl(R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_{F_{n,\mathrm{cyc}}}\bigr)_{\bar\chi},
\]
where the subscript means that the natural $G$-action on the residue field is diagonally twisted by $\bar\chi$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Because $p\nmid n$, the character idempotents split every $R[G]$-module. For a character $\vartheta$ of $G$, the $\vartheta$-isotypic Herr complex is the rank-one universal unramified-twist complex over $F$ attached to $\bar\chi\vartheta^{-1}$. The chosen normalization implies that this family has a trivial specialization exactly when $\bar\chi_n=1$ and $\vartheta=\bar\chi$, and that specialization is then $t=1$.

Over the PID $R$, Smith normal form for the perfect rank-one Herr complex separates a free degree-one part from the possible trivial and cyclotomic crossings. The local Euler characteristic gives free degree-one rank $d_F$ in every isotypic component. At a trivial crossing, the degree-zero-to-degree-one summand is $[R\xrightarrow{t-1}R]$, so it contributes $R/(t-1)$ to global $H^1$. A cyclotomic crossing instead occurs in the degree-one-to-degree-two summand; it contributes to fibers through $\operatorname{Tor}_1(H^2,-)$ and contributes no torsion summand to global $H^1$. These are simple crossings because the universal unramified character evaluates Frobenius by the coordinate $t$. Thus every isotypic component has free rank $d_F$, and the unique extra $H^1$ summand is $R/(t-1)$ in the $\bar\chi$-isotypic component when $\bar\chi_n=1$. Reassembling the idempotents gives the first displayed formula; the free components form $R[G]^{\oplus d_F}$. This also agrees fiberwise with the restricted-cohomology decomposition.

For a ramified family, the modified complex splits off $R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_{F_{n,\mathrm{cyc}}}$ in degree one. For the normalized unramified family, the constant-term differential changes the trivial crossing from $R\xrightarrow{t-1}R$ to the injective map $R\to R\oplus R$, $a\mapsto(a,(t-1)a)$; its cokernel is free of rank one rather than $R/(t-1)$. With the residue-field multiplicity and descent action restored, this replaces the trivial torsion summand by $\bigl(R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_{F_{n,\mathrm{cyc}}}\bigr)_{\bar\chi}$. The remaining free regular summands are unchanged, proving the modified formula.

## Faithful GrobV14 proof

```tex
\begin{proof}
By Lemma \ref{fact:F01-018},
$H^1_{\Herr}(M')$
does not have the cyclotomic terms,
compared to Theorem \ref{fact:F04-012}.
When $\bar\chi_n$ is trivial,
there is the unramified
cocycle $(1,0)\in Z^1_{\Herr}(M')$ (c.f. Subsection
\ref{subsec:unramified}),
which is a coboundary over the locus
$(t\neq 1)$.
Quotienting out by $[(1,0)]$,
we obtain a finite free
$\bFp[t^{\pm1}]$-module
whose isotypic parts
have the same rank by Theorem \ref{fact:F04-012}.

Write $H^1_{\Herr}(M')_{\vartheta}$
for the $\vartheta$-isotypic part
of the (maximal) finite free quotient
$H^1_{\Herr, \text{tf}}(M')$
of $H^1_{\Herr}(M')$.
Choose a basis
$\{v_{k, \vartheta}\}_{k=1}^{[F:\Q_p]}$
for each isotypic part.
Set
\[
c'_k:=\sum_{\vartheta} v_{k, \vartheta},~
k=1,\dots,[F:\Q_p].
\]
Note that we have the projection operators
$$e_\vartheta = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{\sigma \in \Gal(F_n/F)} \vartheta(\sigma)^{-1} \, \sigma$$
satisfying
\[
e_\vartheta(c'_k) = v_{k, \vartheta}.
\]
The upshot is that
\[
H^1_{\Herr,\text{tf}}(M')=
\bigoplus_{k=1}^{[F:\Q_p]}
\bFp[t^{\pm1}][\Gal(F_n/F)]c'_k.
\qedhere
\]
\end{proof}
```
