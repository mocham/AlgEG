# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be a finite extension, let $\Gal_F$ be its absolute Galois group, and let $\bG$ be a connected reductive group with connected center. Assume $p>h_{\bG}$, where $h_{\bG}$ is the Coxeter number of $\bG$. Then:

1. If $\bG=\mathrm{F}_4$, every continuous representation $\bar\rho:\Gal_F\to\bG(\bFp)$ has a crystalline lift.
2. If $[F:\Q_p]>\frac{\dim\bG}{2}$, every continuous representation $\bar\rho:\Gal_F\to\bG(\bFp)$ has a crystalline lift.

Here a crystalline lift means a characteristic-zero crystalline $\bG$-valued Galois representation whose reduction is $\bar\rho$.
