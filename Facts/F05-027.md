# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let $\mathbf G$ be a connected reductive group with connected center, under the standing hypothesis $p>h_{\mathbf G}$. If $\mathbf G$ has type $F_4$, then every homomorphism $\operatorname{Gal}_F\to\mathbf G(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$ has a crystalline lift. More generally, if $[F:\mathbb Q_p]>\dim(\mathbf G)/2$, then every homomorphism $\operatorname{Gal}_F\to\mathbf G(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$ has a crystalline lift.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Suppose first that the semisimple rank is at least two. Put $d=[F:\mathbb Q_p]$, $N=\#\Phi^+$, and $r=\operatorname{rank}\mathbf G$. Since $\dim\mathbf G=2N+r$ and $r\geq2$, the integer inequality $d>\dim\mathbf G/2$ implies $d\geq N+2$. Conditional on COR8-001, every irreducible component of the reduced $\mathbf G$-valued Emerton-Gee stack is one of the components produced from a Borel Weil-Deligne component. COR10-004 places each such Borel component in one of finitely many crystalline special fibers, and F05-023 converts membership in the proper flat crystalline image into a pointwise crystalline lift. The same argument proves the type-$F_4$ clause conditional on F05-022.

If the semisimple rank is at most one, MAIN-001 applies directly. Its root-datum classification writes every split connected-center rank-one group as $\operatorname{PGL}_2\times T$ or $\operatorname{GL}_2\times T$. EG23 and Lin22 give the factor lifts, so their Cartesian product is already a lift for $\mathbf G$; no lift to a finite central cover, overlap compatibility, or descent obstruction is involved. This proves the general large-degree assertion in the rank-one and torus cases.

Thus the target follows conditional on the listed high-rank and F4 dependencies, while its former rank-one central-overlap gap is bypassed by MAIN-001.
