# Statement


Let $f:\mathcal F_1\to\mathcal F_2$ be a morphism of nilcomplete derived prestacks admitting deformation theories. Suppose a classical prestack maps by nilpotent embeddings to both $\mathcal F_i$, compatibly with $f$, and at every classical point the induced map from the cotangent complex of $\mathcal F_2$ to that of $\mathcal F_1$ is an equivalence. Then $f$ is an equivalence. If both prestacks are locally almost of finite type and their pro-cotangent complexes at finite-type classical points are actual complexes, it is enough to check the cotangent equivalence after passage to finite-field-valued points.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The first assertion is Gaitsgory-Rozenblyum, Chapter 1, Proposition 8.3.2, in the exact form recalled by Min25. For the reduction in the second assertion, local almost finite type reduces pro-cotangent comparison to finite-type points. Once the pro-objects are actual complexes, faithfully flat descent and residue-field base change detect an equivalence, so it suffices to test finite-field-valued points. Min25 verifies these hypotheses for the nilcompletion of the left Kan extension of the Emerton-Gee stack and for the nilcomplete Laurent-F-crystal stack before applying the criterion.
