# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ and $E/\mathbb Q_l$ be finite, let $G/E$ be reductive, and let $\rho_x:G_K\to G(E^{\prime})$ be a closed point of a framed fixed-type deformation space, with associated Weil-Deligne object $D_x$. Over the dual numbers $E^{\prime}[\varepsilon]$, forgetting the framing makes the tangent space a $\operatorname{Lie}G$-torsor over the unframed deformation groupoid. If $l\ne p$, the unframed fixed-inertial-type deformations are identified with deformations of the associated Weil-Deligne representation. If $l=p$, the unframed potentially semistable deformations of fixed inertial and Hodge type are identified with deformations of the associated filtered $G$-valued $(\varphi,N)$-module satisfying those types.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

For $l\ne p$, the usual equivalence between $l$-adic Galois representations and Weil-Deligne representations is compatible with Artin-local infinitesimal deformations and with the fixed inertial type. For $l=p$, BG19 invokes the Artin-local potentially semistable comparison theorem to identify the unframed deformation category with the corresponding subcategory of filtered $G$-valued $(\varphi,N)$-modules. In either case, changing a framing is the infinitesimal action of $G$, whose tangent space is $\operatorname{Lie}G$; hence the framed tangent space is a $\operatorname{Lie}G$-torsor over the unframed groupoid.
