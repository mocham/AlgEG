# Statement


Let T be a torus over a finite extension K/Q_p, let T_int be its standard integral model, and let g:T(K)->Qpbar^x be a continuous character with corresponding L-parameter. The parameter is crystalline of Hodge type zero if and only if g is trivial on T_int(O_K). Equivalently, the crystalline characters of Hodge type zero are exactly the unramified torus characters.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put `C = T_int(O_K) = T(K)_1`, the kernel of the Kottwitz valuation for the standard integral model. The standard classification of crystalline torus parameters can be checked after a finite splitting extension and against a finite Galois-stable faithful family of algebraic characters of the dual torus. The crystalline/weakly-admissible equivalence and Dieudonne-Manin classification identify the algebraic character on `C` with evaluation of the Hodge cocharacter. Thus a crystalline parameter of Hodge type zero restricts on `C` as `ev(0)=1`; the remaining Frobenius datum is unramified. The use of a Galois-stable family makes this conclusion descend from the split torus and also shows that it detects the original torus parameter, not merely its individual split constituents.

Conversely, local class field theory for tori identifies characters trivial on `C` with unramified torus parameters. An unramified parameter has the standard crystalline filtered Frobenius datum with filtration concentrated in degree zero, hence Hodge type zero. Therefore `g|_C=1` is equivalent both to unramifiedness and to crystallinity of Hodge type zero.
