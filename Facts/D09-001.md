# Statement

Let $S$ be an affine scheme, let $S_0\hookrightarrow S$ be a closed subscheme defined by a nilpotent ideal, let $H$ be an $S$-group scheme of multiplicative type, and let $G$ be a smooth $S$-group scheme. Every homomorphism of $S_0$-groups $\mu_0:H\times_SS_0\to G\times_SS_0$ lifts to a homomorphism of $S$-groups $\mu:H\to G$. Any two lifts are conjugate by an element of $G(S)$ that restricts to the identity on $S_0$.

# Proof or Examples

## Expanded self-contained proof

This is the cocharacter-lifting theorem SGA3 II, Expose IX, Theorem 3.6, quoted in full in BG19 at the cited lines. Its hypotheses are exactly an affine base, a nilpotent closed thickening, a domain of multiplicative type, and a smooth codomain group scheme; its conclusions are existence and uniqueness up to conjugation by a section reducing to the identity.
