# Statement


For the technical developments in the paper, every presheaf over an affine scheme $\Spec R$ is understood on the big affine site over $\Spec R$, and every kernel, cokernel, and image of such presheaves is taken on that big affine site. Quasi-coherent sheaves on this big affine site do not form an abelian category, in contrast with quasi-coherent sheaves on the small Zariski site.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The convention itself needs no proof. The warning that quasi-coherent objects are not an abelian subcategory on the big affine site can be seen explicitly. Let R=k[x] and consider multiplication by x on the quasi-coherent structure presheaf O. Its objectwise kernel K satisfies K(R)=0 because x is a nonzerodivisor in R. After the allowed nonflat base change S=R/(x), however, K(S)=ker(x:S->S)=S. If K were quasi-coherent over Spec R, then K(S) would be K(R) tensor_R S=0, a contradiction. Thus pointwise kernels of maps of quasi-coherent objects need not remain quasi-coherent on the big affine site. On the small Zariski site the relevant restriction maps are flat localizations, so this obstruction does not occur and quasi-coherent sheaves form an abelian category.

The site convention and warning are stated at References/GrobV13/grob-v13.tex:945-956.
