# Statement


Let $F_n/F$ be unramified of degree $n$ prime to $p$, let $M'$ be the universal unramified-twist rank-one etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $F_n$, let $M$ be its descent to $F$, and let $\sigma$ generate $\Gal(F_n/F)$. Put $\dF=[F:\Q_p]$ and $\dkF=[k_{F_{\cyc}}:\F_p]$. There exist cocycles

$$c'_1,\ldots,c'_{\dF+\dkF}\in Z^1_{\Herr,+}(M')$$

such that $\{\sigma^j\cdot c'_i:\sigma^j\in\Gal(F_n/F),\ 1\le i\le\dF+\dkF\}$ is a basis of $H^1_{\Herr,+}(M')$. Moreover, the invariant cocycles

$$c_i:=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\MINOREDITED{\sigma^j}c'_i\qquad(1\le i\le\dF+\dkF)$$

form a basis of $H^1_{\Herr,+}(M)$.

```tex
\[
k_{F_n,\cyc}\cong\F_p[\Gal(k_{F_{n,\cyc}}/\F_p)]
\cong\F_p[\Gal(F_n/F)]^{\oplus \dkF}
\]

\[
H^1_{\Herr,+}(M^{\prime})
\cong
\bFp[t^{\pm1}][\Gal(F_n/F)]^{\oplus \dF+\dkF}

as a $\bFp[t^{\pm 1}][\Gal(F_n/F)]$-module.
```

### Basis and descent argument

Put $R=\overline{\mathbb F}_p[t^{\pm1}]$, $\Delta_n=\operatorname{Gal}(F_n/F)$, and $r=d_F+d_{k_F}$. Assuming D02-004, choose one $R[\Delta_n]$-basis class in each of the $r$ regular summands of $H^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M^{\prime})$, and choose cocycle representatives $c_1^{\prime},\ldots,c_r^{\prime}$. The classes of $\{\sigma^j c_i^{\prime}:0\leq j<n,1\leq i\leq r\}$ are then an $R$-basis.

For the regular module, $R[\Delta_n]^{\Delta_n}$ is freely generated by the norm element $N=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\sigma^j$. Hence the invariant submodule of $H^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M^{\prime})$ has basis $[Nc_i^{\prime}]$. Termwise unramified descent of the modified Herr complex, together with prime-to-$p$ exactness of invariants as in F04-011, identifies this invariant cohomology with $H^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M)$. Each $c_i=Nc_i^{\prime}$ is already an invariant cocycle, so these cocycles represent the asserted basis downstairs.
