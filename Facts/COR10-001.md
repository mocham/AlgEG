# Statement


Let \(F=\mathbb Q_p\), let \(\mathbf G\) be a split connected reductive group over \(\overline{\mathbb F}_p\) with connected center and root system of type \(F_4\), and assume \(p>12\). Fix a Borel subgroup \(\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T\), let \(\Phi^+\) be its positive roots, number the simple roots in Bourbaki order, and write \(\sum_i n_i\delta_i\) as the digit string \(n_1n_2n_3n_4\). Let \(\mathcal X_{\mathbf G,\mathrm{red}}^{\mathrm{EG}}\) be the reduced Emerton--Gee stack and let \(\mathcal W_{\mathbf B}\) be the mod-\(p\) Borel-valued Weil--Deligne stack.

Then \(\mathcal X_{\mathbf G,\mathrm{red}}^{\mathrm{EG}}\) is equidimensional of dimension 24, and its irreducible components are in bijection with \(\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal W_{\mathbf B})\). They are the pairwise distinct closures of the usual full maximally nonsplit Borel images.

The possible exceptional reductive images from non-full top-dimensional Borel sources are indexed by the Serre-component realizations
\[
Z\in\coprod_{j=\mathrm I}^{\mathrm {IV}}\operatorname{Irr}_{24}(S_j)
\]
of the following four configuration types from COR18-001:

1. \(\Phi^+\setminus\Phi_K=\{0010\}\), \(\Psi_0=\{0010\}\), and \(\Psi_2=\{0001,0011,0100,0110,0120\}\);
2. \(\Phi^+\setminus\Phi_K=\{0010\}\), \(\Psi_0=\{0010\}\), and \(\Psi_2=\{0001,0011,0100,0110,0120,1000\}\);
3. \(\Phi^+\setminus\Phi_K=\{1000,0010\}\), \(\Psi_0=\{0010,1000\}\), and \(\Psi_2=\{0001,0011,0100,0110,0120,1100,1110,1120\}\);
4. \(\Phi^+\setminus\Phi_K=\{1000,0001,0011,0010\}\), \(\Psi_0=\{0001,0010,0011,1000\}\), and \(\Psi_2=\{0100,0110,0111,0120,0121,0122,1100,1110,1111,1120,1121,1122\}\).

These are four root-configuration types, not four individual components or images: every Serre-indexed realization \(Z\) is included. Each such reductive image has the positive-root stabilizer dimension drop and is contained, by componentwise rotation, in the closure of a usual full maximally nonsplit image. Therefore none is an additional irreducible component of the reductive stack.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put \(D=\#\Phi^+=24\). COR19-002 applies the finite disjoint-union scheme-image argument and the corrected \(F_4\) inputs to every irreducible reductive component. It proves that the reductive stack is equidimensional of dimension \(D\), that every component is a full maximally nonsplit Borel image, and that these images exhaust the stack.

For distinct \(C,C'\in\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal W_{\mathbf B})\), choose pairwise disjoint component-generic opens \(C^\circ\) and \((C')^\circ\). COR15-003 gives the corresponding component-indexing full-unipotent loci, and COR5-001 gives singleton fibers on their regular maximally nonsplit opens. If their reductive image closures agreed, the two constructible images would meet at a component-generic reductive point and singleton fibers would identify Borel-valued preimages carrying labels in both disjoint opens. This is impossible. Hence the usual images are pairwise distinct, and COR19-002 gives their exhaustivity.

COR18-001 exhausts the non-full top-dimensional Borel sources by all Serre-indexed realizations of rows I--IV and gives the exact count of those realizations. In every row, \(\Psi_0=\Phi^+\setminus\Phi_K\). COR2-001 therefore subtracts the positive number \(\#\Psi_0\) from the reductive image dimension. Independently, COR8-002 applies to each Serre-indexed realization and rotates a dense locus into a usual maximally nonsplit Borel locus without changing its reductive image. Thus every exceptional image is contained in a usual component closure and no exceptional Borel component creates a reductive component. The displayed list is consequently a four-type classification of possible exceptional images, never an exact-four component assertion.

## Post-proof remarks

Verified unconditionally for \(F_4/\mathbb Q_p\). COR19-002 supplies global coverage and ambient equidimensionality, so the former generic-coverage/equidimensionality premise and the use of D10-001 have both been removed. COR15-003 and COR5-001 retain the component-generic proof that the usual images are distinct. COR18-001 quantifies all Serre-indexed realizations of four types, COR2-001 gives their image drop, and COR8-002 gives componentwise containment by rotation. The result classifies possible exceptional images by Serre-indexed realizations of four types; it does not call four types four individual components.
