# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $R=\overline{\mathbb F}_p[t^{\pm1}]$, and let $M$ be the universal unramified-twist rank-one etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module on a component whose specialization at $t=1$ is the mod-$p$ cyclotomic character. Put $u=t-1$. Then
\[H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M,R)\cong R/(u).\]

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

By D02-002, the geometric fiber of $H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M,R)$ is one-dimensional at $t=1$ and vanishes at every other point. COR8-006 makes this a finite $R$-module and identifies its fibers by top-degree base change. Since $R$ is a PID, Smith normal form therefore reduces it to one summand $R/(u^m)$ for some $m\geq1$. The normalized universal-twist calculation in COR4-001 identifies the cyclotomic crossing in the ordinary Herr complex with the degree-one-to-degree-two block $[R\xrightarrow{t-1}R]$: the universal unramified character's Frobenius coordinate is $t$, and the cyclotomic specialization is normalized to $t=1$. Its degree-two cokernel is exactly $R/(t-1)$, while all other degree-two blocks are invertible. Hence $m=1$ and $H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M,R)\cong R/(u)$.
