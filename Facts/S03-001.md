# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, put $k_0=k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}$, let $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_p$, and let $R=k[t^{\pm1}]$. Write $\mathbf E_F=k_0((T_F))$ with its faithful field-of-norms action of $\Gamma\cong\mathbb Z_p$, and form $\mathbf E_{F,R}=\left(\varprojlim_n(\mathbf E_F^+/T_F^n)\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}R\right)[1/T_F]$, with $\Gamma$ acting trivially on $R$. Then $\mathbf E_{F,R}^{\Gamma}=R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_0$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put $A=k\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_0$. Since $k_0/\mathbb F_p$ is finite and $k$ is algebraically closed, $A$ is a finite product of copies of $k$. The completed coefficient-ring definition identifies $\mathbf E_{F,R}$ with $A[t^{\pm1}]\bigl((T_F)\bigr)$, component by component. The field-of-norms action fixes $A[t^{\pm1}]$.

First, $k((T_F))^{\Gamma}=k$. Indeed, choose a topological generator $\gamma$ of $\Gamma$; faithfulness makes $\gamma$ an automorphism of infinite order. If a nonconstant $z\in k((T_F))$ were fixed, then, after subtracting its residue when necessary, it would give a nonzero Laurent series $y$ of nonzero valuation. The extension $k((T_F))/k((y))$ is finite, as follows from the usual valuation-degree argument for one-variable Laurent-series fields. But $\gamma$ would then be an infinite-order element of the finite automorphism group of that extension, a contradiction.

Now let $x\in A[t^{\pm1}]\bigl((T_F)\bigr)$ be $\Gamma$-fixed. For every $a\in k^\times$, specialize $t$ to $a$ and project to each factor of $A$. The resulting element of $k((T_F))$ is fixed, hence lies in $k$. Therefore every coefficient of $T_F^m$ with $m\ne0$ is a Laurent polynomial in $t$ vanishing at every $a\in k^\times$, and so is zero. Thus $x\in A[t^{\pm1}]=R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_0$. The reverse inclusion is fixed pointwise, proving the equality.
