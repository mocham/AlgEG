# Statement


Let $k$ be a field of characteristic different from $2$. The hypersurface $X=\operatorname{Spec}k[x]/(x^2-1)$ has full-rank Jacobian at every geometric point and is smooth over $k$, but it is reducible after base change to an algebraic closure. Consequently, full rank of a defining Jacobian can prove smoothness but does not by itself prove irreducibility of a scheme or of fibers in a family.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The derivative of $x^2-1$ is $2x$. At a geometric zero one has $x=1$ or $x=-1$, so $2x$ is nonzero and the one-by-one Jacobian has rank one. On the other hand, over an algebraic closure, $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)$ and $X$ is the disjoint union of two reduced points.
