# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite. Let $\overline\rho_d:G_K\to\mathrm{GL}_d(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$ have a crystalline lift $\rho_d^\circ$ of regular labeled Hodge--Tate weights $\underline\lambda$, and let $X$ be the irreducible component of its crystalline lifting space containing $\rho_d^\circ$. Let
\[
0\longrightarrow\overline\rho_d\longrightarrow\overline\rho_{d+a}\longrightarrow\overline\alpha\longrightarrow0
\]
be an extension with $\overline\alpha$ irreducible. Let $\alpha^\circ$ be a crystalline lift of $\overline\alpha$ whose labeled Hodge--Tate weights are separated above those of $\underline\lambda$ so that
\[
\operatorname{Hom}_{G_K}(\rho_x^\circ,\alpha^\circ(1))=0
\]
for every characteristic-zero point $x\in X$.

Then the residual extension lifts to a crystalline extension
\[
0\longrightarrow\theta_d^\circ\longrightarrow\theta_{d+a}^\circ\longrightarrow\alpha^\circ\longrightarrow0,
\]
where $\theta_d^\circ$ has weights $\underline\lambda$ and lies on $X$. If $\rho_d^\circ$ and $\alpha^\circ$ are potentially diagonalizable, then $\theta_{d+a}^\circ$ is potentially diagonalizable.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let $R$ be the coordinate ring of $X$ and let $C^\bullet$ be a perfect three-term complex computing
\[
\operatorname{Ext}_{G_K}^\bullet(\alpha^\circ,\rho_R^\circ)
\]
for the universal crystalline representation on $X$. Local Tate duality identifies the top fiber with
\[
\operatorname{Ext}_{G_K}^2(\alpha^\circ,\rho_x^\circ)
\cong
\operatorname{Hom}_{G_K}(\rho_x^\circ,\alpha^\circ(1))^\vee.
\]
The separation hypothesis therefore makes $H^2(C^\bullet)$ set-theoretically supported on the special fiber. For regular crystalline weights, the cited codimension theorem for the $H^2$ jump loci gives exactly the jump-codimension hypothesis of F01-006.

The residual extension is a class in the degree-one cohomology of the special fiber of $C^\bullet$. Apply F01-006. It supplies an integral point of $X$ and a degree-one class over that point reducing to the given residual class. The integral point defines $\theta_d^\circ$, and the lifted class defines the displayed extension by $\alpha^\circ$. Because the two weight ranges are separated, the usual local-duality calculation gives $H^1_f=H^1$, so every such extension is crystalline. The construction stays on $X$, proving the component assertion.

If $\rho_d^\circ$ is potentially diagonalizable, every crystalline point on the same component, including $\theta_d^\circ$, is potentially diagonalizable. A crystalline extension of potentially diagonalizable subrepresentation and quotient is potentially diagonalizable after a common finite base extension, so the final assertion follows.
