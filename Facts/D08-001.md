# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite. There is a finite set of regular labeled Hodge types $\{\underline\lambda_s:s\in S\}$ such that the special fiber of $$\bigcup_{s\in S}\mathcal X_{\operatorname{GL}_2}^{\underline\lambda_s,\tau_{\mathrm{triv}}}$$ contains every irreducible component of the reduced $\operatorname{GL}_2$ Emerton-Gee stack $\mathcal X_{2,\mathrm{red}}$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The reduced $\operatorname{GL}_2$ Emerton-Gee stack is of finite presentation and has finitely many irreducible components. For each component choose a finite-type point in its nonempty open locus disjoint from the other components. MAIN-002 supplies a regular crystalline lift of that point, of some labeled Hodge type $\underline\lambda$ and trivial inertial type. By F05-023, the corresponding fixed-type crystalline image is closed in the special fiber and contains the chosen point. By F01-005, it is equidimensional of the same dimension as an irreducible component of $\mathcal X_{2,\mathrm{red}}$. The fixed-type component through the chosen point is therefore a closed equal-dimensional subset of the unique ambient component through that point, hence equals it. Retaining one Hodge type for each of the finitely many ambient components proves the assertion.
