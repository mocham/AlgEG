# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, put $d_F=[F:\mathbb Q_p]$, and let
$d_{k_F}$ be the residue-field contribution to the rank of the
root-valued Herr $1$-cocycle module. Let
$\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$ be a split pinned Borel with
$p>h_{\mathbf G}$, fix a height-compatible root order and an
irreducible torus component $C$, and fix a positive root $\alpha$.
Let $X_{<\alpha}(C)$ and $X_{\leq\alpha}(C)$ denote the proposed
successive destackified root charts. On $X_{<\alpha}(C)$ form the
complete obstruction $D_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}$ and choose a lift
$A_\alpha$ of its coefficient relative to a chosen standard
obstruction class. Put

$$r_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}
=s_\alpha(t_\alpha-1)+A_\alpha.$$

Then the proposed corrected recursive presentation is

$$X_{\leq\alpha}(C)\cong
\begin{cases}
X_{<\alpha}(C)[s_\alpha]/(r_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}})
\times\mathbb A^{d_F+d_{k_F}},
& C_\alpha\text{ meets the Steinberg component},\\
X_{<\alpha}(C)\times\mathbb A^{d_F+d_{k_F}},
&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}$$

The assertion is only a one-stage presentation. It makes no claim
that the hypersurface is irreducible and no global torsor claim.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put $S=X_{<\alpha}(C)$, $u=t_\alpha-1$, and $d=d_F+d_{k_F}$. We prove the assertion as an isomorphism of functors over $S$, rather than from geometric points.

In the height-$\operatorname{ht}(\alpha)$ quotient, $U_{\alpha^\vee}$ is central in the unipotent radical and the torus acts on it through the root character. After fixing an $S$-scheme $T$, the pulled-back lower cocycle, and its existing destackifying data, a lift to the next stage is therefore a root-valued modified-Herr $1$-cochain. Its root projection has the form

$$d^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(z_\alpha)+D_\alpha^{\mathrm{BCH}}=0.$$

Root-valued degree-zero gauges act through the same modified-Herr differential as in rank one. Centrality also implies that the difference of two lifts over the same lower object is a homogeneous rank-one cocycle, and that the difference of two gauge identifications is a homogeneous rank-one coboundary. These statements commute with every base change $T'\to T$. Consequently the groupoid of next-stage lifts is an affine translate of the homogeneous rank-one destackified cocycle groupoid; this conclusion concerns the already defined relative chart and does not assert that the chart covers the original stack.

F02-019 identifies that homogeneous rank-one functor, functorially after base change along $t\mapsto t_\alpha$. At a Steinberg crossing it has the exhaustive coordinates

$$x_{\alpha,1},\ldots,x_{\alpha,d},s_\alpha$$

and the sole homogeneous relation $s_\alpha u=0$; its proof includes the degree-zero gauge quotient, so these are coordinates on the represented functor and not merely parameters for a set of points. Away from a crossing its root-cocycle part is the free affine $d$-space.

COR14-006 constructs from the lower universal family a base-change-compatible correction cochain containing every BCH class through height $\operatorname{ht}(\alpha)$. At a crossing, its residual degree-two class is $A_\alpha[b_{\alpha,\mathrm{std}}]$. Adding $s_\alpha c_{\alpha,\mathrm{std}}$ changes that coefficient by $s_\alpha u$, while the $d$ free cocycles have zero differential. It follows at once that the displayed universal cochain gives a natural morphism

$$S[s_\alpha]/(s_\alpha u+A_\alpha)\times\mathbb A^d\longrightarrow X_{\leq\alpha}(C).$$

This morphism is exhaustive. Indeed, for any $T\to S$ and any next-stage lift, subtract the correction cochain. The existence of the lift says that $A_\alpha[b_{\alpha,\mathrm{std}}]$ vanishes after pullback to $T$. The rank-one two-term summand used in F02-019 says precisely that this is equivalent to the existence of $s_\alpha$ with $s_\alpha u+A_\alpha=0$. After subtracting $s_\alpha c_{\alpha,\mathrm{std}}$, the remaining difference is a homogeneous cocycle and hence is represented by the $d$ free coordinates of F02-019. If two such coordinate tuples determine isomorphic lifts, their difference is a homogeneous rank-one gauge relation, so injectivity follows from the same rank-one isomorphism. Thus the natural morphism is an isomorphism of functors. In the non-Steinberg case COR14-006 has no residual degree-two class; its correction cochain exists unconditionally, and the identical difference argument leaves exactly the affine $d$-space.

There are no further equations: all lower-root equations already define $S$, the central extension adds only its root-$\alpha$ cocycle equation, COR14-006 computes that equation with the complete BCH series, and F02-019 gives an exhaustive homogeneous chart with no other relations. Finally, replacing $A_\alpha$ by $A_\alpha+uq$ is carried by the triangular translation $s_\alpha\mapsto s_\alpha-q$ from COR14-006, so the presentation is independent of the coefficient lift and glues functorially. This proves only the stated one-stage presentation; it proves neither irreducibility nor that the recursive pseudo-torsors are genuine global torsors.

## Post-proof remarks

Verified by a unit-local representability argument. The cited source range states only that the corollary follows from its equation lemma, so the missing step was supplied explicitly: centrality of the next root subgroup makes the lift groupoid an affine translate of the homogeneous rank-one modified-Herr groupoid. F02-019 handles the degree-zero gauge quotient and gives the exhaustive free coordinates and the two-term Steinberg relation; COR14-006 supplies the functorial complete-BCH translation and its triangular coefficient-lift change. The proof works after arbitrary base change, proves surjectivity and injectivity of the coordinate functor, and shows that the central root equation is the only new equation. It does not infer hypersurface irreducibility, fppf-local nonemptiness of the recursive pseudo-torsors, a global torsor, or a final smooth atlas. No auxiliary COR16-002 is needed.
