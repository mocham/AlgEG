# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $\bB=U\rtimes\bT$ be a split Borel with $p>h_{\bG}$, and let $\BK\subset U^{\bM}$ be stable under $\bT\langle w\rangle$, where the relative roots split over an unramified extension $F_n/F$. Let $C$ be an irreducible component of the rootwise Weil-Deligne parameter space and set $R=\mathcal O_C$. For each relative root $\Balpha$, choose the Galois-averaged level-one coordinate cocycles and, when $H^2_{\Herr}(U_{\Balpha}(\bfE_{F,R}))$ is nonzero, a standard cocycle $b_{\Balpha,\std}$ whose class generates that cyclic $R$-module. Define $$J_{\Balpha}:=\operatorname{Ann}_R([b_{\Balpha,\std}]).$$ Recursively, for each relative-root factor $$B=[c_{\Bbeta_1,\xi_1},\ldots,c_{\Bbeta_s,\xi_s}]_{\Balpha}\in Z^2_{\Herr}(U_{\Balpha}(\bfE_{F,R})),$$ proceed as follows. If $H^2_{\Herr}=0$, choose $c_{\Balpha,[\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_s]}$ with $B=-d^1_{\Herr}c_{\Balpha,[\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_s]}$. If $H^2_{\Herr}$ is nonzero, the class of $B$ determines a unique coefficient $$\bar a_{\Balpha,[\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_s]}\in R/J_{\Balpha}.$$ Choose any lift $a_{\Balpha,[\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_s]}\in R$ and a cochain $c_{\Balpha,[\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_s]}$ satisfying $$B=a_{\Balpha,[\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_s]}b_{\Balpha,\std}-d^1_{\Herr}c_{\Balpha,[\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_s]}.$$ Adjoin the resulting labels and cochains at each level through $h_{\bG}$; these chosen cochains are the twisted gauge cochains. The lifts $a\in R$ and cochains are auxiliary choices, while their residue classes modulo $J_{\Balpha}$ are canonical. At a simple Steinberg crossing with $J_{\Balpha}=(u_{\Balpha})$, where $u_{\Balpha}$ is a nonzerodivisor and $d(c_{\Balpha,\std})=u_{\Balpha}b_{\Balpha,\std}$, replacing finitely many lifts $a_j$ by $a_j+u_{\Balpha}q_j$ changes an obstruction equation $s u_{\Balpha}+\sum_j a_jm_j$ only by the triangular translation $s\mapsto s-\sum_jq_jm_j$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Over the unramified splitting extension, COR4-001 and the normal-basis decomposition identify the modified Herr $H^1$ module with a direct sum of regular $R[\operatorname{Gal}(F_n/F)]$-modules. Choose cocycle representatives of regular-module generators. Their Galois orbits give the required upstairs basis, and F04-011 identifies the invariant norm cocycles with the downstairs classes. This supplies the Galois-averaged level-one coordinate cocycles used to start the recursion.

The relative-root factor $B$ is a degree-two cocycle. If its $H^2$ vanishes, it is a boundary and the first choice exists. Otherwise the chosen generator induces an isomorphism $R/J_{\Balpha}\xrightarrow{\sim}H^2_{\Herr}$, so $[B]$ has one unique coefficient class $\bar a$. Any lift $a$ has $[B-ab_{\Balpha,\std}]=0$, hence $B-ab_{\Balpha,\std}$ is a boundary and a cochain $c$ exists with the displayed sign convention. Finiteness follows from the finite relative root system and the bounded decomposition lengths. D09-003 proves the stated change-of-lift formula in the principal simple-crossing case. Thus the recursion is well-defined as a system of choices without the false assertion that a lift in $R$ is unique.
