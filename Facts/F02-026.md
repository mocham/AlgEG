# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be finite, put $d_F=[F:\Q_p]$, let $F_{\cyc}=F(\mu_{p^\infty})^{\Delta_F}$ with residue field $k_{F_{\cyc}}$, and put $d_{k_F}=[k_{F_{\cyc}}:\F_p]$. Let $\bB=U\rtimes\bT$ be the Borel of a split pinned reductive group $\bG$ with $p>h_{\bG}$, fix a height-compatible positive-root order, fix a Weil-Deligne component $C$, and let $X_{<\alpha}(C)\subset X_{\leq\alpha}(C)$ be two successive recursive destackified root charts. If every irreducible component of $X_{<\alpha}(C)$ has dimension at least $d$, then every irreducible component of $X_{\leq\alpha}(C)$ has dimension at least $d+d_F+d_{k_F}$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Put $N=d_F+d_{k_F}$. By COR14-007 there are two cases. Away from the Steinberg component, \[X_{\leq\alpha}(C)\cong X_{<\alpha}(C)\times\mathbb A^N,\] so every irreducible component has dimension at least $d+N$.

In the Steinberg case, \[X_{\leq\alpha}(C)\cong\bigl(X_{<\alpha}(C)\times\mathbb A^1_{s_\alpha}\bigr)\cap V(r_\alpha)\times\mathbb A^N.\] Every component of $X_{<\alpha}(C)\times\mathbb A^1$ has dimension at least $d+1$. Applying D02-005 to the single equation $r_\alpha$ shows that every component of its zero locus has dimension at least $d$; this remains valid if $r_\alpha$ is a zero divisor or vanishes on an ambient component. The final factor $\mathbb A^N$ raises every component dimension by $N$, giving the required lower bound $d+d_F+d_{k_F}$.
