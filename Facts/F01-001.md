# Statement


Throughout the paper, let $F/\Q_p$ be a finite extension and let $\bG$ be a connected reductive group with connected center. Let $h_{\bG}$ denote the Coxeter number of $\bG$, and assume $p>h_{\bG}$. This inequality is in force throughout the paper to enable the Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula and the truncated maps $\log_{\le h_{\bG}}$ and $\exp_{\le h_{\bG}}$.
