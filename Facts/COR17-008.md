# Statement


Let \(K/\mathbb Q_p\) be finite, let \((G,B,T)\) be a pinned quasi-split connected reductive group over \(K\) that splits over a finite extension \(L/K\), and let
\[
{}^LP=P\rtimes\operatorname{Gal}(L/K),\qquad P=U\rtimes\check M,
\]
be a Galois-stable standard parabolic. Assume that the reductive Levi datum is tame; in particular, this holds in the split setting. For a \(P^-\)-dominant Hodge type \(\lambda\) and an inertial type \(\tau\), both valued in \({}^LP\), the fixed-type potentially semistable stack \(\mathcal X_{K,{}^LP}^{\tau,\lambda}\) exists as a Noetherian formal algebraic stack flat over \(\operatorname{Spf}\overline{\mathbb Z}_p\). It is the unique flat closed substack characterized by its fixed-type points over finite flat coefficient algebras. No hypothesis \(p>3\) is required in this tame case.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Lin25, Lemma 10(5), constructs the fixed-type stack for a tame reductive group and proves that it is a p-adic formal algebraic stack of finite type. For a general parabolic \(P=U\rtimes\check M\), use the final paragraph of the proof of Theorem 10: project \({}^LP\to{}^LM\), base change the ambient parabolic Emerton--Gee stack along the fixed-type Levi stack, and take the \(\overline{\mathbb Z}_p\)-flat quotient. Corollary 6 gives the required finite-flat point characterization, while Definition 5 gives uniqueness.

This parabolic step does not use \(p>3\). In Theorem 10, that inequality is used only in the preceding reductive reduction, where an arbitrarily ramified simply connected cover is expressed through Weil restrictions of tame groups. When the reductive Levi is already tame, Lemma 10(5) supplies that input directly and the rest of the proof applies verbatim.
