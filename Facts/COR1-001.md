# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, put $d_F=[F:\mathbb Q_p]$, let $F_{\mathrm{cyc}}=F(\mu_{p^\infty})^{\Delta_F}$, and put $d_{k_F}=[k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}:\mathbb F_p]$. Let $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_p$ and $R=k[t^{\pm1}]$. Fix a rank-one mod-$p$ character $\bar\chi$ of $G_F$, and let $M$ be its universal unramified-twist rank-one etale $(\varphi,\Gamma)$-module over $\mathbf E_{F,R}$: for each $a\in k^\times$, the fiber $M_a$ is the twist of $\bar\chi$ by the unramified character whose chosen Frobenius value is $a$. For every finite-type $R$-algebra $S$, form $C^\bullet_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M,S)$ by the modified-Herr definition, using the constant-term differential when $\bar\chi|_{I_F}=1$ and the ramified differential otherwise. Then $C^\bullet_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M,S)$ is perfect and satisfies
\[
H^0_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M,S)=0,\qquad H^2_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M,S)=H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M,S).
\]
Moreover, the kernel of the natural universal-coefficient map
\[
H^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M,S)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M,R),S)
\]
is a finite free $S$-module of rank $d_F+d_{k_F}$, functorially in $S$.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let $P^\bullet$ be a finite-projective $R$-complex representing the ordinary Herr complex and compatible with finite-type base change. The modified complex adds the finite free module $R\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}$ in degree one and changes only the degree-zero differential. It is therefore perfect and compatible with the same base changes. Its degree-one differential ignores the added summand, so its degree-two cohomology is the ordinary $H^2$.

We next prove the degree-zero vanishing over every finite-type $R$-algebra. If $\bar\chi|_{I_F}\ne1$, choose inertia on which it has value $u\ne1$. The scalar $u-1\in k^\times$ is a unit after every base change, so there are no invariants. If $\bar\chi|_{I_F}=1$, every ordinary degree-zero class lies in the Gamma-invariant constant submodule, and the added constant-term component of $d^0_{\mathrm{Herr},+}$ is the identity on that submodule. Its kernel is again zero.

Write $P_+^\bullet$ for a finite-projective representative of the modified complex over $R$. Since $R$ is a PID, the universal-coefficient sequence in total degree zero gives, for every residue field $\kappa$ of $R$,
\[
0\longrightarrow H^0(P_+^\bullet)\otimes_R\kappa\longrightarrow H^0(P_+^\bullet\otimes_R\kappa)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(H^1(P_+^\bullet),\kappa)\longrightarrow0.
\]
The middle term vanishes by the preceding paragraph. Hence $H^0(P_+^\bullet)=0$ and the finite $R$-module $H^1(P_+^\bullet)$ is torsion-free, so it is finite free.

For all but at most one $a\in k^\times$, the character of $M_a$ is not cyclotomic. Local Tate duality then gives $H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M_a,k)=0$. Top-degree base change shows that the finite module $H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M,R)$ has rank zero. The ordinary local Euler characteristic is $-d_F$, while adjoining a rank-$d_{k_F}$ module in degree one changes it to $-(d_F+d_{k_F})$. Thus $H^1(P_+^\bullet)$ has rank $d_F+d_{k_F}$. Finally, the universal-coefficient sequence in total degree one is
\[
0\longrightarrow H^1(P_+^\bullet)\otimes_RS\longrightarrow H^1_{\mathrm{Herr},+}(M,S)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(M,R),S)\longrightarrow0.
\]
Its left term is $S^{d_F+d_{k_F}}$, proving the assertion. A constant scalar extension of one exceptional character is not a universal unramified-twist family: its $H^2$ may have positive generic rank. The calculation in C01-003 records exactly that excluded behavior.
