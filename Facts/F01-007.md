# Statement


Let $F/\Q_p$ be a finite extension, let $\bG$ be a connected reductive group, and let $P=U^{\bM}\rtimes\bM$ be a parabolic subgroup with Levi factor $\bM$ and unipotent radical $U^{\bM}$. Let $\rho:\Gal_F\to\bM(\bar\Q_p)$ be potentially semistable with $P^{-}$-dominant Hodge type, and give $\Lie U^{\bM}(\bar\Q_p)$ its $\Gal_F$-action through $\operatorname{ad}\rho$. If $\rho':\Gal_F\to P(\bar\Q_p)$ satisfies $\rho'\times^P\bM=\rho$, then $\rho'$ is potentially semistable of the same inertial type as $\rho$, and its possible Weil--Deligne types are parameterized by $H^2(\Gal_F,U^{\bM}(\bar\Q_p))$.

## Expanded self-contained proof

### Proof

A potentially semistable representation is de Rham, so F09-002 applies to rho. It shows that every P-valued extension rho-prime is de Rham and that its possible Weil-Deligne types are parameterized by H^2(Gal_F,U^M(Qpbar)). By the p-adic monodromy theorem, rho-prime is therefore potentially semistable.

It remains to compare inertial types. Choose a finite quotient J of inertia through which the inertial Weil representations factor. After fixing the inertial type tau:J -> M(Qpbar) of rho, the inertial type of rho-prime has the form j |-> c(j)tau(j), where c is a nonabelian 1-cocycle of J with values in U^M(Qpbar) under the tau-action. Filter U^M by a central series whose successive quotients are vector groups. For every such quotient V, H^i(J,V)=0 for i>0 because |J| is invertible in Qpbar and averaging is available. Induction along the central series therefore gives H^1(J,U^M(Qpbar))=1. Thus c is a coboundary, so the inertial type of rho-prime is U^M-conjugate to the inclusion of tau. This is precisely equality of the inertial types.
