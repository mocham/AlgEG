# Statement


Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let $(G,B,T)$ be split pinned reductive with dual pinned group $(\mathbf G,\mathbf B,\mathbf T)$; assume $p>h_{\mathbf G}$, where $h_{\mathbf G}$ is the Coxeter number. Let $U$ be the unipotent radical of $\mathbf B$, write $\Phi^+=\Phi(\mathbf B,\mathbf T)^+$, let $U_h$ be the descending-central, equivalently root-height, filtration of $U$, put $F_{\mathrm{cyc}}=F(\mu_{p^\infty})^{\Delta_F}$, and let $k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}$ be its residue field. Choose any total ordering of $\Phi^+$ compatible with height and recursively construct the rootwise additive-torsor charts. Then the final morphism
\[
X_{\mathbf B}\longrightarrow\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}
\]
is smooth, surjective, and affine, and every geometric two-fiber is isomorphic to
\[
\operatorname{Res}_{k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}/\mathbb F_p}U\rtimes\mathbf T.
\]
For every integer $h$, the chart obtained after all roots of height at most $h$ have been processed is canonically independent of their ordering within each height. Its accumulated unipotent group is $U/U_{h+1}$. In particular, the final chart is independent of the chosen height-compatible ordering. No ordering-independence assertion is made for a stage indexed by one individual root before all roots of that height have been processed.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

By the verified universal Laurent-family construction in F02-002, the step attached to each positive root $\alpha$ is a genuine torsor under the smooth affine group $\operatorname{Res}_{k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}/\mathbb F_p}U_{\alpha^\vee}$, and its torsor identity commutes with arbitrary coefficient base change. Consequently every rootwise chart map is smooth, surjective, and affine, and these properties are preserved by base change and composition. Starting from the torus atlas and processing all positive roots gives a final geometric two-fiber equal to the iterated semidirect product of $\mathbf T$ with the Weil restrictions of all root groups. The pinned root decomposition identifies this group with $\operatorname{Res}_{k_{F_{\mathrm{cyc}}}/\mathbb F_p}U\rtimes\mathbf T$, proving the final atlas conclusions.

For a fixed height $h$, the root-height commutator relations give $[U_h,U]\subseteq U_{h+1}$. Hence $U_h/U_{h+1}$ is central in $U/U_{h+1}$ and is canonically the product of the root groups of height $h$. The base-change functoriality proved in F02-002 makes the composite chart over this whole layer the torsor obtained from that product; associativity and symmetry of the corresponding fiber products canonically identify the composites for every permutation of the roots in the layer. Once the layer is complete, the accumulated unipotent group is $U/U_{h+1}$. Induction on $h$ proves ordering independence of every completed-height stage and of the final chart.

The qualifier is necessary. In type $A_1\times A_1$, let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be the two height-one roots. Under the order $\alpha<\beta$, the root-indexed quotient $U_{\leq\alpha}$ is $U_{\alpha}$; under the reverse order it is $U_{\alpha}U_{\beta}$. These individual intermediate stages have different dimensions. After both height-one roots are processed, both orders give $U$, exactly as the statement asserts.
