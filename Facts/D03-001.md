# Statement


Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $l$ be a prime, let $E/\mathbb Q_l$ have ring of integers $\mathcal O$ and residue field $\mathbb F$, and let $G$ be a smooth affine group scheme over $\mathcal O$ with reductive geometric fibers. Let $\bar\rho:G_K\to G(\mathbb F)$ be residual, fix an inertial type $\tau$ and, when $l=p$, a p-adic Hodge type $\mathbf v$, and choose a finite Galois extension $L/K$ on whose inertia $\tau$ is trivial. If $x$ is a closed point of $\operatorname{Spec}R_{\bar\rho}^{\square,\tau,\mathbf v}[1/l]$, then the induced morphism from its formal completion to the Weil-Deligne stack, $\operatorname{Spf}\widehat{\mathcal O}_{x}\to G\text{-}\mathrm{WD}_E(L/K)$, is formally smooth.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Let $A_x=\widehat{\mathcal O}_x$. The completed local ring represents framed deformations of the characteristic-zero representation at $x$ with the fixed inertial type and, for $l=p$, fixed p-adic Hodge type (BG19, lines 2971-2976). Forgetting a framing is formally smooth because a lift of the underlying deformation can be framed after lifting a basis through a nilpotent quotient, using smoothness of $G$.

When $l\ne p$, the characteristic-zero equivalence between Galois and Weil-Deligne representations identifies the unframed deformation groupoid with the corresponding Weil-Deligne deformation groupoid. Composing that equivalence with the formally smooth framing map gives formal smoothness of $\operatorname{Spf}A_x\to G\text{-}\mathrm{WD}_E(L/K)$.

Assume $l=p$. The universal fixed-type representation yields an exact tensor-compatible filtered $(\varphi,N,\operatorname{Gal}_{L/K})$-module and hence a morphism from $\operatorname{Spf}A_x$ to the $G$-valued filtered-module groupoid (BG19, lines 2910-2946). Kisin Proposition 3.3.1 proves formal smoothness of this completed fixed-type morphism: for a square-zero extension $B\to B/I$, an admissible filtered module over $B$ gives compatible Galois representations over $B/\mathfrak m_B^i$; formal smoothness of framed deformations lifts these successively, and the fixed-type characterization forces each map to factor through the fixed-type quotient. The proof is reproduced in References/D03/kisin-2008-formal-smoothness-excerpt.txt, lines 39-108. The argument is Tannakian and applies to $G$ because BG19 constructs the filtered objects representation-by-representation, exact and tensor-compatibly, while the framed-to-unframed map is formally smooth.

Finally, forgetting the $G$-tensor filtration is formally smooth. BG19 lines 2562-2600 prove this by lifting a splitting cocharacter across the nilpotent extension; smoothness of the relevant parabolic/flag variety gives a lift, and the possible lifts form the expected tangent torsor. BG19 lines 2235-2241 identify the unfiltered $G$-valued $(\varphi,N,\operatorname{Gal}_{L/K})$ groupoid with $G\text{-}\mathrm{WD}_E(L/K)$. The composite $\operatorname{Spf}A_x\to G\text{-}\mathrm{WD}_E(L/K)$ is therefore formally smooth.
