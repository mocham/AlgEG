# Statement


Let $\bG$ have simple positive roots $\Delta$, let $\bM$ be a Levi subgroup corresponding to $\Delta_{\bM}\subset\Delta$, and write a positive root as $\alpha=\sum_{\delta\in\Delta}n_\delta\delta$. Define its height transverse to $\bM$ and its height internal to $\bM$, respectively, by

$$\Ht^{\bM}(\alpha):=\sum_{\delta\notin\Delta_{\bM}}n_\delta,\qquad \Ht_{\bM}(\alpha):=\sum_{\delta\in\Delta_{\bM}}n_\delta.$$

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

This is a definition, so no proof is required. The two heights partition the usual root height:

  Ht(alpha) = Ht^M(alpha) + Ht_M(alpha).

Both are nonnegative integers. If alpha belongs to the positive root subsystem of M, then Ht^M(alpha)=0 and Ht_M(alpha)=Ht(alpha). If alpha is a positive root outside that subsystem, at least one coefficient outside Delta_M is positive, so Ht^M(alpha)>=1. These checks agree with the later use of Ht^M as the index of the descending central filtration of U^M.
