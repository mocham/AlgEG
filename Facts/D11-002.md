# Statement


Let $\Phi$ be a reduced root system of type $F_4$, $E_6$, $E_7$, $E_8$, or $D_n$ with $4\le n\le10$, with positive roots $\Phi^+$, simple roots $\Delta$, and Coxeter number $h_\Phi$, over a field of characteristic $p>h_\Phi$. For an integer $h$, let $\mathcal B$ be the directed bipartite graph from all height-$h$ positive roots to an arbitrary subset of the height-$(h+1)$ positive roots, with an edge $\beta\to\alpha$ exactly when $\alpha-\beta\in\Delta$. Put
\[
\Delta_{\mathcal B}=\{\alpha-\beta:\beta\to\alpha\}.
\]
For every choice of the target subset, the discriminant variety over $\mathbb G_m^{\Delta_{\mathcal B}}$ defined by the linear equations
\[
\sum_{\beta\to\alpha}N_{\beta,\alpha-\beta}x_{\alpha-\beta}y_\beta=0
\qquad(\alpha\text{ a selected target})
\]
is smooth of relative dimension equal to the number of source roots minus the number of selected target roots.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

For each type and height, the deterministic procedure generates the complete root system and Chevalley constants, enumerates every subset of the target layer, retains the full source layer, and repeatedly eliminates source leaves. A source-leaf equation has unit coefficient $N_{\beta,\alpha-\beta}x_{\alpha-\beta}$ in the standing characteristic range, so this elimination removes one equation and one variable without changing smoothness or relative dimension.

For every nonempty core, the procedure constructs the target-by-source Jacobian matrix and enumerates maximal source-column minors until it finds a nonzero monomial. Since every $x_\delta$ is invertible on the base torus and the recorded integer coefficient is a unit for $p>h_\Phi$, that minor is invertible everywhere. The equations therefore have constant full row rank and define a vector bundle of the asserted relative dimension.

The immutable exceptional certificate exhausts 48, 138, 384, and 1,196 target selections for $F_4,E_6,E_7,E_8$, respectively, with no failures. Its nontrivial minor coefficients have prime support contained in $\{2,3,5\}$, and its SHA-256 manifest passes. Fresh read-only SageMath 10.9 runs of the same exact procedure for $D_4,\ldots,D_{10}$ exhaust respectively 20, 48, 108, 228, 476, 972, and 1,980 target selections, with no failures. The numbers of noncontractible cases are 1, 2, 5, 10, 21, 42, and 85, and every selected monomial minor has coefficient $2$ or $-2$. This proves the finite scope stated here. It does not assert the manuscript's false classification of every $D_n$ core as one six-cycle, nor any uncomputed arbitrary-rank bound.
