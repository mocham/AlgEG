             EXTENSIONS OF DE RHAM GALOIS REPRESENTATIONS

                                                ZHONGYIPAN LIN


       Abstract. For primes p > 3, we construct the parabolic and reductive versions of the integral de
       Rham moduli stacks of Langlands parameters, allowing the group to be arbitrarily ramified. We also
       construct the period morphism from the rigid generic fiber of Borel-valued de Rham moduli stacks to
       the moduli stack of Weil-Deligne representations.
          Furthermore, we formulate the conjecture that the top Chow group of the reduced Emerton-Gee
       stack XLG is isomorphic to that of a certain moduli stack of Weil-Deligne representations. We prove
       this isomorphism in the cases of (1) algebraic tori, and (2) unitary, orthogonal, and symplectic groups.
       Finally, we confirm the conjecture for (3) tame groups when restricted to the cyclotomic-free part of
       the Chow groups.



   The Breuil–Mézard conjecture ([BM02]) expresses the Hilbert-Samuel multiplicities of the special fi-
bre of the de Rham deformation ring in terms of representation-theoretic multiplicities. Gee and Kisin
([GK14]) subsequently employed patching functors to prove the potentially Barsotti-Tate cases of the
conjecture, hinting that the conjecture has a geometrization which furnishes a deformation-theoretic
recipe for the modular weights of residual Galois representations. Emerton and Gee ([EG14], [EG23])
placed this geometric insight on a systematic footing by constructing the Emerton-Gee stacks and
formulating the geometric Breuil-Mézard conjecture for GLn , which consists of a quantitative (numer-
ical) part generalizing the original conjecture of Breuil and Mézard and a qualitative (topological) part
describing the top Chow group of the reduced Emerton-Gee stacks in terms of Serre weights. Very
recently, Feng and Le Hung gave a purely local proof of the geometric conjecture for generic tame iner-
tial types, small Hodge-Tate weights, and unramified groups. They used algebraic local models of the
Emerton-Gee stacks to deduce the numerical conjecture from the decomposition of characteristic cy-
cles on related affine Springer fibres. On the other hand, the topological part of the conjecture bridges
the decomposition pattern on algebraic local models and the decomposition on Galois deformation
rings.
   A precise formulation of the topological Breuil-Mézard conjecture for more general groups is sub-
tle. Indeed, already for PGL2 (on the representation-theoretic side), a Serre weight corresponds to
multiple irreducible components in the reduced Emerton-Gee stacks. Moreover, the combinatorial
parametrization of irreducible components in [EG23] is given by an ad hoc formula, and it is desirable
to have a more conceptual description that is coordinate-free.

2.2. Newton polygon of isocrystals Let K̆ be the p-adic completion of the maximal unramified

positive integer and s is an integer coprime to r. Denote by Dr,s the simple object labeled by the
rational number s/r. Dr,s has dimension r, and we call s/r the slope of Dr,s .
    Let (D, ϕ) be an isocrystal over K0 . Then D̆ = K̆ ⊗K0 D is a direct sum of simple objects Dri ,si . We

Lemma 14. Let X be a reduced rigid analytic Artin stack, and let U ⊂ X be an admissible open
