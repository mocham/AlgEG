Lemma (3.2.1). The morphism of groupoids
\(\operatorname{Mod}_{F,\varphi,N}\to\operatorname{Mod}_{\varphi,N}\),
obtained by forgetting filtrations, is formally smooth.

Let \(A\) be a local Artin ring with maximal ideal \(\mathfrak m_A\), and
\(I\subset A\) an ideal with \(I\mathfrak m_A=0\). Let \(D_{A/I}\) be in
\(\operatorname{Mod}_{F,\varphi,N}(A/I)\) and set
\(D_{A/\mathfrak m_A}=D_{A/I}\otimes_{A/I}A/\mathfrak m_A\).

(1) If \(H_F^2(D_{A/\mathfrak m_A})=0\), then there exists a module
\(D_A\) in \(\operatorname{Mod}_{F,\varphi,N}(A)\) whose reduction modulo
\(I\) is isomorphic to \(D_{A/I}\).

(2) The set of isomorphism classes of liftings of \(D_{A/I}\) to \(D_A\)
in \(\operatorname{Mod}_{F,\varphi,N}(A)\) is either empty or is a torsor
under
\[
H_F^1(D_{A/\mathfrak m_A})\otimes_{A/\mathfrak m_A} I.
\]

Proof. The first claim, regarding formal smoothness, is easily seen, since
if \(A\) is as above, and \(D_A\) is an object of
\(\operatorname{Mod}_{\varphi,N}\) lifting \(D_{A/I}\), then one can
always lift the filtration on \(D_{A/I,L}\) to a filtration by projective
\(L\otimes_{\mathbf Q_p}A\)-modules on \(D_{A,L}\). (This follows from
the smoothness of the corresponding Grassmannian variety, which is well
known.) The claim in (1) follows from this and Proposition (3.1.2)(1),
since \(H_F^2(D_{A/\mathfrak m_A})=H^2(D_{A/\mathfrak m_A})\). The
argument for (2) is similar to that for Proposition (3.1.2)(2).

Proposition (3.3.1). Suppose that the morphism
\(A^\circ\to D_{V_{\mathbb F}}\) is formally smooth. Then the morphism
\[
\widehat A^{\tau,\mathbf v}_{\mathfrak m}
   \longrightarrow \operatorname{Mod}_{F,\varphi,N}
\]
of groupoids on \(E\)-algebras is formally smooth.

Proof. Let \(B\) be an \(E\)-algebra, \(I\subset B\) an ideal with
\(I^2=0\), and
\(h:\widehat A^{\tau,\mathbf v}_{\mathfrak m}\to B/I\) a map of
\(E\)-algebras. Let \(D_{B/I}\) be the object of
\(\operatorname{Mod}_{F,\varphi,N}(B/I)\) induced by \(h\), and let
\(D_B\) in \(\operatorname{Mod}_{F,\varphi,N}(B)\) be an object equipped
with an isomorphism \(D_B\otimes_B B/I\simeq D_{B/I}\). We have to show
that \(D_B\) is induced by a map
\(\widehat A^{\tau,\mathbf v}_{\mathfrak m}\to B\) lifting \(h\).

We may replace \(B/I\) by the image of \(h\), and \(B\) by the preimage
of \(h(\widehat A^{\tau,\mathbf v}_{\mathfrak m})\). Then \(B\) is the
limit of subalgebras \(B'\) which surject onto \(B/I\) and such that
\(I\cap B'\) is a finitely generated \(B/I\)-module. \(D_B\) is induced
from an object of \(\operatorname{Mod}_{F,\varphi,N}(B')\) for one of
these \(B'\), so we may assume that \(I\) is a finitely generated
\(B/I\)-module. In particular, we may assume that \(B\) is a
Noetherian, complete local \(E'\)-algebra, with residue field \(E'\).

Let \(\mathfrak m_B\) denote the maximal ideal of \(B\). If \(i\geq1\),
then \(D_B\otimes_B B/\mathfrak m_B^i\) is in
\(\operatorname{Mod}_{F,\varphi,N}(B/\mathfrak m_B^i)\) and it is a
weakly admissible (and hence admissible) module, because it is a
successive extension of the admissible module
\(D_{B/I}\otimes_{B/I}B/\mathfrak m_B\) [Fo 2, 4.4.4]. Hence one can
associate to \(D_B\otimes_B B/\mathfrak m_B^i\) a finite free
\(B/\mathfrak m_B^i\)-module \(V_{B/\mathfrak m_B^i}\) of rank \(d\),
equipped with a continuous action of \(G_K\). Note that by Corollary
(2.6.2) \(V_{B/\mathfrak m_B^i}\) is of type \(\tau\) and p-adic Hodge
type \(\mathbf v\), since this is true when \(i=1\).

For \(i\geq1\), we now construct maps
\[
\widetilde h_i:\widehat A^{\tau,\mathbf v}_{\mathfrak m}
\longrightarrow B/\mathfrak m_B^i
\]
such that the composite of \(\widetilde h_i\) with the projection
\(B/\mathfrak m_B^i\to B/\mathfrak m_B^{i-1}\) is
\(\widetilde h_{i-1}\), and the composite with
\(B/\mathfrak m_B^i\to B/(\mathfrak m_B^i+I)\) is the reduction of
\(h\) modulo \(\mathfrak m_B^i\). When \(i=1\), we take
\(\widetilde h_1\) to be the reduction of \(h\) modulo \(\mathfrak m_B\).
Given \(\widetilde h_{i-1}\), consider the composite
\[
\widehat A^{\tau,\mathbf v}_{\mathfrak m}
\longrightarrow B/(\mathfrak m_B^{i-1}\cap I)
\longrightarrow B/(\mathfrak m_B^i\cap(\mathfrak m_B^{i-1}+I)),
\tag{3.3.2}
\]
where the first map is the unique morphism which reduces to
\(\widetilde h_{i-1}\) modulo \(\mathfrak m_B^{i-1}\) and to \(h\)
modulo \(I\). We again write \(\mathfrak m\) for the maximal ideal of
\(A\) induced by \(\mathfrak m\). By [Ki 1, 2.3.2, 2.3.3] the formal
smoothness of \(A^\circ\to D_{V_{\mathbb F}}\) implies that the
composite of \(\widehat A_{\mathfrak m}\to
\widehat A^{\tau,\mathbf v}_{\mathfrak m}\) and (3.3.2) lifts to a map
\(\widehat A_{\mathfrak m}\to B/\mathfrak m_B^i\) inducing
\(V_{B/\mathfrak m_B^i}\). Since \(V_{B/\mathfrak m_B^i}\) is of type
\(\tau\) and p-adic Hodge type \(\mathbf v\), this map factors through
\(\widehat A^{\tau,\mathbf v}_{\mathfrak m}\) by Theorem (2.7.6) and
yields the required map \(\widetilde h_i\). Finally, passing to the
limit with \(i\) gives a lifting of \(h\) to \(B\).
