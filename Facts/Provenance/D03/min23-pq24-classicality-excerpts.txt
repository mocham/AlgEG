Sources for the local classicality argument
===========================================

1. Vytautas Paskunas and Julian Quast, "On local Galois deformation
rings: generalised reductive groups", arXiv:2404.14622, Theorem 1.1.

The following is a faithful TeX transcription of the theorem and its
immediate derived-deformation consequence from the introduction.

Let p denote any prime number, let F be a finite extension of Q_p, and
let Gamma_F denote its absolute Galois group. Let L be another finite
extension of Q_p with ring of integers O, uniformiser varpi and residue
field k=O/varpi. Let G be a smooth affine group scheme over O, such that
its neutral component G^0 is reductive and the component group G/G^0 is
a finite group scheme over O. Fix a continuous representation
rho:Gamma_F -> G(k), and let R_rho^square be its universal framed
deformation ring.

Theorem 1.1 (Corollaries 13.27, 15.29). The ring R_rho^square is a local
complete intersection, flat over O and of relative dimension

    dim(G_k)([F:Q_p]+1).

In particular, every continuous representation rho:Gamma_F -> G(k) has
a lift to characteristic zero. Moreover, R_rho^square is reduced and
R_rho^square[1/p] is normal.

Obstruction theory provides a presentation

    R_rho^square = O[[x_1,...,x_r]]/(f_1,...,f_s)

with r equal to the dimension of the tangent space and s equal to
dim_k H^2(Gamma_F,ad rho). The Euler-Poincare characteristic formula
gives

    r-s = dim(G_k)([F:Q_p]+1).

Having the expected dimension implies that varpi,f_1,...,f_s is a
regular sequence and that R_rho^square is a local complete
intersection. It also implies (see Galatius-Venkatesh, Lemma 7.5) that
the derived deformation ring of rho is homotopy discrete, which means
the derived deformation theory of rho does not contain more information
than the usual deformation theory of rho.

2. Yu Min, "Classicality of derived Emerton-Gee stack",
arXiv:2309.05066, Proposition 3.51.

The following is a faithful transcription of the proposition statement
and its three-step proof decomposition. The intervening calculations in
the published preprint identify each displayed step by reducing
finite-type coefficient rings to their completed Artinian local
quotients and then using the homotopy-discreteness result above.

Proposition 3.51. There is an equivalence of functors

    (Lan X_EG)^#_{rho-bar} ~= F_{K,GL_d,rho-bar}.

Proof. By Lemma 3.52, we have
(Lan X_EG)^#_{rho-bar} ~= (Lan X_EG)_{rho-bar}. So we just need to focus
on proving

    (Lan X_EG)_{rho-bar} ~= F_{K,GL_d,rho-bar}.

We proceed in three steps:

1. prove
   (Lan X_EG)_{rho-bar}
   ~= (Lan^cl R^c_{G_K,GL_d/GL_d})_{rho-bar};

2. prove
   (Lan^cl R^c_{G_K,GL_d/GL_d})_{rho-bar}
   ~= R^c_{G_K,GL_d/GL_d,rho-bar};

3. prove
   R^c_{G_K,GL_d/GL_d,rho-bar}
   ~= F_{K,GL_d,rho-bar}.

Summary of the intervening calculations (not part of the quotation):

For Step 1, limit preservation expresses both classical prestacks as
filtered colimits of finite-type affine test objects. Fibres over the
finite residual point decompose through completions at the corresponding
maximal ideals, and those completions are filtered colimits of finite
Artinian local quotients. The equivalence between etale
(phi,Gamma)-modules and Galois representations over finite coefficient
rings identifies the resulting Artinian overcategories.

For Step 2, the framed derived representation prestack is a filtered
colimit of derived affine schemes almost of finite presentation. Its
fibre is pro-corepresented by the completed Artinian local quotients.
The local-complete-intersection theorem and Galatius-Venkatesh Lemma 7.5
identify the left Kan extension of the classical deformation functor
with the derived framed deformation functor. Left Kan extension commutes
with the geometric realization defining the unframed quotient.

Step 3 follows from the definitions: the framed continuous
representation functor is the framed Galois deformation functor, and
geometric realization of its conjugation action groupoid is the
unframed deformation functor.
