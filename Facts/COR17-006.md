# Statement


Let \(F/\mathbb Q_p\) be finite. Let \(\mathbf G\) be a split connected reductive group over \(\overline{\mathbb Z}_p\) with connected center, maximal torus \(\mathbf T\), simple roots \(\Delta\), and Coxeter number \(h_{\mathbf G}\), and assume
\[
p>h_{\mathbf G}.
\]
Let \(\mathbf M\subsetneq\mathbf G\) be the standard Levi defined by \(\Delta_M\subsetneq\Delta\), and let \(U^{\mathbf M}\) be the unipotent radical of the corresponding standard parabolic. Choose a Tits representative \(\dot w\in N_{\mathbf M}(\mathbf T)(\overline{\mathbb Z}_p)\) whose Weyl image \(w\) is elliptic in \(\mathbf M\), and write \(n\) for the order of \(\dot w\). Let \(v:G_F\to\widehat{\mathbb Z}\) be the unramified quotient normalized by arithmetic Frobenius and let \(q_n\) be its reduction modulo \(n\).

Choose a \(w\)-fixed cocharacter \(\mu\in X_*(\mathbf T)\) satisfying
\[
\langle\gamma,\mu\rangle=0\quad(\gamma\in\Delta_M),\qquad
\langle\delta,\mu\rangle=1\quad(\delta\in\Delta\setminus\Delta_M),
\]
and choose \(t\in\mathbf T(\overline{\mathbb Z}_p)^w\) with \(t^n=1\) as follows. For \(\delta\in\Delta\setminus\Delta_M\), let \(m_\delta=\#\langle w\rangle\delta\) and let \(c_\delta\) be the scalar by which \(\operatorname{Ad}(\dot w^{m_\delta})\) acts on the \(\delta\)-root line; require
\[
c_\delta\,\delta(t)^{m_\delta}=1.
\]
With \(\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}:G_F\to\mathbb Z_p^\times\), define the corrected twisted normalizer representation
\[
N_{\mathrm{cyc}}(g)=t^{v(g)}\mu(\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}(g))\dot w^{q_n(g)},
\]
and let \(\underline\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}}\) be its Hodge type.

For a positive root \(\alpha=\sum_{\delta\in\Delta}n_\delta\delta\) in \(U^{\mathbf M}\), put
\[
\operatorname{Ht}^{\mathbf M}(\alpha)=\sum_{\delta\notin\Delta_M}n_\delta.
\]
Let \(U^{\mathbf M}_{\geq h}\) be the relative-height filtration, let
\(U^{\mathbf M}_{1}=U^{\mathbf M}/U^{\mathbf M}_{\geq2}\), and let \(\boldsymbol\Delta\) be the set of \(w\)-orbits of roots of relative height one. An omitted simple root means a simple root \(\delta\in\Delta\setminus\Delta_M\), namely one omitted from the Dynkin subset defining the Levi; its root group lies in \(U^{\mathbf M}\). Assume orbit-normalizability: every member of \(\boldsymbol\Delta\) contains an omitted simple root. For \(\boldsymbol\alpha\in\boldsymbol\Delta\), let \(U_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\) be the product of its root factors in \(U^{\mathbf M}_{1}\).

Let
\[
\mathfrak X_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}^{\underline\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}},\mathrm{tame}}
\]
be the rigid generic fiber of the fixed-Hodge-type, tame-inertial-type potentially semistable stack. On a torus-normalizer destackification with ring \(R\), let \(\mathfrak W\) be the integral formal stack representing
\[
S\longmapsto
\prod_{\boldsymbol\alpha\in\boldsymbol\Delta}
\operatorname{Tor}_1^R\!\left(
H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(U_{\boldsymbol\alpha}(\mathbf A_{F,R})),S
\right),
\]
descended through the unramified degree-\(n\) groupoid, and write \(\mathfrak W_\eta\) for its rigid generic fiber. Then the natural potentially-semistable-to-Weil--Deligne morphism
\[
\mathfrak X_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}^{\underline\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}},\mathrm{tame}}
\longrightarrow \mathfrak W_\eta
\]
induces a canonical bijection of irreducible components.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The cocharacter \(\mu\) gives every radical root \(\alpha\) Hodge--Tate weight
\[
-\langle\alpha,\mu\rangle=-\operatorname{Ht}^{\mathbf M}(\alpha)<0.
\]
Hence the fixed type is \(P^-\)-dominant and F01-007 supplies potential semistability with the fixed inertial type. For an omitted simple root \(\delta\), the finite factor \(t\) cancels the scalar action of \(\dot w^{m_\delta}\); the stabilizer root line is cyclotomic. Orbit-normalizability extends this calculation to every relative-height-one orbit.

Put
\[
P_{\le h}=(U^{\mathbf M}/U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge h+1})\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle,
\qquad
V_h=U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge h}/U^{\mathbf M}_{\ge h+1}.
\]
The commutator formula raises relative height, so
\[
1\longrightarrow V_h\longrightarrow P_{\le h}\longrightarrow P_{\le h-1}\longrightarrow1
\]
is a central vector-group extension. For \(h\ge2\), the dual Tate twist of every root in \(V_h\) has nonzero Hodge--Tate weight \(h-1\). Local Tate duality and Herr base change therefore give \(H^2(V_h)=0\) in the rigid fixed-type family. The nonabelian cohomology sequence
\[
H^1(V_h)\longrightarrow H^1(P_{\le h})\longrightarrow H^1(P_{\le h-1})\longrightarrow H^2(V_h)
\]
shows that every lower-stage object lifts and that its lifts form a torsor under a rigid vector bundle. These geometrically irreducible fibers induce a canonical component bijection from the full source to the first truncation \(P_{\le1}\).

On the first truncation, COR13-001 and COR17-001 identify every universal orbit module on the unramified splitting atlas as
\[
H^2_{\mathrm{Herr}}(U_{\boldsymbol\alpha})
\cong L_{\boldsymbol\alpha}/(u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}-1)L_{\boldsymbol\alpha}
\]
with exponent one. For every coefficient algebra \(S\), its length-one resolution and the perfect Herr complex give the universal-coefficient edge sequence
\[
0\longrightarrow H^1(C_{\boldsymbol\alpha})\otimes_RS
\longrightarrow H^1(C_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\otimes_R^{\mathbf L}S)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(H^2(C_{\boldsymbol\alpha}),S)
\longrightarrow0.
\]
Taking the product over height-one orbits identifies the map from the first truncated potentially semistable stack to \(\mathfrak W_\eta\) as a torsor under the locally free \(H^1\) kernel on the splitting atlas. It is surjective with geometrically irreducible fibers, so it induces a component bijection.

COR16-003 makes this construction equivariant and effective for the finite unramified splitting group, whose order is prime to \(p\). Components of the quotient are the orbits of components upstairs, and the torsor correspondence is equivariant. It therefore descends to the asserted canonical component bijection over \(F\).

## Post-proof remarks

Verified by the direct truncated-parabolic Tor argument. Higher relative-height layers have vanishing top cohomology and contribute only rigid-vector torsors. At relative height one, the universal-coefficient Tor sequence identifies the non-vector quotient with the formal Weil--Deligne target. This replaces the former broad componentwise adaptation of F09-005 and makes clear why only the orbit-normalizable cyclotomic type, not the arbitrary source statement F01-008, is proved.
