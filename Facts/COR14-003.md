# Statement


With explicit descent coordinates, the twisted fine-locus obstruction is the invariant projection of the full absolute-root BCH polynomial and its absolute pairing tensors. Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$ be split over an unramified extension of degree $n$, and let $w$ have order $n$ in the Weyl group, with $p>h_{\mathbf G}$. Let $\mathbf K$ be stable under $\mathbf T\langle w\rangle$, and fix a twisted fine pair.

Assume that the descent automorphism $\sigma$ is given explicitly on every absolute-root cochain module and on a chosen polynomial-coordinate basis, that the invariant lower recursive chart exists, and that its lower logarithms $F_<^{\mathrm{univ}},G_<^{\mathrm{univ}}$ satisfy the lower cocycle equation. Let $P_<^{\mathrm{univ}}=\operatorname{Ad}(f^{\mathrm{ss}})\varphi(G_<^{\mathrm{univ}})$ and $Q_<^{\mathrm{univ}}=\operatorname{Ad}(g^{\mathrm{ss}})\gamma(F_<^{\mathrm{univ}})$ upstairs. Since $p\nmid n$, let $\mathcal R=n^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\sigma^j$ be the Reynolds operator. For a relative-root orbit $\boldsymbol\alpha$, let $\Pi_{\boldsymbol\alpha}$ mean absolute-root projection to the roots in that orbit followed by $\mathcal R$, and define

$$D_{\boldsymbol\alpha}^{\mathrm{BCH}}=\Pi_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\left(\operatorname{BCH}(G_<^{\mathrm{univ}},Q_<^{\mathrm{univ}})-\operatorname{BCH}(F_<^{\mathrm{univ}},P_<^{\mathrm{univ}})\right).$$

Every BCH class through the maximal absolute-root height is included before descent. Assume that the relative-root obstruction module is cyclic with chosen standard generator, annihilator generated by $u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}$ at a simple crossing, and $d(c_{\boldsymbol\alpha,\mathrm{std}})=u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}b_{\boldsymbol\alpha,\mathrm{std}}$. If $A_{\boldsymbol\alpha}$ is any lift of the coefficient of $[D_{\boldsymbol\alpha}^{\mathrm{BCH}}]$, then the corrected twisted equation is

$$r_{\boldsymbol\alpha}^{\mathrm{BCH}}=s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}u_{\boldsymbol\alpha}+A_{\boldsymbol\alpha}=0.$$

Changing a lift gives a triangular translation of $s_{\boldsymbol\alpha}$. Under the explicit recursive and descent hypotheses, the twisted fine locus is cut out by the corrected equations, the torus component equations, the vanishing equations prescribed by the fine pair, and the linear invariant-coordinate equations $v-\sigma(v)=0$ written in the chosen basis.

Form the absolute-root local-duality pairing tensors upstairs before taking invariants. On a splitting atlas, COR17-017 puts each regular-coordinate tensor $J_{\beta,\gamma}$ in the form of a retained unit multiple of the identity on a distinct-dual block or a retained unit multiple of the symplectic antidiagonal $H_{2m}$ on a self-dual block. The quadratic part of $A_{\boldsymbol\alpha}$ is obtained by taking every unordered absolute-root decomposition once with a coherent orientation, applying its Chevalley constant to
$$x_{\beta,\mathrm{reg}}^{\mathsf T}J_{\beta,\gamma}x_{\gamma,\mathrm{reg}},$$
and then applying $\Pi_{\boldsymbol\alpha}$ to the resulting tensor-valued absolute expression. The invariant tensor and its determinant-line unit descend; no chosen symplectic basis is asserted to descend. All remaining quadratic terms and all cubic or higher mixed BCH terms form $Q_{\boldsymbol\alpha}^{\mathrm{BCH}}$. The remainder is independent of the penultimate relative-root-height coordinate block. Thus prime-to-$p$ descent preserves the corrected leading Jacobian block up to coordinate permutations and unit row or column scalings, not entrywise equality, and the complete twisted obstruction polynomial differs from the pure ordered higher-cup formula.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

Apply the complete absolute-root cocycle equation before descent. The semilinear Weyl and Galois actions are Lie algebra automorphisms, so they commute with every bracket, with truncated exponential and logarithm, and hence with each homogeneous BCH class. The absolute obstruction is therefore invariant after applying the Reynolds operator, and its relative-root component is exactly the displayed $D_{\boldsymbol\alpha}^{\mathrm{BCH}}$. Exactness of invariants follows from $p\nmid n$.

The cyclic obstruction argument converts this class into the equation $s u+A=0$. Quotient-valued uniqueness and the standard-cochain boundary relation give the triangular change of coordinate under a different lift. Writing $v-\sigma(v)$ in a fixed polynomial-coordinate basis makes the descent ideal polynomial and removes the undefined coordinate action in the source formula.

At bracket length two, first form upstairs the correctly oriented absolute-root terms with their intrinsic pairing tensors. COR17-017 gives unit identity tensors on distinct-dual blocks and unit symplectic antidiagonal tensors on self-dual blocks. The Reynolds operator averages these tensors and their quadratic contractions equivariantly. Since a symplectic basis need not be invariant, descent is applied to the pairing tensor and its determinant-line unit rather than to a chosen basis. This construction is therefore basis independent and descends to the relative-root orbit.

Averaging neither changes bracket length nor creates a penultimate-height occurrence in a cubic word. The height-block independence consequently descends unchanged. On a splitting atlas the hyperbolic slice changes a former scalar leading matrix only by coordinate permutations and unit row or column scalings. Hence the descended leading matrices are permutation/unit equivalent rather than entrywise identical, and all existing rank and minor witnesses and Sage certificates remain valid.
