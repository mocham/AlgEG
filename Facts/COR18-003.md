# Statement


Let \(F/\mathbb Q_p\) be finite and let \(\mathbf G\) be split pinned connected reductive with connected center and positive roots \(\Phi^+\), with \(p>h_{\mathbf G}\). Let \(\mathbf M\subsetneq\mathbf G\) be a proper standard Levi containing \(\mathbf T\), let \(U^{\mathbf M}\) be its standard unipotent radical, and choose the fixed Tits representative \(\dot w\in N_{\mathbf M}(\mathbf T)\) whose image \(w\in W_{\mathbf M}=N_{\mathbf M}(\mathbf T)/\mathbf T\) is elliptic in \(\mathbf M\). Let \(C\) be an irreducible component of \(\mathcal W_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}\). Then:

1. \(\mathcal W_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}\) is equidimensional of dimension zero.
2. If \([F:\mathbb Q_p]\ge\#\Phi^++2\), the inverse image \(\mathcal X_{U^{\mathbf M}\rtimes\mathbf T\langle\dot w\rangle}(C)\) is irreducible for every \(C\). Consequently the natural map is generically smooth and induces a bijection on irreducible components.
3. If \(\mathbf G\) has type \(F_4\), the conclusions of part 2 hold without the degree hypothesis.

# Proof or Examples


## Expanded self-contained proof

The image \(w\in W_{\mathbf M}\), not the normalizer representative itself, is the elliptic object in the surrounding manuscript setup. COR6-001 gives the omitted-direction cancellation and excludes exceptional full-source cycles. COR3-002 and F02-002 construct the smooth affine final twisted atlas using the fixed representative \(\dot w\). F04-024 supplies affine-space geometric fibers of the expected dimension and uniform Jacobian corank zero.

In the large-degree range, the corrected fine-stratum estimate excludes lower strata, so each inverse image over \(C\) is irreducible. In type \(F_4\), COR6-001 and F04-024 give the same conclusion without the degree bound. The smooth surjective atlas and zero-dimensional target then give generic smoothness and the component bijection.
