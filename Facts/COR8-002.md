# Statement


Assume $F=\mathbb Q_p$, let $\mathbf G$ be a split connected reductive group with connected center and root system of type $F_4$, and assume $p>12$. Fix a Borel subgroup $\mathbf B=U\rtimes\mathbf T$, let $\Phi^+$ be its positive roots, number the simple roots in Bourbaki order, and abbreviate $\sum_i n_i\delta_i$ by the digit string $n_1n_2n_3n_4$. Let $K\subset U$ be a $\mathbf T$-stable normal subgroup with root set $\Phi_K$, and put $\Psi=(\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$. Assume $(\Phi^+\setminus\Phi_K,\Psi_0,\Psi_2)$ is one of:

(I) $(\{0010\},\{0010\},\{0001,0011,0100,0110,0120\})$;

(II) $(\{0010\},\{0010\},\{0001,0011,0100,0110,0120,1000\})$;

(III) $(\{1000,0010\},\{0010,1000\},\{0001,0011,0100,0110,0120,1100,1110,1120\})$; or

(IV) $(\{1000,0001,0011,0010\},\{0001,0010,0011,1000\},\{0100,0110,0111,0120,0121,0122,1100,1110,1111,1120,1121,1122\})$.

Let $S_j$ be the complete row-$j$ fine stratum of COR18-001, including its torus Serre component and all nonvanishing conditions, and let
$$Z\in\operatorname{Irr}_{24}(S_j)$$
be any irreducible top-dimensional Serre-indexed realization of that row. Then there exists a Zariski-dense substack $\mathcal U_Z\subset Z$ such that, for every $\bar\rho\in\mathcal U_Z(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$ represented by a homomorphism $\bar\rho:\operatorname{Gal}_F\to(K\rtimes\mathbf T)(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)\subset\mathbf B(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$, there are a deformation $\rho:\operatorname{Gal}_F\to\mathbf B(\overline{\mathbb F}_p[[\varepsilon]])$, an element $\mu\in\overline{\mathbb F}_p[[\varepsilon]]$, and a root $\delta_{\mathrm{rot}}\in\Psi_0$ such that
$$\bar\rho=\exp(-\mu e_{-\delta_{\mathrm{rot}}})\rho\exp(\mu e_{-\delta_{\mathrm{rot}}})\pmod{\varepsilon}$$
and $\rho[1/\varepsilon]$ is a $\overline{\mathbb F}_p((\varepsilon))$-point of the maximally nonsplit locus $\dot{\mathcal X}_{\mathbf B}=\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}[U]$.

This assertion is componentwise: it applies to every member of $\operatorname{Irr}_{24}(S_j)$, not to a single component chosen for each row. The four rotation certificates depend only on the displayed root data. In particular, the free Serre and unramified parameters occurring in row I do not enter the divided rotational equations.

## Expanded self-contained proof

The four triples in the Statement are exactly the four triples explicitly bound in COR16-005: its set $S$ is $\Phi^+\setminus\Phi_K$, it sets $\Phi_K=\Phi^+\setminus S$ and $K=U_{\Phi_K}$, and its two remaining entries are $\Psi_0$ and $\Psi_2$. It also identifies $V_K$ with $U/K_2$. Thus the corrected reduction applies to the present root data without importing any table or notation from the source.

Fix a row $j$ and an arbitrary $Z\in\operatorname{Irr}_{24}(S_j)$. COR18-001 refines the row by its open-and-closed torus Serre data before taking irreducible top-dimensional pieces. The divided systems of COR9-002 are constructed solely from the displayed root sets, the selected rotation root, and the Chevalley root-string constants. They use rotation root $0010$ in cases I, II, and IV and $1000$ in case III. Their equations and exact rational Jacobian witnesses are therefore unchanged after restriction to any Serre-indexed realization. In row I, the free Serre and unramified parameters vary on the clopen realization but do not occur in the divided equations.

COR9-002 gives exact rational solutions with invertible Jacobian and all bad primes at most $12$. Since $p>12$, the pointwise Jacobian criterion and Henselian lifting give the required $V_K\rtimes\mathbf T$-valued deformation on a nonempty open of the fixed irreducible realization. This open is dense in that realization. Every geometric point of it has property $\operatorname{Rot}_{V_K}$ in the notation of COR16-005. Because the construction is independent of the Serre label, the same argument applies separately to every $Z\in\operatorname{Irr}_{24}(S_j)$.

COR16-005 proves that reduction $q:\mathcal X_{K\rtimes\mathbf T}\langle\Psi\rangle\to\mathcal X_{\bar K_1\rtimes\mathbf T}\langle\Psi\rangle$ is smooth, surjective, and open. Restricting to the fixed irreducible realization and taking the inverse image of its dense small-rotation open gives a dense substack $\mathcal U_Z$. For every $\bar\rho\in\mathcal U_Z(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$, COR16-005 lifts the small rotational witness through each transition $R_{n+1}\to R_n$ and each higher $K$-layer, with compatible reductions; affineness and completeness then give a continuous $\mathbf B(\overline{\mathbb F}_p[[\varepsilon]])$-valued representation preserving the same $\delta_{\mathrm{rot}}$, $\mu$, and residual conjugacy congruence. Finally, its normal-root-subgroup argument proves that the lifted generic fiber avoids every proper $\mathbf T$-stable normal root subgroup of $U$ exactly when its $V_K$ quotient does. Hence the lifted generic fiber lies in $\dot{\mathcal X}_{\mathbf B}=\mathcal X_{\mathbf B}[U]$.
